# Station Road on Foot

Route survey of Station Road, Claverdon, for planning application 26/01470/FUL: measured footway widths, video of the walk to Claverdon station, the Connectivity Tool score, and appeal decisions on sustainable location under the August 2026 NPPF.

Published at https://planningdistilled.org/research/settlement/claverdon/station-road-on-foot/.

There is no build here. The master copy is the claude.ai artifact the page was written in; the exported page, its five appeal sub-pages (`case-<ref>.html`, deep-linked as `#case-<ref>` from the artifact) and the video clips (`media/`) live in `main-site` at the path above, which is the archive of record.

To update: change the artifact, export its HTML into a folder named `station-road-on-foot/`, run `python3 site/seo.py <parent folder>` (adds the title, description, canonical link and licence line), copy the files into `main-site`, then run `node site/finish.mjs` (see `site/README.md`).

The appeal letters it cites are in `data/open-sources/pins-corpus/` (6006637, 6008688, 6010313, 6010471, 6011736).
