## Summary
Prior approval was allowed for a lambing barn on a 13 ha holding in the Kent Downs National Landscape. The inspector accepted evidence of an agricultural trade or business without accounts, and found the siting behind a dense roadside hedge acceptable (DL ¶9, ¶16-17).

## What made the difference
Persuasive non-financial evidence of an agricultural business (invoices, a grazing tenancy, stock seen on site) and DEFRA-based sizing (DL ¶8-11). The Framework was not relevant to this GPDO determination. The National Landscape duty was treated as satisfied by the finding of no harm (DL ¶18).
