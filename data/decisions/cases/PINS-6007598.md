A companion decision to PINS-6007360 by the same inspector. A BT Street Hub outside the listed Forum theatre in Kentish Town was dismissed for harm to the open footway, the theatre's setting and pedestrian movement (DL 9-22).

Here the harm arose from introducing clutter into an open, well-spaced footway (DL 8-9).
