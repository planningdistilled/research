## Summary
A front retail extension to a Bradford hall was dismissed. It harmed the façade and the street pattern, and it removed required parking, causing dangerous overspill (DL ¶10, ¶19).

## What made the difference
Loss of the parking secured by a previous permission, together with extra trade. Decided on local plan policies only.
