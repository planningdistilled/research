## Summary
Mesh security fence around a car sales forecourt at a busy junction. Allowed. The Inspector found "some harm" to the streetscene, but "not so significant to justify this appeal failing" in a mixed commercial area (DL ¶11).

## What made the difference
Mesh construction kept views through the fence. The brewery's palisade fence opposite already set the tone of the street (DL ¶¶9–10).
