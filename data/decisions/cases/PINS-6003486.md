## Summary
Conditions controlling the joinery, materials, roof ventilation and eaves of an approved outbuilding next to Blyth Conservation Area and a listed church were upheld as reasonable and necessary. The linked Appeal A against refusal of extensions was also dismissed on heritage harm. The letter is dated 17 Aug 2026 and applies the 2024 Framework paragraphs.

## What made the difference
The outbuilding would be visible in views towards the listed church, and the approved plans did not define its final appearance. Detailing controls were therefore justified (DL 34-36).
