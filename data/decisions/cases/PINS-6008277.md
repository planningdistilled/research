## Summary
Converting a rear workshop into a flat was dismissed for poor outlook and privacy for future occupiers. The letter expressly relies on L2's "acceptable living standards" limb (DL ¶9). The benefit of reusing an existing building for one home got only limited weight because of its scale (DL ¶11).

## What made the difference
Outlook onto a close fence and overlooking from the host house. The letter shows the L2(1)(d)(ii) living-standards qualification being used as a stand-alone reason for refusal. It also shows the L2(1) "substantial weight" to reuse not being applied to a one-unit scheme.
