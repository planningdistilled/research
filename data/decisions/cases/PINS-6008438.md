## Summary
A non-determination appeal for a one-bed flat in the rear of a Croydon shop unit was dismissed. The sole bedroom relied on a tiny lightwell, the access ran under a derelict undercroft that is chained at night, and no s106 secured permit-free status or transport contributions. The parties were consulted on the new Framework but made no representations (DL ¶4).

## What made the difference
The poor light, outlook and access for the only bedroom decided the appeal (DL ¶¶8–10). The balance combines the old and new tests. The adverse impacts "would substantially and demonstrably outweigh the benefits" (DL ¶21), which mixes S4's "substantially outweighed" with the 2024 "significantly and demonstrably" wording.
