## Summary
A retrospective timber fence on a prominent corner plot in Goole, dismissed on an appeal planning officer's recommendation. The Framework's changes were noted without consulting the parties: "The substantive changes thereto do not materially affect the matters at appeal" (DL ¶4). The benefits were of very limited weight and "they would be incapable of outweighing the substantial weight I would attach to the harm I have found, which would be obvious and enduring and conflict with the design related decision-making policies of the Framework" (DL ¶11).

## What made the difference
The fence's height, length and solidity on a corner plot, in a street of low walls and hedges. The decision is plan-led (ENV1); no Framework policy is named and no S4 balance is run.
