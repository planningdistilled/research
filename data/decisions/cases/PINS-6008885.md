## Summary
A retrospective second-floor walk-out balcony on a suburban semi was dismissed. It harmed the character of the house and caused actual and perceived overlooking of neighbouring gardens. The benefits were limited and private. The 2026 Framework is acknowledged, but the letter cites no Framework policy.

## What made the difference
The change from an approved Juliet balcony to a platform people can stand on and move around. Perceived surveillance counted as harm even over long separation distances (DL ¶14-18).
