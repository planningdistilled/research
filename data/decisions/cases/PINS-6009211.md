## Summary
A flat subdivision with a near-ridge rear dormer and a hip-to-gable change on a prominent Southgate corner semi was dismissed, on an appeal planning officer's recommendation. The design conflicted with DP3's identity principle, which under DP3(3) requires refusal. Both units also fell below London Plan ceiling heights. Under S4, the limited benefit of one extra flat was substantially outweighed.

## What made the difference
The prominent corner position and the loss of the pair's symmetry. The London Plan space standard added a living-conditions failure. This is a routine application of the DP3(3) refusal trigger inside a settlement.
