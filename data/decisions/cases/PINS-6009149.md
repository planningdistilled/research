## Summary
An eight-bed HMO scheme was dismissed. Its large flat-roofed side dormers would overwhelm a prominent period corner house, and the claimed BNG de minimis exemption was not demonstrated. Intensification from six to eight occupants caused no neighbour harm. The Inspector found the new Framework "does not differ significantly from the 2024 version in respect of the substance" (DL ¶3).

## What made the difference
The extensions were treated as part of the development, despite the appellant's PD claim (DL ¶¶7–13). That brought in both the design harm and the BNG baseline. The Inspector held BNG failure "a fundamental flaw" that could not be cured by condition (DL ¶38).
