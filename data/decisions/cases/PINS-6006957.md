## Summary
Technical details consent for a single house replacing derelict outbuildings behind a Torquay terrace was allowed. The design fitted the close-knit context, and a daylight report submitted at appeal showed negligible change against the existing structures. The principle was fixed at the PiP stage.

## What made the difference
The existing structures set the baseline for overshadowing. An amended drawing removing one window was accepted at appeal because it was on the portal before the Council's statement (DL ¶¶6, 14–16).
