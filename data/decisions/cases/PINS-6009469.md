## Summary
A four-bed HMO above a shop in Stourbridge town centre was allowed. There was no evidence that the HMO would raise crime or the fear of crime. The external works were found to enhance the conservation area and the setting of listed buildings.

## What made the difference
The evidence gap. Area crime statistics and objectors' assumptions about HMO occupiers did not establish a link to the proposal (DL ¶9, 12).
