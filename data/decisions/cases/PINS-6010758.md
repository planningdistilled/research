## Summary
A retrospective variation to keep an as-built front boundary treatment on a prominent Birkdale corner was dismissed. It caused significant harm to the area's open character of low brick walls and gardens (DL ¶14).

## What made the difference
The prominent corner and the consistent local boundary pattern. The inspector ran the S4 test even for a boundary treatment, finding the harm substantially outweighed "the benefit afforded to development within a settlement" (DL ¶17).
