## Summary
Class Q prior approval for a timber barn conversion at Audlem was allowed. Internal insulated blockwork was held to be primarily a thermal envelope, with only incidental structural support, so the works were not a rebuild under *Hibbitt* (DL ¶8-9, ¶13).

## What made the difference
A structural report, and the site visit, showed the frame could stand without the blockwork (DL ¶9).
