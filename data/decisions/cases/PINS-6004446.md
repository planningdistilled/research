## Summary
An Aldi store on a greenfield parcel at the eastern edge of Ivybridge was allowed. The only issue was outlook from neighbouring new houses, and the landscaped buffer and separation distances made it acceptable (DL 13). The sequential and impact tests were not in dispute (DL 19). The letter does not mention the Framework.

## What made the difference
The narrow, terraced rear gardens meant neighbours would not see the building in its entirety. Separation distances of 17-20 m, a planted embankment and green cladding kept it from being overbearing (DL 10-13).
