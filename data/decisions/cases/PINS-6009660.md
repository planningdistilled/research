## Summary
A one-bedroom increase to an HMO was allowed. The Council's refusal rested on the Framework alone. The Inspector applied new TR6 directly: "the test before me is the effect of the increase from 6 to 7 residents … not existing parking conditions generally", and spare capacity within reasonable walking distance meant no unacceptable safety or severe cumulative impact (DL ¶¶11–12).

## What made the difference
The appellant's parking survey showed spare capacity on a street reachable by a lit route with a signal crossing (DL ¶10). The test was the increment, not the existing parking stress.
