## Summary
Class MA prior approval for two flats was granted. The natural-light problem was solved with a condition preventing occupation until windows approved under a separate extant planning permission are built. The Courts have recognised this as an "obvious solution" (DL ¶¶11–13). The Framework is not cited.

## What made the difference
The separate extant full permission for the external works, coupled by condition, following *LW Zenith* (DL ¶13).
