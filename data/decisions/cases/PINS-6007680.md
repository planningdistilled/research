## Summary
A 10-child home nursery with a side extension was dismissed on three grounds: design (a terracing effect), noise to close neighbours, and drop-off parking on a busy road. The new Framework was "broadly consistent", so the parties were not consulted (DL ¶4). It is cited generically, without codes.

## What made the difference
Close neighbours and a main-road site with tight drop-off space. Management conditions (staggered drop-off, staff living on site, play hours) were rejected as unenforceable or unreasonable (DL ¶¶14, 17, 19).
