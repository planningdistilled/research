## Summary
A small side roof terrace to a Bournemouth flat was allowed. It was a lightweight addition in an area that already has visible roof terraces, and with obscure screening the overlooking affected only non-habitable rooms. The Inspector checked local policy CS41 for consistency against L2, DP3 and P3, and applied HE4(2) to the adjoining conservation area (DL ¶¶19–20).

## What made the difference
The neighbour's facing windows served non-habitable spaces, and the terrace was too small for parties (DL ¶¶11–12, 15). The letter shows the pattern of testing local-plan consistency with the new codes (L2/DP3/P3) instead of applying them directly.
