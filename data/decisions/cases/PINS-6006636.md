## Summary
A three-child care home was dismissed because Policy 11 was read as requiring a need specific to the Borough. County and national sufficiency evidence was not enough, set against a county email saying no local children were awaiting placement. The loss of a C3 home in an area of significant housing need counted against it. No Framework is cited (letter dated 17 Aug).

## What made the difference
The up-to-date, Borough-specific county email (DL ¶12). Compare PINS-6006244 (Havering), where the Council's own Sufficiency Assessment established need, and the fork cases on HO7/HO9 for children's homes (PINS-6010861, PINS-6011401).
