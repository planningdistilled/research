## Summary
An upward extension above a shop to create two studio flats was dismissed. The loft flat had not been shown to meet London Plan Guidance minimums for living, dining and kitchen space or headroom, and no refuse storage was provided. The external design matched an approved scheme. The Framework is not cited, and L2(1)(d) airspace support was not considered.

## What made the difference
The appellant bears the burden of demonstrating compliance with minimum standards, and a condition cannot be used to redesign the layout (DL ¶10). This is a letter where L2(1)(d)'s "substantial weight" for airspace homes could have been argued but was not raised.
