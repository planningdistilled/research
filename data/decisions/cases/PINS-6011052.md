## Summary
A retrospective five-bed student HMO in Middlesbrough was dismissed for poor internal living conditions: a cramped shared kitchen and no outdoor space. Parking was acceptable (DL ¶12, ¶16).

## What made the difference
The inspector used DP3's liveability requirement for "features that promote social interaction" to fill a gap in local policy, which had no explicit communal-space standard (DL ¶6). Licensing standards did not carry over to planning (DL ¶10).
