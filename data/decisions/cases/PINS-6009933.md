## Summary
Proposal to take an 8-person HMO to 10 persons with a full-width rear dormer, in a street of family houses. Dismissed on character/amenity from intensified use, the dormer's design, and overlooking. The Inspector did not consult on the new Framework because it "do[es] not substantially alter matters" (DL ¶2). DP3 was cited once as an additional conflict (DL ¶13).

## What made the difference
Evidence of existing complaints about noise and ASB at the site distinguished it from the Council's other HMO uplift approvals: "there are material differences in the evidence" (DL ¶9). The dormer extended over the side extension, so it went beyond what PD would allow, and the fallback carried limited weight (DL ¶17).
