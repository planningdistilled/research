## Summary
Residents of an 8-home mews wanted to drop the private bin collection condition and put bins out for Council kerbside collection. The Inspector found the collection point impractical and outside the appellant's control, and the condition still necessary. Dismissed. The letter does not cite any Framework.

## What made the difference
The Inspector's own judgement on practicality and future occupiers with mobility issues outweighed the lack of technical objection from the waste and highway authorities (DL ¶11).
