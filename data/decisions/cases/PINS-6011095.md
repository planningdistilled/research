## Summary
A timber-clad shipping container on a Kirtlington paddock was dismissed. It harmed the rural lane's character, and no agricultural or rural need was shown (DL ¶14, ¶18).

## What made the difference
Lack of evidence that the paddock use or the stable permission had been implemented, so no need for storage was shown (DL ¶4, ¶18). The inspector also noted that the council's report had not discharged the s66 duty on a nearby listed cottage, but found no harm (DL ¶19).
