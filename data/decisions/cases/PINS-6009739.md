## Summary
Class Q prior approval for six dwellings in two barns was granted. The existing 4.8m access was suitable, transport impacts were acceptable, and a neighbouring storage barn conditioned against livestock would not make the conversion undesirable.

## What made the difference
The council had already accepted residential conversion of Barns A and B, and the neighbouring Barn E was already restricted by condition. Q.1(p)'s "suitable existing access" was read as meaning fit for normal day-to-day residential use (DL ¶12).
