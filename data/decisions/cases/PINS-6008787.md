## Summary
Class AA prior approval for two extra storeys, making six flats, on an Orpington corner building was refused, and the appeal was dismissed on external appearance. The building would read as five storeys in a three-storey street (DL ¶12). The Framework's support for upward extensions, and the council's five-year supply shortfall, were irrelevant: "the principle of the development is already accepted through the GPDO" (DL ¶22).

## What made the difference
A shallow mansard, little set-back and large dormers on a prominent corner. The Framework counts in prior approval only "in so far as … relevant to the subject matter of the prior approval" (DL ¶22).
