## Summary
A new house in the side gap of a 1950s-style estate corner in Worcester was dismissed. It would erode one of the repeated "designed gaps" that give the estate its openness, overlook a neighbour's garden, and it had no BNG baseline (DL ¶10-21).

## What made the difference
The gap's role in the estate's layout (DL ¶10). With a five-year supply now shown, one dwelling's limited benefits were "substantially outweigh[ed]" (DL ¶23).
