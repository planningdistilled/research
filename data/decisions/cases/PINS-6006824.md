## Summary
An agricultural building and polytunnel were accepted as reasonably necessary on a new holding, but the appeal failed because the polytunnel frame had already been partly put up (DL 24).

## What made the difference
Development had begun before the prior-approval process, which takes it outside Part 6 Class A.
