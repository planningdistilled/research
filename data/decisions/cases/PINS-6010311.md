## Summary
Heritage-style uPVC to replace the timber joinery of two side porches on a positive building in Buxton College CA. Allowed, with detailed specifications reserved by condition, so the CA would be preserved (DL ¶17). There was no consultation on the new Framework because its content "has not significantly changed" for this case (DL ¶3).

## What made the difference
The rear porch timber was visibly rotten, so replacement rather than repair was justified. The Conservation Officer's objection was about missing specifications, not uPVC in principle, and a condition could cure that (DL ¶¶12–16).
