## Summary
A replacement design for an already-implemented house behind a Grade II former pub in Clophill was allowed. It preserved the settings of the listed building and the conservation area under HE4/HE6. Poor garden privacy was no worse than under the extant permission (DL ¶17-18, ¶27).

## What made the difference
The implemented permission for a dwelling on the same site was the fallback. It neutralised the privacy conflict, and the site "provides very little contribution to the setting of the listed building" (DL ¶12).
