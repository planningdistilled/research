## Summary
A rear extension 1.5m deeper than an extant approval on a spacious corner plot. Allowed. The extra depth was subordinate, and the design guide's 3.5m figure is "only guidance" (DL ¶8). The Framework changes did "not affect the substance of the main issue", so there was no consultation (DL ¶3).

## What made the difference
The extant 3.5m fallback and the plot's spaciousness.
