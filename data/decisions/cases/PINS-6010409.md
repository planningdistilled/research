## Summary
Class Q barn-to-dwelling prior-approval appeal, dismissed on a point the Inspector raised herself. The barn's 1997 permission restricted it to agricultural use "only and for no other purpose", and under Article 3(4) that impliedly excludes PD rights (DL ¶¶10–14). The new Framework was noted as not raising determinative matters (DL ¶5).

## What made the difference
The word "only" in the use condition "evinced an intention to identify an acceptable use, whilst prohibiting unacceptable uses" (DL ¶12). The Class Q limitations were never reached. A separate costs decision was issued.
