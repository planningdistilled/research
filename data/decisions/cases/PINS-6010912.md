## Summary
A retrospective glazed veranda on a corner restaurant building. Allowed. The lightweight contemporary contrast was accepted under DP3(1), which the inspector noted "expressly allow[s] for innovation or change where appropriate" (DL ¶10). Pedestrian circulation was unaffected, so there was no TR6 conflict even though the highway boundary was unresolved.

## What made the difference
Transparency and the corner siting facing an open junction. The DP3(1) innovation clause was used positively.
