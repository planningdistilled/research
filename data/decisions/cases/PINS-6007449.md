## Summary
A like-for-like replacement of an InLink kiosk with a larger BT Street Hub on Shepherd's Bush Green was allowed. The south side of the road contributes neutrally to the conservation area and the war memorial's setting, and similar screens already exist on bus stops (DL 11-17).

## What made the difference
An existing hub on the same spot and a context of large bus-stop displays and modern buildings.
