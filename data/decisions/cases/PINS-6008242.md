## Summary
A retrospective restaurant extraction flue with flats above was dismissed for odour and harm to outlook for neighbours. Noise was found acceptable. The 2026 Framework is acknowledged in a single line (DL ¶4), and the decision turns on Poole local policies.

## What made the difference
Because the flue was already installed, residents' repeated first-hand experience of odour, backed by the EHO and the inspector's own visit, displaced a desk-based odour assessment that conceded a "very high" risk (DL ¶14, 17).
