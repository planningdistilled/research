## Summary
A second-floor rear balcony was dismissed because it would overshadow and overlook the roof terrace and the only window to a habitable room of the flat below. The decision is purely plan-led (D.DH8) and does not mention the Framework.

## What made the difference
The balcony sat directly over the neighbour's only light source and amenity space. People linger on a balcony in a way they do not at a window, which made the overlooking worse (DL ¶8). The index flagged E3 for this letter, but E3 is not engaged: the letter cites no Framework policy.
