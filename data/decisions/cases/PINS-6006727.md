## Summary
A retrospective, enlarged rear extension to a locally listed Hindu temple was dismissed. It harmed the character of the terrace and neighbours' outlook and light, although the non-designated heritage asset's significance, which lies in its frontage, was not harmed (DL 14-15, 21). The community benefit was modest (DL 27-28).

## What made the difference
Depth and roof height well beyond any comparable rear addition, next to narrow neighbouring gardens.
