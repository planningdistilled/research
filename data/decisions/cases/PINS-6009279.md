## Summary
A self-build dwelling in a side garden in Walpole St Andrew was allowed on the recommendation of an appeal planning officer. There was no harmful overlooking, and the street's eclectic character absorbed the loss of the corner garden. The letter records that BNG amendment regulations in force from 6 August 2026 removed the self-build exemption but introduced a 0.2 ha small-site exemption, which this 0.0308 ha site met (DL ¶4).

## What made the difference
The separation distances and intervening features protected privacy. The Inspector held that the Council's 21 m window-to-window standard is a matter of judgement "in each case", not a blanket rule (DL ¶15). A point to note for other cases: the new 0.2 ha BNG small-site exemption.
