## Summary
A new industrial unit next to fresh housing in Martham was dismissed. It would create an oppressive outlook for small gardens, and because the B2 use was undefined, its noise could not be assessed. The only Framework policy named is DM4, used to give the emerging plan policy moderate weight (DL ¶3).

## What made the difference
An ill-defined use class. Without knowing what B2 operation would take place, conditions could not be shown to control noise. The Framework's transition is not mentioned.
