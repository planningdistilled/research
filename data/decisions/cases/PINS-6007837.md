## Summary
A single-family traveller site off a narrow unlit lane was allowed after a hearing. The poor B1403 junction would be avoided in favour of a better route through the village, so the highway impact was not unacceptable. The letter, dated 1 Sep 2026, quotes the 2024 ¶116 highways test and does not mention the new Framework.

## What made the difference
The availability of a much better alternative junction, and no accident record (DL ¶¶15–17). The pedestrian route was accepted as poor but not determinative for a traveller site.
