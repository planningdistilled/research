## Summary
An s73 appeal to substitute plans for a taller Plot 6 house in a six-dwelling Chesham scheme was allowed. The only harm found in an earlier dismissed appeal had come from the neighbour's flank kitchen window, which has since been removed. The neighbour's deep garden keeps the outlook acceptable (DL ¶8-12). The Framework is not cited.

## What made the difference
The neighbour had removed the flank window, so "the only aspect of the earlier scheme that was judged harmful … has therefore fallen away" (DL ¶8).
