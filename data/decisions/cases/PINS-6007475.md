## Summary
De-conversion of seven substandard flats into one family house was refused under Islington's housing-optimisation policies. The net loss of at least five units outweighed improved living conditions (DL 8-11). The Framework is not referred to.

## What made the difference
The plan permits a net loss of at most one unit, and there was no evidence that smaller high-priority units could not be provided.
