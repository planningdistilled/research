## Summary
An 8-bed HMO on the Morecambe seafront was allowed. The Council had not quantified HMO concentration within the policy's 100 m radius and had wrongly mapped the site inside a regeneration area. Bedroom sizes were resolved by capping occupancy at eight. A homeowner pack condition dealt with habitats effects and was cited to N6.

## What made the difference
The policy test is a local concentration count, and the Council produced no count. Area-wide SHMA figures on self-contained flats did not show HMO over-concentration (DL ¶¶18–19).
