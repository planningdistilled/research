## Summary
A BT Street Hub on a cluttered Camden Town footway was refused. Lower-level harm to the setting of a conservation area and a Grade II* listed building, clutter and pedestrian obstruction outweighed benefits that were given substantial weight but were modest (DL 13-15, 22).

## What made the difference
The already cluttered footway and pedestrian desire line. The hub's bulk made it the most prominent object on the street (DL 8-9, 20).
