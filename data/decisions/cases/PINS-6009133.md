## Summary
A one-child children's home in a Perry Barr terrace was allowed. Activity and parking demand would be akin to a family household (DL ¶8, ¶12). The case was decided on the local plan's care-home criteria, and the Framework is not cited.

## What made the difference
Its modest scale (one child) was locked in by condition (DL ¶17), and there was no site-specific safeguarding objection (contrast PINS-6009126).
