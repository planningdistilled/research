## Summary
Change of a three-bed family house to a small HMO was dismissed because it would lose a family dwelling, contrary to Local Plan policy DMH5. The third bedroom was also too small for an adult (DL ¶6, ¶14-16).

## What made the difference
A recent (2024) local plan policy that expressly protects family homes from HMO conversion. The efficiency benefits could not outweigh it. The Framework is not cited.
