## Summary
An appeal against an hours-of-use condition on a replacement village motor-repair workshop. Allowed, with the hours aligned to a later variation the council itself granted. The letter never mentions the Framework, old or new. It is a short conditions decision resting on development plan noise policies and the later permission.

## What made the difference
The council's own later, more generous permission made the disputed condition unreasonable. A narrow use restriction was refused because it would jeopardise financing (DL ¶8).
