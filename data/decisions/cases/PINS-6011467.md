## Summary
A retrospective car wash behind a social club, surrounded by housing. Dismissed on character and noise grounds. Because the council's cited local policies (design DE1, industrial JE5) did not fit a sui generis car wash, the inspector used "national decision making policy P3 Paragraph 2" as the operative noise test (DL ¶11, 14). The parties were not consulted on the new Framework; the letter does not discuss the transition.

## What made the difference
Noise observed on site, with a late noise assessment excluded under Wheatcroft. Framework decision-making policies, here P3(2), can fill gaps where local plan policies do not fit the use.
