## Summary
Air-conditioning condensers were allowed after the inspector held that an ambient level equal to the WHO guideline does not 'exceed' it. So the less stringent day-time limit applied, and the noise assessment's predictions met the SPD limits (DL 11-16).

## What made the difference
The ordinary-meaning reading of 'exceeds' in the Council's own SPD, and an unchallenged noise methodology.
