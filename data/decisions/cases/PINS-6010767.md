## Summary
The listed building consent half of the Mayfield roller-shutter appeals (see PINS-6010765). Dismissed. The shutter and box would harm the special interest of the Grade II 18th-century terrace. Reversibility "does not overcome the harm that would occur while it is in place" (DL ¶19).

## What made the difference
Same as the planning appeal: the security need was unevidenced, and less intrusive alternatives had not been ruled out (DL ¶23).
