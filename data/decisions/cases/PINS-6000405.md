## Summary
Conversion and extension of an end-terrace house in Barnet into two flats. Allowed despite a breach of the size thresholds in local policy HOU03(C), because the scheme kept a 3-bed family unit and so met the policy's "overarching aims" (DL ¶9-10). The letter never mentions the Framework. The footer carries a different reference (6006098), which looks like a template slip.

## What made the difference
Keeping a family-sized unit, and a second flat that exceeds NDSS, outweighed a numerical conflict with the plan (DL ¶18: "material considerations indicate that a decision should be made other than in accordance with it"). No Framework policy was engaged. L2(1)(d) (additional homes within existing plots) could have been cited but was not.
