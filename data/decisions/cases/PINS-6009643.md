## Summary
A crossover and front parking space across a grass verge in Luton was dismissed on pedestrian safety. The forecourt was too shallow to park without blocking the front door, so cars would overhang a busy footway (DL ¶10-11). There was no character harm, and a 2021 permission for the same scheme did not bind the inspector (DL ¶12).

## What made the difference
Where the front door sits relative to the space made a full-length park impractical (DL ¶10).
