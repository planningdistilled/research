## Summary
Two Class MA prior approvals for commercial-to-residential conversions were dismissed. Flooding and noise impacts were not shown to be acceptable, and amended layouts and reports submitted at appeal were rejected as fundamental changes (DL ¶9-10, ¶24, ¶27).

## What made the difference
Inadequate technical evidence at application stage, which the appeal could not cure. The Framework was relevant only to the prior approval matters (DL ¶13).
