## Summary
A BT Street Hub with illuminated ads on a wide footway in Ilford Metropolitan Centre was allowed, for both planning and advertisement consent. It sat comfortably in a busy urban street scene and left an adequate footway.

## What made the difference
Context: a large-scale mixed-use centre with plenty of street furniture already, and distance from the junctions. The letter never cites the Framework. Other street-hub letters in the corpus (for example the Reading hub, 6002960, indexed with CO1) do engage the telecoms policy.
