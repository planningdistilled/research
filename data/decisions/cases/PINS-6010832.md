## Summary
A seven-person HMO in Erith was dismissed for noise and disturbance to neighbours, even against a real-prospect fallback of a six-person HMO (DL ¶8-10).

## What made the difference
The inspector found the seventh occupant produced a cumulative increase "significantly over and above" the current position (DL ¶10).
