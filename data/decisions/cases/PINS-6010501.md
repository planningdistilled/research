## Summary
Splitting a 2-bed maisonette in Thornton Heath into a studio and a 1-bed flat. Dismissed. The inspector found the loss of a home under 130 sq m (protected by Croydon DM1.2) contrary to HO7 as well, and found the remote cycle and refuse stores contrary to TR4 and DP3. With a five-year supply agreed, one net unit had limited weight and was substantially outweighed under S4 (DL ¶28-30).

## What made the difference
The inspector read HO7 as supporting a local policy that protects existing mid-sized homes. Weak cycle and bin access added national-policy conflicts, which made the S4 balance straightforward. Parking was not a problem.
