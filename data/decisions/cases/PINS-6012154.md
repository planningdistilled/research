## Summary
Class Q prior approval for a barn-to-dwelling conversion. Allowed. The internal floors and infill works were not "substantial rebuilding", and a suitable access track existed. The Framework is not mentioned. None of the Class Q matters in dispute (building operations, access) invoke it, which explains its absence.

## What made the difference
Retained principal stone walls, a structural report, and the PPG's acceptance of internal floors. Phosphate/Ramsar was left to the separate regulation 77 route.
