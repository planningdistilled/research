## Summary
A blanket PD-rights removal condition on a barn conversion was replaced with a class-specific one. The original condition was imprecise and lacked the "clear justification" that DM6(2)(c) requires for most roof alterations. Extensions and curtilage buildings could still harm the barn's isolated agricultural form, so removing those classes was justified (DL ¶¶9–12).

## What made the difference
The Inspector tested each GPDO class against the character reason. Classes C and G (limited roof alterations, flues) "would be relatively limited in scale and preserve the character", so removing them was unnecessary (DL ¶12). The Framework was said not to change the policy context on this issue (DL ¶2).
