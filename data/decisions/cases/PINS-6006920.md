## Summary
A replacement house was allowed with two front dormers that the extant permission did not include. The street's roofscape is so varied that the dormers "would respect and enhance the prevailing character" (DL ¶8). No Framework is cited in the reasoning.

## What made the difference
The varied roofscape at the street entrance and the small, symmetrical dormers. The Council's request to remove permitted development rights was declined as "overly restrictive" (DL ¶16).
