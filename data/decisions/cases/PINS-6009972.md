## Summary
Two adjoining houses converted into two separate 5-bed HMOs. Dismissed because the communal kitchen-diners were inadequate: one was undersized, and neither had an openable window, so there was "unacceptable risk of unhealthy condensation and the accumulation of mould" (DL ¶13). The new Framework was taken into account (DL ¶3) but only referenced at a high level: effective use of land "should not diminish healthy living conditions" (DL ¶14).

## What made the difference
Quality, not just area. Floor area met NDSS-equivalent levels, but "the quality of an essential facility such as a kitchen … is not determinable solely on the basis of floor area" (DL ¶12). Significant weight was given because these were the only communal rooms (DL ¶14).
