## Summary
A five-person HMO conversion with extensions in Coventry was dismissed. It breached the 10% HMO concentration cap, and the extensions caused loss of light and enclosure to neighbours (DL ¶7-8, ¶15).

## What made the difference
The quantified concentration, uncontested by the appellant. The prior-approval fallback for a 6 m extension could not cover the infill element (DL ¶17).
