## Summary
A BT Street Hub and its adverts on a Hull Road footway in York were dismissed. The unit would harm pedestrian and cyclist safety where the footway meets a cycle route (DL ¶12-13).

## What made the difference
The site-specific footway geometry and cycle movements. The public benefits of the hub could not outweigh the safety harm (DL ¶14, ¶16).
