## Summary
A self-service launderette pod behind a wall in hotel grounds on Hagley Road was allowed. Natural surveillance and conditioned security measures dealt with the crime concerns. There was no conservation-area harm, and noise was controlled by operating hours.

## What made the difference
The police evidence was generic: high area crime rates but no incidents at the existing facility. Surveillance from the B&B reception and windows was judged enough, so conditions could make the scheme acceptable.
