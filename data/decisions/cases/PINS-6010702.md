## Summary
Extending a house in Canons Park to form a second 3-bed dwelling. Dismissed on the recommendation of an Appeal Planning Officer, with no re-consultation on the new Framework (DL ¶4). Sub-standard ceiling heights (P3), overspill parking at PTAL 2 (TR6), and bin storage reachable only via a barriered footpath (DP3) were each given substantial weight. One net unit had moderate weight and was substantially outweighed under S4 (DL ¶23-24).

## What made the difference
Several separate failures, each linked to a Framework decision-making policy. Notably, a TR6 highway-safety finding rested on *parking displacement* with no survey evidence, not on a specific access defect (DL ¶12-14).
