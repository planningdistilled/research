## Summary
Class Q conversion of two barns to 7 dwellings, dismissed on noise from retained farm buildings. The decision-maker "must have necessary certainty … prior to an award of prior approval", so noise could not be left to a condition (DL ¶11). Partial demolition was accepted as "reasonably necessary". The letter does not mention the Framework at all.

## What made the difference
The noise review was "light on detail" and did not assess whether mitigation was needed or would work, including against intensified future agricultural use (DL ¶¶8–9). The EHO's suggestion of a condition was rejected (DL ¶¶10–11).
