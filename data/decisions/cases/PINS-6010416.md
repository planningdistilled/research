## Summary
Substantial extensions and a garden extension onto "transitional land" at a prominent hillside house in the Bramham Conservation Area, above the Grade II war memorial. Dismissed. The inspector did not re-consult because the relevant policy had "not substantively changed" (DL ¶2). Moderate heritage harm, risk to TPO trees, unsafe reversing manoeuvres and an unsupported BNG exemption substantially outweighed the largely private benefits under S4 (DL ¶38-39).

## What made the difference
Mainly the elevated, prominent position: doubling the first-floor width made a neutral building dominant over the historic core. The BNG exemption failed because the application described the extra land as "transitional land", undermining the claim that it was already garden (DL ¶32-34).
