## Summary
Linked Class MA prior-approval appeals (6010163 and 6010167) for office-to-residential conversion. Both were dismissed for insufficient information on refuse and highway, contamination, tidal flood risk, noise and daylight. The Framework was treated only as a material consideration relevant to prior-approval matters (DL ¶13), and no 2026 policy was applied expressly.

## What made the difference
Amended layouts and replacement technical reports submitted at appeal were refused on Holborn grounds as an attempt to "evolve a scheme" (DL ¶¶8–11). That left the original, deficient FRA and noise reports as the only evidence.
