## Summary
A retrospective static caravan in a Riseley field was dismissed for significant harm to rural character (DL ¶14).

## What made the difference
The caravan's utilitarian form in an open field (DL ¶6). The inspector cites the Framework by "Section" (14 design, 4 countryside) rather than by policy code (DL ¶12).
