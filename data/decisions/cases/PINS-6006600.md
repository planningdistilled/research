## Summary
Larger-home extension prior approval for a bungalow. Dismissed. Inconsistent drawings meant the eaves limit in A.1(d) could not be shown to be met, and the extension needed a soil-pipe alteration, which Class A excludes (A.1(k)). The Framework is not relevant to PD eligibility and is not cited.

## What made the difference
Drawing accuracy. Also, a Class G-permitted soil-pipe change cannot be bundled into a Class A extension (DL ¶18).
