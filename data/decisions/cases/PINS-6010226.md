## Summary
EV charging hub in a retail-park car park requiring the loss of one young TPO lime. Allowed. The tree loss was a "limited, but nonetheless harmful" impact, justified and compensated by two replacements (DL ¶12-14, 18). The inspector found "no significant material differences" between the 2026 and superseded Frameworks for this issue and did not consult the parties (DL ¶2). N2, N3 and TR4 were cited in general terms.

## What made the difference
The tree was of moderate quality, a near-identical lime next to it remained, and replacements were secured by condition. The EV charging need was supported by TR4.
