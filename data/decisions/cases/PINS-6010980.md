## Summary
A flat-roofed dormer on a rear outrigger in an Islington terrace was dismissed for harm to the host building and the terrace (DL ¶9).

## What made the difference
Poor fit with the terrace's roofscape. The appellant's energy-efficiency and space benefits were modest (DL ¶13).
