## Summary
A self-build house in a corner side garden on a Hampsthwaite estate. Allowed. Following an earlier appeal finding of no character harm, the inspector found the character preserved. The shaded, awkward garden caused limited harm and conflict with HP4. Under S3/S4, with a significant supply shortfall, that did not substantially outweigh the combined benefits, which the inspector weighted substantially.

## What made the difference
The earlier inspector's finding on character, and the S4 threshold. A garden that is sub-optimal but usable is "limited" harm, which falls well short of substantially outweighing a new home in a settlement during a supply shortfall.
