## Summary
A detached house on a side plot in Worcester Park was dismissed for harm to character, poor privacy and visual intrusion, and missing BNG information (DL ¶7, ¶12, ¶16).

## What made the difference
Cumulative design and amenity failings. The inspector added two amenity harms the council had not raised (DL ¶12). The new Framework did not alter the plan-led outcome (DL ¶18).
