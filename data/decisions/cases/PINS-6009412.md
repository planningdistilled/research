## Summary
An attempt to use Class MA to absorb a commercial side extension into an existing flat was dismissed. Class MA grants rights to create new dwellinghouses, not to enlarge existing ones (DL ¶14-17). The Framework is not cited.

## What made the difference
No new dwelling would be created. The inspector followed a 2024 appeal reading of the 2021 Explanatory Memorandum (DL ¶13-15).
