## Summary
Black cladding on a Bolton local-centre shopfront, refused on appearance, was allowed on appeal. Among varied shopfronts, grilles and shutters it "does not appear stark" (DL ¶9). The decision is plan-led, under Places for Everyone JP-P1.

## What made the difference
The street's commercial, mixed-material context (DL ¶8-9).
