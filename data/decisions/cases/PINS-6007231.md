## Summary
A new field access for an approved agricultural building was dismissed on highway safety. The gate position and dropped kerb could not accommodate large agricultural vehicles waiting off the road (DL 10-15). The biodiversity concerns were resolved by replacement hedging.

## What made the difference
There was no evidence of vehicle sizes or numbers, and conditions restricting the access's use were unenforceable (DL 8, 14).
