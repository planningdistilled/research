## Summary
This was a retrospective s73 variation for a pub-to-flats conversion in a conservation area. The appellant won on heritage: the trickle vents, applied glazing bars and taller rear extension were neutral, so S4 was complied with (DL ¶28, 30). The appeal still failed, because the replacement car-free UU was an unsigned, undated draft with no plans (DL ¶15).

## What made the difference
The incomplete obligation. Under the updated Procedural Guide, the inspector would not wait for a revised UU (DL ¶33). Without a completed UU, the car-free and car-club obligations secured on the original permission fell away, which "would likely lead to unmitigated harm through the exacerbation of parking stress" (DL ¶31).
