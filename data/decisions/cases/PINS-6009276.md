## Summary
Conversion of a town-centre commercial building in Bolton's St George's Conservation Area into two flats was dismissed. The flats would give poor living conditions (P3, significant weight), and the lack of detail on windows, heat pumps and PV meant CA harm could not be ruled out (HE6). Under S4 the benefits were "substantially outweighed" (DL ¶35).

## What made the difference
Poor internal amenity (below NDSS, oppressive 3.8 m outlook, workshop noise) and missing heritage detail. HO7 weight was cut to moderate because "there is no substantive evidence before me which identifies such a need for the type of accommodation proposed" (DL ¶31). That is a notable reading: HO7's substantial weight was tied to evidenced need for the specific type of home.
