## Summary
Intensification of a Stanwell HMO from six to eight residents was dismissed. Communal space would be inadequate, and the front bin and cycle store would harm the open frontage character (DL ¶9, ¶11).

## What made the difference
The communal-space deficiency. Suggested conditions could not cure it (DL ¶19).
