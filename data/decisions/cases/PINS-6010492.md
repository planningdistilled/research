## Summary
A first-floor rear extension refused for exceeding SPD depth guidance by about 0.5 m was allowed. The inspector held that the SPD is guidance to be applied with judgement, and there was no harm to the neighbours (DL ¶14).

## What made the difference
The council's mechanical use of SPD formulae. The Framework played no part.
