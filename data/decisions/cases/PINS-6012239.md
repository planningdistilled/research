## Summary
Ground-floor city-centre shop converted to two flats behind a shopfront with obscured lower glazing. Allowed. City-centre occupiers "could reasonably expect a more active street environment", and outlook over the manifestation was meaningful (DL ¶¶11–12). Heritage assets were preserved.

## What made the difference
The Inspector saw the installed glazing on site and judged it a balance "between securing privacy … and maintaining a meaningful outlook" (DL ¶11). The context was a busy city centre at the foot of a sloping street.
