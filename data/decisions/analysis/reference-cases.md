# Reference cases by NPPF 2026 test (curated)

These are hand-picked from about 970 decisions (17 Aug–30 Sep 2026). `../index/policy-index.md` lists every finding; this file picks the **2–6 best** per test.
- **Order:** highest weight first. Hearings and inquiries come before written representations (WR), which come before councils. No SoS or court authority exists yet.
- **Sources:** propositions and paragraph refs are taken from the topic analyses and the case files. Quote from the letter (`data/open-sources/pins-corpus/<ref>.txt`) before relying on one.
- **Key:** ↓ supports refusal; ↑ supports approval; ◇ a limit or trap.

## Principle (S3–S6)

**S4(1) within settlements, "substantially outweighed"**
- ↓ [PINS-6010836](../cases/PINS-6010836.md) (WR): three substantial-weight benefits (HO7, E2, TC2) lost to a DP3 trigger with "no clear overriding justification".
- ↓ [PINS-6007054](../cases/PINS-6007054.md) ¶22 (WR): "S4(2) is not exhaustive". Moderate Grade II setting harm beats 1 home at 2.98 years.
- ↓ [PINS-6008167](../cases/PINS-6008167.md) ¶21 (WR): substantial housing weight at 3.55 years still "substantially outweighed" via DP3(3).
- ↑ [PINS-6008314](../cases/PINS-6008314.md) ¶102–103 (hearing): the Annex B settlement definition uses "includes", so it is non-exhaustive. A site built up on three sides is inside S4.
- ↑ [PINS-6010195](../cases/PINS-6010195.md) ¶26 (WR): a local-only conflict (bedroom size) is not an adverse effect "against the national decision-making policies".
- ↑ [PINS-6011148](../cases/PINS-6011148.md) ¶24 (WR): L2 substantial weight plus HO7; no substantial adverse effect; PiP allowed.
- ◇ [PINS-6009919](../cases/PINS-6009919.md) ¶49 (WR): villages defined as part of the Green Belt "are not settlements for the purposes of the Framework", so S4 is not engaged and S5 does not apply. The first square application of the Annex B exclusion. Contra: [PINS-6010097](../cases/PINS-6010097.md) ¶15 ran S4 for a Green Belt site inside a village settlement boundary (dismissed on heritage; do not cite as S4 authority).

**S5(1)(e) limited infilling within groups of houses**
- ↓ [PINS-6008739](../cases/PINS-6008739.md) ¶8–10: one neighbour or the end of a row is not infill.
- ↓ [PINS-6010442](../cases/PINS-6010442.md) ¶20: extending the group is not "within" it.
- ↓ [PINS-6010354](../cases/PINS-6010354.md) ¶20–21: a group made of caravans or light industry, or a mobile-home neighbour, does not count.
- ↓ [PINS-6008264](../cases/PINS-6008264.md) ¶9–10: consented but unbuilt houses are disregarded. (The appeal was still allowed on an extant-PIP fallback.)
- ↑ [PINS-6009593](../cases/PINS-6009593.md) ¶15–16: a plot "sandwiched between existing dwellings". (e) needs no settlement and no "well-related" test.
- ↑ [PINS-6009632](../cases/PINS-6009632.md): a non-linear group, counting houses under construction.
- ↑ [PINS-6010973](../cases/PINS-6010973.md): passes even with a five-year supply and a new plan.

**S5(1)(j) evidenced unmet need plus "physically well-related"**
- ↓ [PINS-6007133](../cases/PINS-6007133.md) ¶40 (hearing): (j)(i) covers "how the proposal itself would relate to the surrounding character", not only the site's spatial relationship. Logistics scheme.
- ↓ [PINS-6008253](../cases/PINS-6008253.md) ¶80 (hearing): very close to the village, but limited harm to its setting fails (j)(i). Then allowed under S5(4) (see below).
- ↓ [PINS-6009838](../cases/PINS-6009838.md) ¶15, 44–49 (WR): the restrictive, form-based reading ("regardless of whether it would be of a scale…").
- ↓ [PINS-6008785](../cases/PINS-6008785.md) ¶39: (j) met, but lost via DP3(3) and S5(2).
- ↓ [PINS-6011337](../cases/PINS-6011337.md) ¶10, 22: where the council has a five-year supply, the need must be shown for the type of housing proposed. "There is no unmet need for housing in general."
- ↓ [PINS-6011872](../cases/PINS-6011872.md) ¶19–26: a self-build deficit counts as unmet need at 5.5 years, but "access to services" was read into the infrastructure limb.
- ↑ [PINS-6005809](../cases/PINS-6005809.md) ¶57–60 (inquiry): "including, but not limited to". A marginal compliant supply plus a housing register satisfies the need limb.
- ↑ [PINS-6001260](../cases/PINS-6001260.md) ¶24–25 (WR): no requirement to adjoin the settlement or to have a segregated footway.
- ↑ [PINS-6006893](../cases/PINS-6006893.md) ¶69 (inquiry): "physical connectivity, rather than landscape and visual terms". Dismissed on other grounds.
- ↑ [PINS-6008238](../cases/PINS-6008238.md) ¶22 (inquiry): a trunk road in cutting is "a soft connection", not a hard boundary. Allowed.

**S5(2) / S4(2) "should be refused" triggers**

- ↑ [PINS-6007730](../cases/PINS-6007730.md) ¶16–17: L2(1)(d) checked as an S4(2)(a)(ii) trigger at PIP stage and no "substantial adverse impact" found. [PINS-6006322](../cases/PINS-6006322.md) ¶36–37 does the same for a local wildlife site under N6(1)(c).
- ↓ [PINS-6009997](../cases/PINS-6009997.md) ¶14–15: TR6(4) splay over third-party hedge land beats 4 homes at 1.98 years.
- ↓ [stratford-26-01376-FUL](../cases/stratford-26-01376-FUL.md) (delegated): F7 via S4(2)(c). The only SDC trigger refusal.
- ↑ [PINS-6009340](../cases/PINS-6009340.md) ¶22: W3 substantial weight supplied DP3(3)'s "clear justification".
- ◇ [PINS-6010682](../cases/PINS-6010682.md): S5(2) was used to skip S5(4), and F5 treated as a trigger. Both are contestable.

**S5(4) outside every category**

## Green Belt

**GB7(1)(b) reuse, extension, replacement**
- ↓ [PINS-6008745](../cases/PINS-6008745.md): about 93% plus 13% over the original. A "desire for a larger house" is not grey-belt need (¶14).
- ↓ [PINS-6011691](../cases/PINS-6011691.md): a relocated barn is not part of the "original building" (fn40).
- ↓ [PINS-6010236](../cases/PINS-6010236.md): about 145% cumulative volume.
- ↑ [PINS-6010603](../cases/PINS-6010603.md): about 39–42% cumulative, set down and narrow. Openness is not a separate test once (b) is met.
- ↑ [PINS-6006761](../cases/PINS-6006761.md) ¶7–8: reuse of permanent buildings with no openness test (lost on TR6(4)).
- ◇ [PINS-6010313](../cases/PINS-6010313.md) ¶9/14: passed (b) then held inappropriate for failing (g). The categories are alternatives.

**GB7(1)(c) limited infilling in villages**
- ↓ [PINS-6010709](../cases/PINS-6010709.md) ¶8–11 (WR): the on-the-ground village test; dispersed houses are not a village, and a 1.3 ha vehicle store is not limited infilling. The only 2026 appeal analysis, and non-residential.
- ↓ [wychavon-W-26-01639-PIP](../cases/wychavon-W-26-01639-PIP.md) (delegated): extending along a lane is not infill.

**GB7(1)(e) PDL, not substantial openness harm**
- ↓ [APP-A2335-C-26-3378663](../cases/APP-A2335-C-26-3378663.md) ¶30 (hearing): "development that has never been lawful … is not capable of rendering land PDL".
- ↓ [PINS-6008579](../cases/PINS-6008579.md): extending a building that remains in place is not "redevelopment".
- ↓ [PINS-6011330](../cases/PINS-6011330.md): the whole site must be PDL; garden land beyond the stables' curtilage is not.
- ↓ [PINS-6011972](../cases/PINS-6011972.md): agreed PDL, but taller and bulkier houses cause substantial openness harm.
- ↑ [PINS-6012162](../cases/PINS-6012162.md) and [PINS-6012043](../cases/PINS-6012043.md): a garden or curtilage outside a built-up area is PDL, and the harm fell short of substantial.
- ↑ [PINS-6009189](../cases/PINS-6009189.md): equestrian buildings are PDL; consolidated built form.
- ↑ [PINS-6010198](../cases/PINS-6010198.md) ¶12–14: a low-density lane-side area without lighting, footways or building lines is not a built-up area, so the plot is PDL; a materially larger replacement still passes (e), as size "is not the test".

**GB7(1)(g)(i) grey belt**
- ↓ [PINS-6009281](../cases/PINS-6009281.md) ¶15–17: an uncontained site at a town edge, open on three sides. This is the only appeal-level rejection. ◇ It imports purpose (c).
- ↓ [basildon-25-01190-OUT](../cases/basildon-25-01190-OUT.md) (committee): purpose (b), where the site is a substantial part of a ~2.3 km gap between towns.
- ↑ [PINS-6011103](../cases/PINS-6011103.md) ¶12–16: a village is not a large built-up area (Annex E ¶3). The members' overturn was reversed; costs were refused.
- ↑ [PINS-6010471](../cases/PINS-6010471.md) ¶12–15: assess the site, not the parcel; purpose (c) is irrelevant.
- ↑ [PINS-6007184](../cases/PINS-6007184.md) (hearing): a contained parcel prevails against a "strong" study rating; Golden Rules met.
- ↓ [PINS-6008723](../cases/PINS-6008723.md) ¶14 (WR): a village in "a continuous urban form" with a town, treated with it by the Local Plan, is part of the same large built-up area.
- ◇ [APP-P0119-C-26-3378284](../cases/APP-P0119-C-26-3378284.md) ¶36–41 (hearing): a village absorbed into the Bristol conurbation *is* part of a large built-up area; "in a meaningful way" (¶41).

**GB7(1)(g)(ii) evidenced unmet need of this type**

- ↓ [PINS-6012188](../cases/PINS-6012188.md) ¶15–16: yoga studio.
