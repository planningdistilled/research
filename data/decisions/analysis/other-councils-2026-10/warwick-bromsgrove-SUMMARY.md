## (b) How each council handles the four points

**Warwick District Council.** Three scorable reports, all by the same two case officers, and the pattern is Stratford's. Location is settled by the Local Plan H1 tier and a boundary with no route fact: Norton Lindsey is a washed-over Limited Infill Village and the GB7(1)(g)(iii) test is dispatched in one sentence, (p.5) "The application site is located within the settlement boundary of Norton Lindsey, which includes a number of facilities for day to day living and therefore the proposal is considered to be in compliance with Policy TR3 of the Framework". The same report lists and quotes S4 as the principle policy (p.3) for a site it has just described as "washed over by Green Belt" (p.1), never mentions S5(5) or Annex B, then runs GB6 and GB7 grey belt properly on the back of a 2025 appeal decision. Warwick's local plan has its own "TR3 - Parking", so every Warwick report mentions "TR3" while the NPPF policy is unreasoned (Norton Lindsey) or absent (Cubbington, Bridge End). On DP3(3) Warwick found no design conflict in any of the five, so there was nothing to trigger; DP3 paragraph 1 is quoted as a design aspiration and paragraph 3 never appears. Two further Warwick habits worth noting: the 2024-Framework Cubbington committee report was issued as a decision on 7 October 2026 with no 2026 re-anchoring at all, and the Bridge End refusal quotes F7(2) "should be refused" (p.10) but never takes the S4(2)(c) step it sets up at p.3.

**Bromsgrove District Council.** Eight reports, published as reasoned decision notices rather than separate delegated reports, and markedly better on the first three points. On Green Belt sites Bromsgrove's template quotes the S3 split and then (Beoley p.2) "S5 does not apply to development proposals in the Green Belt and that these proposals should be determined in accordance with NDMP GB6 and GB7", runs GB7(1)(a) to (f) as a closed list and then (g), and in Rubery closes with the S5(5) balance in terms: (p.9) "NDMP S5 (5) states that where development would not be inappropriate in the Green Belt" proposals should be approved unless substantially outweighed. The GB7(1)(g)(iii) heading is answered with route facts in four of five grey belt cases; Beoley is the model, with footway side, 40 mph limit, lighting, distances to each facility, a once-a-week bus, the Bournheath PIP appeal on the "significant amount of movement" threshold, and the conclusion (p.4) "the site is not considered to offer a genuine choice of transport modes for future residents who are likely to be reliant on use of the private car". The lapses are at the small end: Fairfield's PIP (1 bungalow) dodges TR3(1)(a) by scale and rests location on a facilities list plus "served by a bus route"; Barnt Green's short-form notice settles location with "located within an established settlement" (p.2); Crabmill Lane invokes S5(1)(j) on a Green Belt site in one paragraph before running GB7(1)(g) properly. The two in-town or inset reports (York Close, Barnt Green) do not mention NPPF TR3 at all. On DP3(3) Bromsgrove is the same as Stratford: the one report that found a character conflict (Beoley, against BDP19, BDP21 and N2) carried it into the Green Belt balance as "other identified harms" without the clear-justification question, exactly the Gorcott Hill pattern, and like Gorcott Hill it was a refusal so the step could not have changed the outcome.

## (c) Where the Stratford coding looks unfair, or where other officers plainly read the policy differently

- **TR3 "not-mentioned" for small schemes inside settlements.** Five of the five "not-mentioned" rows here are 1 to 4 homes, three of them inside a town or an inset village (Bridge End 1, York Close 2, Barnt Green 2). No officer in either council treats TR3(1)(a) as engaged by a scheme of that size inside a settlement, and Bromsgrove's Fairfield report says in terms that one dwelling "would not generate significant amounts of movement". The Stratford table already flags that the threshold point is doubtful for single dwellings; this set suggests the bar for "not-mentioned" as an *error* should start at the GB7(1)(g)(iii) cases and at schemes of several homes outside a settlement, where TR3 is expressly imported. Recording the fact is still right; counting it as a failure for a 1-bed garage conversion in Warwick town is not.
- **Connectivity Tool.** 0 of 13 here, 0 of 44 at Stratford. Including the best-reasoned report in the whole set (Beoley), which did the work the Tool is meant to evidence by hand. A code every council fails is not measuring officer error; TR3(2)'s "should be used" is being read everywhere as a plan-making and major-scheme expectation. The project's own memory note (TR3(2) is one input, never a standalone gap) matches what these reports show.
- **"partial" sweeps up very different things.** Cubbington (tier plus a conditioned footway link), York Close (four distances to bus, shops, footpath, school inside a town) and Fairfield (facilities plus "a bus route") all code "partial". The Stratford rule that any transport fact beside a tier finding is partial is applied here as instructed, but York Close is close to a genuine access assessment and Fairfield is close to an error. A reader comparing rates should know "partial" is a wide band.
- **DP3(3) for refusals.** Beoley is coded error on the Gorcott Hill rule. Both are refusals where the character conflict was itself a reason for refusal. Officers in both councils clearly read "should be refused ... without clear justification" as satisfied once the harm is found and the scheme is refused; the missing step is the explicit "is there a clear justification" question, which on a refusal is a formality. The rubric is consistent, but a count that presents Beoley and a Stratford approval like Stockton as the same error overstates the point; the note column separates them and the summary should too.
- **Annex B "cited" column.** In every one of the 5 Bromsgrove citations Annex B is cited for the grey belt definition. That is also the Stratford pattern. The column as it stands measures whether officers have read the glossary at all, not whether they applied the "settlement" exclusion; the latter is what matters and it is zero in both samples. The Bromsgrove route (S5(5) first, GB7, S5(5) balance) gets to the right answer without ever reading the definition, so "annexb_cited = no" does not predict "annexb = error".
- **Bromsgrove reaches S5(5) by a different textual route.** None of the Bromsgrove reports cites S5(5) by number except Rubery, yet five of six take the right path, because their template quotes S3's S4/S5 split and then a one-line paraphrase of S5(5). The Stratford coding (ok where the route is respected) handles this correctly; a stricter "must cite S5(5)" rule would wrongly fail them.
- **A point against the councils, not the rubric.** Both councils' templates import TR3 only through GB7(1)(g)(iii). Where a Green Belt scheme passes under another limb (GB7(1)(b), (c) or (e)) or is in a washed-over village, TR3 falls out of the report entirely (Finstall; Norton Lindsey only named it because grey belt was argued). That is the same gap the Stratford table shows and the rubric is right to record it.

## Repeat instructions

Both portals are Idox. One cookie jar per council for the whole session, browser User-Agent, `/usr/bin/curl`, https only for Warwick.

1. `GET {base}/search.do?action=advanced` and read `name="_csrf" value="..."` from the form.
2. `POST {base}/advancedSearchResults.do?action=firstPage` with form fields `_csrf`, `searchType=Application`, `searchCriteria.caseStatus=` (blank), `date(applicationDecisionStart)=01/09/2026`, `date(applicationDecisionEnd)=17/10/2026`.
3. `GET {base}/pagedSearchResults.do?action=page&searchCriteria.page=N&searchCriteria.resultsPerPage=100` for N = 1.. until a page returns 0 `class="searchresult"` items (Warwick: 273 results, 3 pages; Bromsgrove and Redditch: 161, 2 pages). Each result gives `keyVal`, address, description and status in `summaryLinkTextClamp`, `address`, `metaInfo`.
4. For each candidate: `GET {base}/applicationDetails.do?activeTab=summary|details|documents&keyVal={keyVal}`; the documents tab lists `/online-applications/files/{hash}/pdf/{name}.pdf` links, downloadable in the same session.
5. `pdftotext -layout` each PDF; pages are form-feed separated.
