## Summary

An overview of the August 2026 National Planning Policy Framework (NPPF) by a law firm. It covers Spatial Development Strategies (SDSs), small-site allocations, support for development near well-connected stations, and the replacement of the paragraph 11 tilted balance with S4 (within settlements) and S5 (outside settlements). It notes that S5 support can apply where there is no five-year housing land supply or the Housing Delivery Test (HDT) result is below 75%. It also covers the simpler grey belt definition ("does not strongly contribute to any of purposes (a), (b), or (d)"), the new Annex E guidance on Green Belt assessments, Biodiversity Net Gain (BNG) evidence, TR4 street design and DP3 safety for women and girls. It advises developers to revisit sites that were previously constrained, especially near stations.

## What it tells officers/decision-makers to do

- Approve development within settlements unless adverse effects substantially outweigh the benefits (S4).
- Recognise S5 support outside settlements in specified circumstances, including a supply or delivery shortfall.
- Still carry out a detailed analysis of whether land is grey belt. The five Green Belt purposes remain.
- Councils seeking BNG above the statutory minimum must justify it with robust evidence.

## Positions against our propositions

- **SUP, agrees.** "Development outside settlements may also benefit from policy support in specified circumstances, including where authorities cannot demonstrate a five-year housing land supply or have Housing Delivery Test results below 75%." (section on S4/S5). A shortfall "may" bring S5 support. It opens the gate but is not presented as deciding the case.
