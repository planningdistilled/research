# NPPF: More focus needed on brownfield and affordable homes, we say (CPRE with English Rural, Kerry Harrison)

Note: cpre.org.uk returns 403 to direct download; text read via WebFetch only. Despite the brief, this page is dated 19 December 2023 and responds to the December 2023 NPPF, not the 2026 draft or Framework.

## Summary

CPRE's reaction to the December 2023 Framework. It welcomes the then changes to five-year land supply policy, saying speculative development had harmed the countryside and produced car-dependent executive homes. It calls for a brownfield-first policy with a 60% brownfield target, social-rent targets, and a Land Use Framework. It has no bearing on how the August 2026 Framework is applied.
