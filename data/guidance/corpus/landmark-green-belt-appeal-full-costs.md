## Summary

A three-sentence case note: Rosconn Group won permission on appeal (reference 6005246) for up to 80 homes on an unallocated greenfield Green Belt site in Solihull, with a full award of costs against the council; Rob Walton KC acted. The key issues were flood risk and whether the scheme was inappropriate development in the Green Belt. The note does not say which edition of the NPPF was applied, whether the site was found to be grey belt, or where it sits relative to a village. It gives no guidance on applying the 2026 Framework and takes no position on our propositions.

## What it tells officers/decision-makers to do

- Nothing explicit. By implication, a Green Belt refusal on an unallocated greenfield site can attract a full costs award if not substantiated.
