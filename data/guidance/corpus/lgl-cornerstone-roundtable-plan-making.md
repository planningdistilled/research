# Cornerstone Barristers roundtable on the Draft NPPF: Plan-Making Provisions

## Summary

Listing on Local Government Lawyer for a free, discussion-led two-hour Cornerstone roundtable on 9 February 2026 about the plan-making provisions of the draft NPPF, 'primarily aimed at local authority lawyers and officers'. Its stated aim is what practitioners and authorities should say in response to the consultation. The panel included Cornerstone barristers, Catriona Riddell and a Chelmsford City Council spatial planning manager. It is evidence of how council lawyers and officers were briefed at draft stage, but gives no content on decision-making policies.
