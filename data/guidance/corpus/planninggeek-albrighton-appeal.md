# Albrighton: Secretary of State recovers 800-home appeal

## Summary

Reports the Secretary of State's recovery (direction of 16 Sep 2026) of appeal 6007402 for up to 800 homes, a care home, a secondary school and a local centre on Green Belt land at Patshull Road, Albrighton (Shropshire). The case is recovered as significant Green Belt development raising novel issues. The council argues inappropriate development and disputes whether Albrighton is a large built-up area for sprawl purposes. The appellant relies on GB7(1)(g), with a very special circumstances fallback. The article states that grey-belt status alone is insufficient for GB7(1)(g), notes that the Inspector's main issues include spatial strategy and access to services, and warns that recovery does not signal the outcome.

## What it tells officers/decision-makers to do

- Treat grey-belt status as one requirement only; GB7(1)(g) also needs unmet need, a sustainable location and the Golden Rules.
- Where development is inappropriate, apply GB6 very special circumstances (harm clearly outweighed).
- Assess the provision of schools and facilities for need, delivery and weight rather than accepting it at face value.
- Do not read recovery as a signal of the outcome.

## Positions against our propositions

- **GB (grey belt / Green Belt): agrees.** "Under the August 2026 NPPF, grey belt status alone is insufficient for the GB7(1)(g) route." (section "The central dispute is whether the grey belt route applies"). Passing the grey-belt definition is not approval.
- **METH (our method): silent.** The line "There is currently no decision to treat as authority for allowing or refusing a comparable scheme. Any eventual decision will need to be read with the Inspector’s report and the particular evidence before it." (section "Why practitioners should follow the eventual decision") is a caveat that this appeal is still undecided, not a critique of using appeal decisions as evidence. The same section says the eventual decision "may help illustrate how the August 2026 Green Belt policies are applied", which fits our approach. Reading a decision with its evidence is ordinary practice and matches how we code findings. Not coded as a qualification.
