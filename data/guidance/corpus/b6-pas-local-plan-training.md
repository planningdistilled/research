## Summary

The PAS page for its local-plan training offer for councillors and officers. The live page returns 403. The only archived copy found is from 5 August 2025, so it comes before the August 2026 Framework and says nothing about it. PAS offers a set of PowerPoint presentations that councils can deliver themselves, or a half-day session led by PAS (normally charged for) that can include a councillor peer. The tips stress tailoring the slides, adding quizzes, involving officers at different levels, and not making this the only member training in the year. It covers plan-making only, not how to decide applications.

## What it tells officers/decision-makers to do

- Train members regularly on the current and emerging local-plan systems, using PAS slides adapted locally (for example, a slide on the council's land-supply position).
- Hold separate training on new issues "such as policy adoption or changes in legislation".
- Bring officers and councillors together in training, away from formal public meetings.
