## Summary

The last Chief Planner newsletter before the August 2026 National Planning Policy Framework (NPPF). Despite the draft Framework having been out for consultation since December 2025, it does not mention the NPPF, its expected timing or any transitional handling at all. It covers the new plan-making system (mandatory data standards for local plans from 7 May 2026, indicative plan timetables, Strategic Environmental Assessment), Active Travel England's (ATE) new statutory role in new-style local plans from 25 March 2026 and its planned Spatial Plan Service, compulsory purchase, Department for Transport cost-recovery regulations, a Historic England guide on archaeology and solar farms, and capacity schemes (Nationally Significant Infrastructure Project Innovation and Capacity Fund, Public Practice, Pathways to Planning graduate intake, the Planning Skills and Capacity Survey).

## What it tells officers/decision-makers to do

- Publish local plan timetables and housing requirements in the new data standard.
- Invite ATE to comment on new-style local plans and supplementary plans.
- Use capacity schemes (Pathways to Planning, Public Practice) to recruit.
- Nothing about preparing for, or applying, the forthcoming NPPF.

## Positions against our propositions

- None. Silent on all propositions, and silent on the NPPF itself.

## Leads

- ATE Spatial Plan Service and new guidance (active travel evidence in site allocations; possible relevance to TR3 walking-route evidence).
- ATE "Making Active Places 2026" conference (Manchester, September 2026): officer training on highway and active travel design.
- Public Practice report "Planning Teams for the Future".
