## Summary

Legal news note published on the day the August 2026 NPPF came out. Only the free summary is readable; the body is behind the Lexis+ paywall. The summary says the new NPPF separates plan-making and decision-making policy and changes the presumption in favour of sustainable development, development around well-connected stations, housing density, Green Belt policy and viability. It says the same-day consultation response took forward most December 2025 proposals with changes, including minimum densities around stations, wider application of the station policy, new strategic-sites provisions and changes to Protected Landscapes. It promises more detailed decision-making and plan-making analysis later.
