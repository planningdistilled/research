# What does the 2026 NPPF say about construction traffic? (Iceni Projects, Simon Possee)

## Summary

A short note on TR6(4), which says development should be refused for a severe adverse network impact or an unacceptable highway-safety impact, and which "applies both during the construction phase and following completion". Iceni says construction traffic was assessed in Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA) transport chapters but not in Transport Assessments, and was not a reason for refusal. Under the new policy, construction traffic data and a cumulative construction assessment will be needed at application stage, not when the Construction Management Plan is discharged. It says nothing on walking-route quality or access to services (TR3).

## What it tells officers/decision-makers to do

- Consider construction-phase traffic impact, including cumulative impact, at application stage under TR6(4).
- Agree the cumulative construction sites to assess at pre-application stage.
- Consider construction traffic for all developments, even where operational traffic does not need assessing.

## Positions against our propositions

- Silent on all our propositions.

## Leads

- Appeals refused or allowed on construction-phase traffic under TR6(4).
