# Planning practice guidance (collection)

## Summary
This is the index page for the government's Planning Practice Guidance (PPG), the official guidance that sits under the National Planning Policy Framework (NPPF). Its only change linked to the August 2026 Framework is a 23 September 2026 entry: "Updated link to the National Planning Policy Framework, which was revised 17 August 2026." The topic pages most relevant to our work have not been revised for the 2026 Framework: Green Belt is dated 27 February 2025, Housing supply and delivery 12 December 2024, Rural housing 19 May 2016, Historic environment 23 July 2019, and Transport evidence bases 13 March 2015. The only companion documents updated around publication are the Housing Delivery Test (HDT) measurement rule book (17 August 2026) and Planning policy for traveller sites (27 August 2026). So, six weeks after publication, officers are applying the new policy codes (S3-S6, TR3, GB1-GB8, HE6) with practice guidance that still cites December 2024 paragraph numbers. (The brief gave the collection date as 27 August 2026; the page itself says it was last updated on 23 September 2026.)

## What it tells officers/decision-makers to do
- Read the NPPF together with the PPG topic pages. The page gives no transitional note on how to read guidance that cites the old paragraph numbers.
- For housing delivery, use the HDT measurement rule book, which was updated on 17 August 2026.
- Traveller-site policy now sits in the NPPF itself (see the separate traveller sites entry).

## Positions against our propositions
None. The page is an index and takes no position on any proposition. Its value is as evidence of a gap: there is no 2026-specific practice guidance on settlements, walking routes (TR3), grey belt (GB7) or the S5 categories.
