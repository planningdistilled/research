## Summary

A short statement by TCPA Chief Executive Fiona Howie on the day the draft National Planning Policy Framework (NPPF) was published (16 December 2025). It welcomes climate change being one of twelve key policy changes, but regrets that the draft no longer sets out planning's role in health and tackling health inequalities, and that the Garden City Principles were removed. It agrees there should be a period of stability once the Framework is final. It gives no guidance on applying the Framework.
