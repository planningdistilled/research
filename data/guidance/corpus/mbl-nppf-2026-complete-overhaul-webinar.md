## Summary

This is a paid one-hour CPD webinar by Bob Pritchard, available from 13 October 2026. It costs £199 at the standard price, or £99 to £149 for subscribers. The course description is still written for the December 2025 consultation draft. It says the Framework "proposes" national development management policies and tells delegates to "engage with the draft". So the description, and possibly the recorded content, may describe draft rather than final wording. The syllabus covers: the numbered-policy structure; national decision-making policies; "the new presumption in favour of sustainable development based on whether development is within or outside a settlement"; Green Belt; development near railway stations; the medium development category; heritage; and new policies such as swift bricks and chalk streams. The course is aimed at people with no prior knowledge (Solicitors Regulation Authority, or SRA, competency level B "Update"). It is typical of the commercial training market from which officers buy CPD.

## What it tells officers/decision-makers to do

- Nothing substantive beyond the syllabus. The course is built around the within- or outside-settlement split as the organising idea of the presumption.
- Risk to note: the description uses consultation-stage language, so attendees may learn draft text (for example the draft Golden Rules or density figures) rather than the final policy.

## Positions against our propositions

None. The syllabus line on the presumption depending on "whether development is within or outside a settlement" matches our S3/S4/S5 framing. It does not address local tiers (SH1), washed-over villages (SH2) or any other proposition, so it is not coded.
