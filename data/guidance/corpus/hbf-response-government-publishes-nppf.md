# HBF response: Government publishes long-awaited NPPF (Home Builders Federation)

## Summary

The housebuilders' trade body welcomes the August 2026 National Planning Policy Framework (NPPF): the 10% small-sites allocation and limits on local biodiversity net gain (BNG) demands above the statutory level. Its main concern for decisions is the presumption in favour of development around railway stations: the requirement that the whole site be within 800 metres or a 10-minute walk of a station could limit its use. It adds that housing supply depends on more than planning (tax and policy costs). A member briefing and members-only webinar (3 September 2026) are mentioned but not public.

## What it tells officers/decision-makers to do

- Nothing directly. It signals that developers will test the station-presumption distance requirement.

## Positions against our propositions

- **TR (not applicable; different test)**: the HBF concern is about the separate presumption in favour of development around railway stations, where the Framework sets a whole-site 800 metres / 10-minute-walk threshold. It says nothing about how TR3 or GB7(1)(g)(iii) access should be judged, so it neither supports nor qualifies our route-quality-not-distance proposition. "requirements for the whole of a site to be within 800 metres or a 10-minute walk of a train station could limit the practical application of the new presumption in favour of development around railway stations." (paragraph 4). Worth noting only as a reminder that the station presumption is a distance-gated route, distinct from TR3.
