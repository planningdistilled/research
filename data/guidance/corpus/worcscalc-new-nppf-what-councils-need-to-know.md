## Summary

This is an event listing, not guidance. The Worcestershire County Association of Local Councils (CALC) lists a one-hour Zoom webinar on the August 2026 National Planning Policy Framework (NPPF), run by the Yorkshire Local Councils Associations (YLCA), on Wednesday 11 November 2026 at £18.80 per delegate. It describes the Framework as "the most substantial rewrite of planning rules in at least a decade", aimed at certainty and consistency and at reducing "unjustified local variation". The webinar will "explain the main changes" for councillors and clerks. The page contains no content on how the policies apply. Its value is as evidence of how training reaches parish councils: a single Yorkshire provider sells the session to other counties' associations.

## What it tells officers/decision-makers to do

Nothing substantive. Councils must approve attendance, and delegates receive the slides and handouts after the session.

## Positions against our propositions

None.
