## Summary

The GOV.UK hub page for the National Planning Policy Framework (NPPF) consultation that ran from 16 December 2025 to 10 March 2026, updated on 17 August 2026 with the government response (155 pages) and the Public Sector Equality Duty (PSED) assessment (49 pages). It links the consultation document, the draft NPPF text, the question list and an Easy Read version. It also records three corrections made to the consultation document during the consultation, one of which removed a sentence about giving plan policies "due weight" according to their consistency with the Framework from the transitional arrangements section. It contains no guidance on applying the Framework beyond those links and corrections.

## What it tells officers/decision-makers to do

- Nothing directly; it points readers to the government response and the updated Framework.
- The correction notes show that the draft's transitional arrangements were not intended to carry forward the old "due weight according to consistency" approach.

## Positions against our propositions

- **A2 - agrees.** "On page 106 regarding transitional arrangements, we have removed the sentence ‘Therefore, the draft Framework sets out that due weight should be given to development plan policies relative to the consistency with the Framework’ for clarity." (Detail of outcome / original consultation corrections, 29 January 2026 update). The graded "due weight by consistency" test was dropped in favour of the Annex A para 2 rule that cuts only materially inconsistent policies or parts; consistent policies are not discounted. Indirect support, from a correction note rather than policy text.
