## Summary

The Planning Advisory Service (PAS) archive of its 2026 events for council planning officers. On the National Planning Policy Framework (NPPF), it lists the January to February 2026 roadshows and online deep dives on the December 2025 draft Framework: a reform overview with the Chief Planner, an NPPF overview with the Ministry of Housing, Communities and Local Government (MHCLG), and deep dives on "Stations, density, and Green Belt" (28 January), "Economy, retail, communities and transport" (29 January) and "Climate Change, Flood Risk, & Natural Environment" (30 January), all with YouTube recordings. Later entries cover appeals changes (17 March), planning committee reform (April, May, July), biodiversity net gain (May), nature recovery (June) and standard section 106 agreements (September). The page has no event on the published August 2026 Framework. The October 2026 roadshows and the November "development boundaries" deep dive described in the task brief are not on this page (the PAS "future events" URL returned "Page not found"). The direct fetch returned 403; the local copy was taken through a reader proxy (r.jina.ai) on 2 October 2026, and an Internet Archive capture of 29 January 2026 is also kept (b4-pas-2026-events.wayback.txt).

## What it tells officers/decision-makers to do

- From 1 April 2026, list all plans and documents assessed on decision notices, because part 1 written-representation appeals rely only on the application file.
- Put any supporting evidence, such as "other inspectorate decisions", in the officer report, because nothing else can be added at appeal.
- Get ready for planning committee reform and the national scheme of delegation, in force 31 October 2026: update constitutions, run the "gateway" test for Schedule 2 applications and record reasons.
