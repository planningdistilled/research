## Summary

This is the Planning Advisory Service (PAS) home page. PAS is part of the Local Government Association (LGA) and is funded by the Ministry of Housing, Communities and Local Government (MHCLG). The live page returns HTTP 403 (a Cloudflare bot check). The local copy is the Internet Archive snapshot of 23 September 2026. That snapshot shows only the headline list for the "PAS Bulletin August 2026", whose items include "NPPF events", "Councillor training: Local Plan leaders" and "Planning committee reforms: best practice guidance now available". It does not include the details of the officer-training programme that the search snippets described: a fireside chat with the Head of PAS and the Chief Planner on 14 Oct 2026, development-management roadshows in Birmingham, London, Bath and Manchester on 6 to 22 Oct 2026, and online deep dives in November, including "The new approach to development inside and outside settlement boundaries" on 3 Nov. Those details could not be checked against a saved copy. The page itself gives no guidance on how to apply the 2026 Framework.
