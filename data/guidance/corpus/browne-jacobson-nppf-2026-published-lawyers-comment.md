## Summary

A press release published on the day the National Planning Policy Framework (NPPF) came out, with comment from two planning partners. Ben Standing (public sector) calls it "a seismic shake-up" and says government is "replacing the carrot with the stick". Councils must keep local plans up to date or risk development where it is cheapest to build, without the chance to shape places. Will Thomas (private sector) welcomes the support for housebuilding but warns that viability, financing and infrastructure costs cloud long-term delivery. It gives no guidance on how individual policies should be applied in decisions.

## What it tells officers/decision-makers to do

- Councils should keep local plans continuously up to date, to keep control over where development goes.
- Tie development to visible local infrastructure gains (roads, schools, GP surgeries) to keep community support.
- No policy-by-policy guidance for decision-making.

## Positions against our propositions

None. The release is silent on all of our propositions (SH1, SH2, TR, S5, SUP, GB, A2, DIV, METH).
