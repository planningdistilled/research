## Summary

A campaign guide for local groups on the December 2024 NPPF (page dated 26 November 2025); it says nothing about the August 2026 Framework. It walks through fossil fuels, renewables, energy efficiency, nature, housing, Green Belt and grey belt, and local plan transition, and tells campaigners how to use each section. On Green Belt it explains grey belt as "not inappropriate development" where conditions are met, warns of ad-hoc release and cumulative harm, and notes early appeals allowed under the grey belt changes. It does not deal with settlement tiers, walking routes, or plan-policy weight.

## What it tells officers/decision-makers to do

- Aimed at campaigners, not officers: use the "significant weight" for renewables and the climate-change consideration in support of good schemes.
- Watch for grey belt applications and argue cumulative harm to Green Belt purposes and the "sustainable locations" caveat.
- Expect more appeals and legal challenges on whether Green Belt purposes are undermined.

## Positions against our propositions

- **GB — silent.** The page reports that "two recent planning appeals (one for a minor housing scheme and another for battery storage) have already been allowed because of these grey belt policy amendments" (section "The green belt and the grey belt"). This is a factual note about early outcomes under paragraph 155 of the December 2024 Framework, not a claim that grey belt status alone wins permission. It does not address whether passing the grey belt test is approval, very special circumstances, or the GB6-GB8 policies of the August 2026 NPPF, so it neither supports nor qualifies our proposition.
