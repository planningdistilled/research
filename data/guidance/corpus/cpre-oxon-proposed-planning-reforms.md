# Proposed Planning Reforms Raise Serious Concerns for Oxfordshire's Countryside

## Summary

A CPRE Oxfordshire post on the December 2025 draft NPPF identifying four county concerns: a default 'yes' for development around rail stations (including Green Belt sites), a requirement to notify the Secretary of State of refusals of more than 150 homes, fewer statutory consultees (Sport England, the Gardens Trust, the Theatres Trust; less input from National Highways and Active Travel England), and abolition of the Duty to Cooperate. It links to the branch's consultation response. It gives no guidance on applying the final August 2026 Framework and does not touch our propositions.

## What it tells officers/decision-makers to do

- Nothing addressed to officers.

## Positions against our propositions

- Silent on all our propositions.
