# Exploring the new NPPF draft, Monday 12 January 2026 (slides, Landmark Chambers and LPDF)

## Summary

Slide deck for the Landmark Chambers and LPDF webinar on the December 2025 draft NPPF. Most slides reproduce draft policy text (PM2, PM4, PM6 on plan content; draft Annex A para 2; S4 and S5) with headings and questions rather than commentary. The draft Annex A slide shows the wider wording later dropped: plan policies 'in any way inconsistent' with the national decision-making policies get very limited weight. A stations slide maps the routes: S4 inside settlements, S5(1)(h) outside settlements, and GB7(1)(h) with S5(5) in the Green Belt. The draft S5(1)(j) text used 'well related to an existing settlement', not the final 'physically well-related'. The speaker notes are not in the PDF, so the oral guidance is not recorded.

## What it tells officers/decision-makers to do

- Ask whether the draft NDMPs (national decision-making policies) are strong enough against a 'kaleidoscopic' local plan context, and how the S4/S5 tilt affects the weight of adopted and emerging plan policies.
- For stations, use S4 inside settlements, S5(1)(h) outside settlements outside the Green Belt, and GB7(1)(h) with S5(5) in the Green Belt.
- Follow the transitional-arrangements flowchart (Twenty5) for plan-making deadlines (30 June 2026, 31 October 2026, 31 December 2026).

## Positions against our propositions

- **A2 – silent (draft text only)** (slide 'Out of date; transition and effect on 2024 NPPF plans' (Annex A para 2, draft text)): "Development plan policies which are in any way inconsistent with the national decision-making policies in this Framework should be given very limited weight" The slide reproduces the December 2025 draft wording with no commentary. It does not say whether the whole policy or only the inconsistent clause loses weight, and it does not touch heritage, design or access policies, so it takes no position on A2. Useful only as background: the draft threshold was 'in any way inconsistent', and the final August 2026 text is narrower (see the Cornerstone article). The oral commentary is not in the PDF.
- **S5 – agrees** (slide 'Outside settlements' (draft S5(1))): "1. Only certain forms of development should be approved outside settlements, as set out in the following list." Restates the closed-list structure of S5(1) that underpins our reading that the category is the whole case outside settlements. Draft text.
