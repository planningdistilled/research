## Summary

A Planning Policy Manager's report seeking Portfolio Holder approval of Gedling's consultation response (the response itself is Appendix 1, not in this file). It summarises the draft's 12 headline reforms and the plan-making changes, and gives members an officer reading of what the new Framework will do to the existing local plan: decision-making policies "will still carry very considerable weight" and will make inconsistent local plan policies, "however recently adopted", out of date and of little weight. It also notes the grey belt definition change (removal of "footnote 7" areas) and Green Belt reviews becoming routine.

## What it tells officers/decision-makers to do

- Treat the new national decision-making policies as carrying very considerable weight although non-statutory.
- On publication, treat inconsistent local plan policies as out of date with little weight, however recently adopted.
- Expect the emerging Gedling plan (prepared under the 2024 NPPF) to be affected.

## Positions against our propositions

- **A2 — silent (restates the draft; does not engage the clause-versus-whole-policy question).** Para 1.3: "once adopted these policies will render out of date any existing local plan policies which are inconsistent with them." Para 1.12: "on publication existing local plan policies which are inconsistent with the NPPF decision making policies, however recently adopted, will be considered out of date and be accorded little weight in decision making." This is a February 2026 consultation-stage summary for members of what the draft Framework proposed, not an officer reading of the final Annex A. It says nothing about whether only the inconsistent part of a policy loses weight, or about heritage, design, access or spatial-preference policies, so it is not evidence against A2. (Adversarial check, 2 Oct 2026: quotes verbatim; stance downgraded from "disagrees".) The final Annex A para 2 cuts "policies (or parts of those policies) which are materially inconsistent"; a post-August 2026 Gedling report would be needed to test A2.
