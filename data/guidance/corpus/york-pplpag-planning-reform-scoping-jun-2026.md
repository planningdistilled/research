## Summary

This is a report by the Head of Strategic Planning Policy to York's member advisory group. It briefs members on the planning reforms, and on the December 2025 consultation draft of the National Planning Policy Framework (NPPF) in particular. It then seeks endorsement for a Local Plan scoping consultation under the 2026 Local Planning Regulations. The NPPF section is a high-level summary of the draft. It covers the split between plan-making and national decision-making policies, a "more rules-based" Framework, a "revised presumption in favour of development in suitable locations", growth within settlements with "a more selective approach in the countryside", and grey belt reform. The report expects the final Framework to bring "more clearly defined policies for decision-taking". It does not explain how any decision test works.

## What it tells officers/decision-makers to do

- Note that national decision-making policies are intended to give "a comprehensive and consistent basis for development management across England", so the Local Plan should not duplicate them.
- Note that an up-to-date Local Plan is the main protection against speculative development and supports a five-year housing land supply.
- Endorse a scoping consultation as the first step towards Gateway 1 (October 2026) and the 30-month plan timetable.

## Positions against our propositions

None. The report summarises the direction of the draft (for example "supporting growth within settlements while adopting a more selective approach in the countryside") without reading any test we code.
