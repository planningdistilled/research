## Summary

An investor briefing published five days before the final NPPF. It covers the Planning and Infrastructure Act 2025, the revised NPPF, devolution, the New Towns programme and building safety. On the NPPF it gives only headline bullets: more weight to meeting housing need, more pressure on authorities with out-of-date plans, more explicit Green Belt review and release guidance, and a shift "from discretionary decision-making toward more predictable outcomes". It gives no guidance on applying any specific policy code. Marginal for our purposes.

## What it tells officers/decision-makers to do

- Nothing specific. For investors: expect more predictable outcomes, opportunity where plans are out of date, and more scrutiny of affordable housing, viability and deliverability.

## Positions against our propositions

- None coded. It says "Increased opportunity in areas with planning policy gaps or delays" and "Greater weight given to meeting housing need" (section "NPPF reform – stronger presumption in favour of delivery"), which touches on SUP (housing land supply) but does not say whether supply decides cases; too generic to code.
