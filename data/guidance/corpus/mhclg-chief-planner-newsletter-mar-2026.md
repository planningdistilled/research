## Summary

Chief Planner newsletter written while the draft National Planning Policy Framework (NPPF) was under consultation. It does not discuss the draft Framework. Items relevant to how decisions are made: a consultation on a national scheme of delegation and a cap on committee members (Planning and Infrastructure Act 2025), and the extension of the expedited written-representations appeal procedure to all section 78(1) planning appeals for applications made on or after 1 April 2026, with the Planning Inspectorate's procedural guide. Other items cover New Towns, the Land Use Framework, locally set planning fees, a new consultation direction, onshore wind permitted development, about £50 million of capacity grants and the 2026 Planning Skills and Capacity Survey ("want to access more training opportunities").

## What it tells officers/decision-makers to do

- Respond to the national scheme of delegation consultation (by 23 April 2026).
- Follow the Inspectorate's updated procedural guide for appeals on applications dated on or after 1 April 2026 (expedited written representations).
- Complete the Planning Skills and Capacity Survey to inform workforce and training support.

## Positions against our propositions

- None. Silent on all propositions.

## Leads

- Planning Inspectorate, "Planning appeals: procedural guide" for applications dated on or after 1 April 2026: expedited written representations change the appeal evidence base our METH relies on (shorter, written-only decisions for most 2026 appeals).
- Planning committee reform: statutory consultation on draft regulations and guidance; government response to the 2025 technical consultation (who decides: officers vs members, relevant to DIV).
- 2026 Planning Skills and Capacity Survey results (officer training gaps).
