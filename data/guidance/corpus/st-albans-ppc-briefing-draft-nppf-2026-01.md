## Summary

A two-page officer briefing note for St Albans' Planning Policy Committee (PPC). It mostly reproduces the Chief Planner's Newsletter of 16 December 2025 (list of key proposals, decision not to commence statutory NDMPs) and links to the WMS, consultation, press release and Secretary of State's letter. Its own "Headlines" say there are 3,096 changes between the 2024 NPPF and the draft, 225 questions, that it is the biggest change since 2012, and that it "will take a considerable period of time to comprehend them all". Despite St Albans being a Green Belt authority, it says nothing on settlements, washed-over villages, grey belt or the S4/S5 route.

## What it tells officers/decision-makers to do

- Nothing operational: members are told the scale of change and the consultation deadline (10 March 2026), and that the final Framework takes effect on publication.

## Positions against our propositions

- None. Silent on every proposition.

## Leads

- St Albans' actual consultation response to the draft (later PPC or Cabinet papers), especially on Q35 (settlement definition) and Green Belt villages.
- Chief Planner's Newsletter, December 2025.
