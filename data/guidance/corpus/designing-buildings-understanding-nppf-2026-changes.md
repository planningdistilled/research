## Summary

A Chartered Institute of Building (CIOB) policy article republished on the Designing Buildings wiki. It recalls the CIOB's response to the December 2025 consultation, quotes industry reaction (Savills, Landmark Chambers, BusinessLDN), and lists headline changes to the August 2026 National Planning Policy Framework (NPPF): "default yes" near stations in the top 80 Travel to Work Areas, the split between plan-making and decision-making policy, densification, the 1,500-home strategic site category, 40% M4(2) accessible homes, and HO13 build-out. It gives no guidance on applying the decision-making policies and is silent on settlements, S5, Green Belt routes, Annex A and appeals.

## What it tells officers/decision-makers to do

- Nothing concrete; it asks the government to let the 2026 Framework bed in before changing it again.
