# The New NPPF 2026 Has Arrived: What Does it Mean for Planning and Development?

## Summary

A same-day announcement from a Kent consultancy that the revised NPPF has been published. It notes the restructuring into policy codes, the split between plan-making and decision-making policies, new interpretive provisions, support for development near transport hubs and stations, and transitional arrangements for existing plans. It says areas without an up-to-date plan "are still able to contribute towards meeting development needs" and that the practical consequences are still being reviewed. It gives no concrete guidance on how any test should be applied and takes no position on our propositions.

## What it tells officers/decision-makers to do

- No concrete instructions; says local planning authorities (LPAs), developers and consultants are entering "a period of adjustment" while they interpret the Framework.
- Notes that further change is expected through National Development Management Policies (NDMPs), secondary legislation and updated Planning Practice Guidance (PPG).

## Positions against our propositions

- None. The source is silent on all our propositions.
