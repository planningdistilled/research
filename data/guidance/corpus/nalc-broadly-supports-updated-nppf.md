# NALC broadly supports the updated NPPF (NALC)

## Summary

A news item on NALC's consultation response to the draft National Planning Policy Framework (NPPF). NALC broadly welcomes the draft, accepts the need for more housebuilding, stresses long-term sustainable communities and strong support for neighbourhood planning. It warns that easing restrictions on the edge of the Green Belt and other green spaces must be matched by infrastructure (roads, schools, hospitals). It gives no application-level guidance, which leaves a gap for parish councils commenting on applications under the final Framework.

## What it tells officers/decision-makers to do

- Nothing directly; it is a sector policy statement about the draft.

## Positions against our propositions

- Silent on all propositions.
