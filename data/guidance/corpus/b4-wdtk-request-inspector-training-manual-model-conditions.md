## Summary

A Freedom of Information (FOI) request of 25 January 2024 for the most up-to-date Inspector Training Manual (ITM) and the Planning Inspectorate (PINS) "Suite of Suggested Planning Conditions – England". PINS released the ITM in three parts (watermarked 26 January 2024) plus the conditions suite. The release repeats that the Manual "does not constitute Government policy or guidance and does not seek to interpret Government policy" and that national policy is conclusive in any discrepancy. It gives no guidance on the August 2026 National Planning Policy Framework (NPPF). The live page returned 403; the local copy is the Internet Archive capture of 19 May 2025.

Note: the orchestrating task described this as an FOI release reported on 3 August 2026. The archived page shows a January 2024 release only; we could not see the live page to check for later correspondence.

## What it tells officers/decision-makers to do
