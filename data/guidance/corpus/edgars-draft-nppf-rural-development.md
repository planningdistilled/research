## Summary

A rural consultancy summary (Amy Powell) of the December 2025 consultation draft of the National Planning Policy Framework (NPPF). It describes the replacement of the "tilted balance" by a location-based presumption that is "switched on" by default, with S4 inside and S5 outside settlements. It lists the draft S5 categories, notes the presumption does not apply in the Green Belt or Local Green Space, and that isolated homes go to HO11. It stresses substantial weight for economic benefits (E2) and rural business support (E4), and that "existing building" means one existing at publication.

## What it tells officers/decision-makers to do

- Apply S4 inside and S5 outside settlements; apply Green Belt and Local Green Space policy instead of S5 there.
- Give substantial weight to economic benefits of rural business proposals.
- Treat buildings existing at the date of publication as "existing buildings" for reuse.

## Positions against our propositions

- S5 (agrees): "applying certainty to what forms of development are acceptable inside settlements (Policy S4) and outside settlements (Policy S5)" ("A Clearer – and Stronger – Presumption in Favour of Sustainable Development"). About the draft.
