## Summary

A property consultancy's summary (James Williams, Planning Associate) of the August 2026 Framework for developers and landowners. curl was blocked (HTTP 403), so this was read only through WebFetch and the text below is from that summary, not a saved copy. It says the Framework widens support for development outside settlement boundaries (grey belt and station routes), encourages higher density and infill within settlements, treats grey belt as "not inappropriate" where the tests (including unmet need and sustainable location) are met, and sets 35/45 dwellings per hectare (dph) minimums near well-connected stations. It also reports that plan policies materially inconsistent with the new national decision-making policies get very limited weight unless examined against the new Framework.
