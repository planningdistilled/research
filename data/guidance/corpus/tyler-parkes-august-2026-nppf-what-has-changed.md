## Summary

A short (three-minute) planning consultancy overview of the August 2026 National Planning Policy Framework (NPPF): the split between plan-making and decision-making policies, housing delivery, grey belt "now firmly incorporated", new station-area policy and densities, efficient use of land, and up-front viability. It gives no guidance on settlement definition, S4/S5 or washed-over villages. The claim in our search brief that it says washed-over Green Belt villages are excluded from the settlement definition is not in the retrieved text (the word "washed" does not occur); that claim appears in the LandTech blog instead.

## What it tells officers/decision-makers to do

- Nothing concrete for decision-makers; it tells landowners to have sites reassessed against the new policies.
- Notes that substantial weight is given to homes meeting evidenced need and that the Golden Rules still apply to major housing on Green Belt land.
