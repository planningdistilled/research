## Summary

A short Chief Officer report (dated 19 February 2026, to the Council Business Committee rather than Cabinet) seeking authority to submit Lancaster's consultation response as agreed by the Planning Policy Cabinet Advisory Group; Planning Regulatory Committee members were invited to that meeting. It tells members the draft gives "a more consistent presumption in favour of sustainable development within settlements, and outside settlements on sites that are well-located", separates plan-making from decision-making policies, and that the non-statutory national policies "will be a considerable material consideration that are capable of being directly applied to decisions and plans". It notes the draft is "notably more rules-based". The response itself (Appendix A) is not in this file.

## What it tells officers/decision-makers to do

- Treat national decision-making policies as directly applicable to decisions and as a considerable material consideration.
- Expect local plans not to repeat or be inconsistent with national policy.

## Positions against our propositions

- None coded. It describes the presumption and the direct application of national policy only at headline level and does not address settlements, tiers, Green Belt villages, walking routes, supply or Annex A weight.
