## Summary

The Listen Notes feed page for Planning magazine's weekly podcast. The episode pages for Ep219, Ep221 and Ep226 were also saved. Only show notes are available: Listen Notes has no transcript and the audio was not transcribed. Ep219 (20 August 2026, 26 minutes) is a first take on the updated National Planning Policy Framework (NPPF): station-area rules "relaxed in some ways, but tightened up in others", dropped tougher affordable housing proposals, more leeway on energy efficiency, and stronger protection for national parks and landscapes. Ep221 (28 August 2026, 39 minutes) is a guide to the rewritten NPPF: transitional arrangements, densification, "significant changes to the long-established presumption", heritage, Green Belt and plan-making. Ep222 covers "appeal-ready" applications, Ep225 the new heritage impact test, and Ep226 Casebook (an inspector citing a new NPPF policy one day after publication; how inspectors test the neighbourhood-plan disapplication of the presumption).
