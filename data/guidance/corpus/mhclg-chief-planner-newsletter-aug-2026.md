## Summary

The Chief Planner's official briefing to every Chief Planning Officer on the new National Planning Policy Framework (NPPF). It says the decision-making policies "took effect on 17 August 2026" and repeats the Minister's list of post-consultation changes (stations "default yes" in the top 80 Travel to Work Areas, density, strategic sites, local standards, retail parking, telecoms, community facilities, Protected Landscapes). Its only instruction on application is to "familiarise themselves with the new Framework, and in particular the decision-making policies". It contains nothing on Annex B settlements, the S4/S5 route, TR3 walking access, grey belt or land supply, and promises Planning Practice Guidance (PPG) updates "over the coming months". The rest covers statutory consultee reform, Housing Delivery Test (HDT) results, the AI tool Extract, an AI prototype for householder applications, Sustainable Drainage Systems (SuDS) templates and a SuDS e-learning course for planners.

## What it tells officers/decision-makers to do

- Treat the decision-making policies as in force from 17 August 2026.
- Use the 2025 HDT results for decision-making.
- "Familiarise themselves with the new Framework" (no structured training offered on it).
- Take the free Town and Country Planning Association (TCPA) / Environment Agency SuDS e-learning; use national SuDS Strategy Templates.
- Use MHCLG's Extract AI tool to digitise conservation areas, Article 4 Directions and Tree Preservation Orders.

## Positions against our propositions

- None. Silent on all propositions. The absence is itself evidence: the main official officer briefing on the 2026 Framework offers no interpretive guidance on settlements, S5 categories, TR3 or grey belt, leaving those to decisions (and so to appeal casework).

## Leads

- TCPA SuDS e-learning (https://learning.tcpa.org.uk/courses/intro-suds-for-planners): an example of nationally provided officer training, but on drainage, not the NPPF.
- MHCLG Digital blog on the householder-application AI prototype (i.AI, Google DeepMind, Faculty).
- Guidance on digital tools for site identification and assessment (nine LPA case studies).
