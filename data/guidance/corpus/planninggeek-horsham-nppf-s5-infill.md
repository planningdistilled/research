# NPPF S5 helps self-build home win Horsham countryside appeal

## Summary

Reports appeal 6009593 (Inspector Jane Smith, 10 Sep 2026) for a self-build house at Spring Gardens, Washington, Horsham, outside the defined built-up area and in conflict with the Horsham District Planning Framework spatial strategy (Policies 2, 4 and 26). The scheme qualified as limited infilling within a group of houses under S5(1)(e). The S5(1)(j) unmet-need route failed because the nearest defined settlements were too distant. The Inspector refused to import the (j) settlement-relationship requirement into (e). The stable and hardstanding did not make the whole plot previously developed land. Car dependence was given moderate weight, but the benefits were not substantially outweighed, and the NPPF justified approval despite the development-plan conflict.

## What it tells officers/decision-makers to do

- Identify the precise S5(1) limb relied on and apply its wording; do not merge tests from different limbs.
- For S5(1)(e) limited infilling, establish the group of houses, the shared road frontage, the gap filled and the relationship to neighbouring plots.
- Do not treat a whole plot as previously developed land because part of it carries a stable or hardstanding.
- After a limb is met, still weigh the harms (character, access to services and car reliance) against the benefits under the S5 'substantially outweighed' test.
- Remember the development plan stays the starting point; national policy can justify a departure.

## Positions against our propositions

- **S5 (S5 categories): agrees.** "The Inspector found no basis for importing the settlement-relationship requirement from another limb into the limited-infill provision." (section "Do not merge the separate S5 routes"). The S5(1) category decided the case, with each limb read on its own wording.
- **S5 (S5 categories): agrees.** "It is a reason to identify the actual route relied upon and apply its wording, rather than combining several different tests into one." (section "Do not merge the separate S5 routes"). Matches 'the S5(1) category is the whole case'.
- **S5 (S5 categories): silent (checked 2 Oct 2026; earlier coded 'qualifies').** "The Inspector did not find that route satisfied here: the nearest defined settlements were too distant." (section "Do not merge the separate S5 routes"). A one-line report of the Inspector's finding on S5(1)(j), not a position: it gives a single distance-based application of 'physically well-related' with no discussion of other readings, so it neither qualifies nor shows competing readings. At most one data point (6009593) on how (j) is applied; check the decision letter itself before citing it.
- **A2 (Annex A para 2): silent.** "The new national policy justified approval notwithstanding conflict with the development plan taken as a whole." (section "The planning balance still mattered"). The article never mentions Annex A, inconsistency between plan and Framework, or reduced weight for Policies 2, 4 and 26. It reports an ordinary s.38(6) departure where national policy (a met S5(1)(e) limb) was a material consideration outweighing the plan conflict. A2 is about how much weight a plan policy keeps, not whether it can be outweighed, so this neither qualifies nor contradicts A2. It is better read as support for S5 (the S5(1) limb carried the case).
