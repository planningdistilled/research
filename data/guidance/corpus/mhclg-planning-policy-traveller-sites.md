# Planning policy for traveller sites

## Summary
On 27 August 2026 the page was changed to say that "planning policy for traveller sites has been replaced by the NPPF". The new Framework now contains traveller-site policy for plan-making and decisions, with transitional rules in Annex A. The attached document still has its December 2024 cover, and its body is internally inconsistent: the new paragraph 1 says the policy has been replaced, but paragraphs 5-6 still describe a separate document that might be folded into the Framework later. The old text (Policy H on decisions, Policy E on the Green Belt, and paragraph 28 on temporary permission where there is no 5-year supply) remains in the PDF. We found no "reasonable walking distance" wording in it, contrary to the brief. It gives no guidance on applying the 2026 tests.

## What it tells officers/decision-makers to do
- Treat the NPPF (2026) as the source of traveller-site policy, and use Annex A for transitional provisions.
- Include traveller accommodation need in the Housing Delivery Test requirement (see the HDT rule book, footnote 8).

## Positions against our propositions
None. The document is superseded and says nothing on settlement tiers, walking routes, S5 categories, supply, grey belt, Annex A paragraph 2, council-inspector divergence or method.
