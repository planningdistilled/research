## Summary

This is an update notice from Planning Jungle, posted on 18 August 2026 by Steve Speed. Planning Jungle is a subscription knowledge service used by many council development-management officers. The notice gives no analysis. It links the August 2026 National Planning Policy Framework (NPPF), the December 2025 consultation, the government response, the "Creating a clear, rules-based planning system" policy paper and letters, and the station press release. It also links the earlier NPPF versions back to 2012. The rest of the site shows how the service works: it is built round curated, counted collections of appeal decisions (for example "GPDO Part 1 (All Classes) – LDC Appeal Decisions" with a running total of 2,101). That suggests officers who use it learn how to apply policy largely from appeal decisions.

## What it tells officers/decision-makers to do

- Read the August 2026 NPPF and the government's response to the consultation (links only).
- Note that this version replaces all earlier NPPFs (2012 to December 2024) and that the supporting Planning Practice Guidance (PPG) is on GOV.UK.
