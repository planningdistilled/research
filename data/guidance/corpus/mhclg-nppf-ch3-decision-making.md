## Summary

This is the official text of Chapter 3 of the August 2026 National Planning Policy Framework (NPPF), the general procedural policies for deciding planning applications (DM1 to DM10). It covers pre-application engagement and planning statements for major development (DM1), proportionate validation lists (DM2), how local planning authorities (LPAs) should approach a decision (DM3), weight to emerging plans and prematurity (DM4), viability (DM5), conditions and obligations including national model conditions (DM6), the boundary with other regulatory regimes (DM7), intentional unauthorised development (DM8), development orders (DM9) and Article 4 directions (DM10). It is procedural: it does not touch settlement status, the S4/S5 locational route, walking access, Green Belt or housing supply, so it is silent on all our substantive propositions.

## What it tells officers/decision-makers to do

- Work positively with applicants and seek solutions; take a proportionate approach scaled to size, complexity and impact (DM3(1)(a)-(b)).
- Consult statutory or internal consultees only where necessary, and do not delay decisions past statutory deadlines for advice, save for three listed exceptions (DM3(1)(d)).
- Consider whether conditions or obligations could make otherwise unacceptable proposals acceptable (DM3(1)(e)).
- "Not refuse applications for development which should clearly be approved", having regard to the development plan, the Framework and other material considerations (DM3(1)(f)).
- Weigh emerging plans by stage, unresolved objections and consistency with the Framework; refuse on prematurity only in narrow circumstances and explain the prejudice in the reason for refusal (DM4).
- Assume plan-compliant schemes are viable; accept site viability assessments only in four listed circumstances (DM5).
- Apply the six-test-style condition tests (now four: necessary, relevant, precise, reasonable), use national model conditions and obligations unless there are strong reasons not to, and avoid pre-commencement conditions without clear justification (DM6).
- Assume other regulatory regimes will operate effectively; do not duplicate them (DM7).
- Give substantial weight to evidence that unauthorised development was intentional (DM8(2)).

## Positions against our propositions

None. The chapter is procedural and silent on SH1, SH2, TR, S5, SUP, GB, A2, DIV and METH. DM3(1)(f) ("should clearly be approved") is a general instruction and does not speak to any council-versus-inspector divergence.

## Leads

- National model conditions and model obligations (DM6(3)-(4)): the government response says these will be developed with the Planning Inspectorate and published "before the end of the year" (2026). Worth tracking as a source of officer practice.
- Annex C (national information requirements summary) referenced by DM2.
- Viability planning practice guidance (PPG) and its standardised inputs, referenced by DM5(4).
