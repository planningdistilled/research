# NPPF timeline: every Framework from 2012 to 2026

## Summary

Reference page listing every NPPF edition from March 2012 to August 2026, with archived PDFs. It explains that the August 2026 Framework applies to decisions from 17 August 2026, whenever the application was submitted, because national policy is a material consideration at the date of the decision. Plan-making follows separate Annex A transitional rules. It warns against reading old appeal decisions or paragraph numbers as if they applied the current wording.

## What it tells officers/decision-makers to do

- Decide applications and appeals now against the August 2026 NPPF, whenever they were submitted.
- Read an old decision against the Framework current at its date; check current wording before relying on it.
- Check Annex A transitional rules for plan-making separately.

## Positions against our propositions

- **METH (our method): agrees.** "National policy is a material consideration at the date of the decision, so an application submitted under an earlier Framework does not normally freeze the national policy position." (section "Which NPPF applies now?"). Supports including in the corpus all decisions dated from 17 Aug 2026 as applications of the 2026 Framework, whenever submitted.
- **METH (our method): agrees (weak, by implication).** "When an old appeal decision quotes a superseded paragraph, it may still be useful for a broader planning principle, but the current wording and policy context should be checked before relying on it in a new application or appeal." (section "A note on archived national planning policy"). A general practitioner caution about citing pre-August-2026 decisions, not a critique of counting or coding appeal outcomes. Our method already uses only decisions made under the August 2026 Framework, so the caution supports that restriction rather than qualifying it. No change needed; the one implication is that the coded dataset will itself need re-checking if the Framework wording changes again.
