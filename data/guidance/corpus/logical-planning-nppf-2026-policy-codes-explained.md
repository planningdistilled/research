## Summary

A short lookup guide to the lettered policy codes of the August 2026 National Planning Policy Framework: chapter prefixes (PM, DM, S, CC, F, W, HO, L, DP, E, TC, CO, M, GB, N, HE, TR, HC, P), the codes most cited in land appraisals (S3 to S5, GB2/GB3/GB6/GB7/GB8, L2/L3, TR3, HE5/HE6 and others), and Annexes A to F. It advises citing code, version and year, citing the annex where the operative detail sits, and saying explicitly when a decision relied on the December 2024 Framework. It says there is no one-to-one paragraph conversion table. It gives no guidance on how the policies should be applied.

## What it tells officers/decision-makers to do

- Cite as "NPPF (August 2026), policy GB7(1)(g)", plus the relevant annex.
- Say explicitly when a decision or appraisal relies on the December 2024 Framework rather than silently renumbering.
- Read each policy in full rather than treating it as an old paragraph under a new label.

## Positions against our propositions

None (silent on every proposition). Accuracy note: it lists "HO3 — housing land supply and the Housing Delivery Test"; in the Framework HO3 is "Providing land for homes" (a plan-making policy), with the supply and HDT mechanics in Annex D.
