## Summary

Launch-day news coverage of the August 2026 National Planning Policy Framework (NPPF), led by the "default yes" for homes near well-connected stations. It records the 40% accessible-homes minimum on major sites, reduced blanket statutory consultation, a new "medium" development category and pub and music-venue protection. Industry comment (Rapleys, Shakespeare Martineau, Chartered Institute of Housing, Michelmores) warns that applications are still determined against the wider development plan, that "consentable" is not "deliverable", and that newly elected non-Labour councils may resist. It gives no detailed guidance on applying the policies.

## What it tells officers/decision-makers to do

- Continue to determine station-area applications against the wider development plan and other material considerations; the default yes does not replace them.
- Local planning teams need resources to turn policy into homes.

## Positions against our propositions

- **A2 (agrees):** "adding that applications would still be determined against the wider development plan and other relevant considerations." (Angus Irvine, Rapleys). Supports our reading that plan policies (beyond any inconsistent clause) still bite under the new Framework.
