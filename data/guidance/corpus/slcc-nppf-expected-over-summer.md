# NPPF expected to be published over the summer (SLCC)

## Summary

A short notice to clerks that the Housing and Planning Minister, Matthew Pennycook MP, told a conference (UKREiiF 2026) that the final National Planning Policy Framework (NPPF) would be published "this summer". It also notes the planned National Scheme of Delegation, under which planning committees will consider only the most significant applications. It gives no guidance on applying the Framework.

## What it tells officers/decision-makers to do

- Nothing; it is a notice.

## Positions against our propositions

- Silent on all propositions.

## Leads

- Minister's speech: https://www.gov.uk/government/speeches/housing-and-planning-minister-speech-to-ukreiif-2026
- National Scheme of Delegation (affects which applications reach committee, and so what members are briefed on)
