## Summary

A Freedom of Information (FOI) request made on 7 February 2019 for the latest Planning Inspectorate (PINS) Inspector Training Manual (ITM). PINS released a consolidated ITM in three parts on 21 February 2019. The release says the Manual is regularly updated, is not Government policy or guidance and does not seek to interpret it, and that national policy wins in any discrepancy. It also says the ITM had "not yet been fully updated" for the then-revised National Planning Policy Framework (NPPF, July 2018). It says nothing about the August 2026 Framework. The live page returned 403 to direct fetches; the local copy is the Internet Archive capture of 9 October 2025.

Note: the orchestrating task described a response dated 3 August 2026. The archived page shows only the 2019 correspondence.

## What it tells officers/decision-makers to do

- Nothing directly. It shows that inspectors work from an internal training manual that is updated after Framework changes, with a lag (in 2019, the ITM was not yet fully updated some months after the 2018 Framework).

## Positions against our propositions

None. The page is silent on all our propositions.

## Leads

- Precedent for the lag: "The Inspector Training Manual has not yet been fully updated in response to the revised National Planning Policy Framework." A post-August-2026 ITM may likewise be part-updated; ask PINS for the chapters on the presumption (S3 to S6), Annex B settlements and Green Belt / grey belt (GB6 to GB8) with version dates.
