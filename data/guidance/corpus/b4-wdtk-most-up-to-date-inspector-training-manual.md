## Summary

A Freedom of Information (FOI) request of 27 April 2025 for the most up-to-date Inspector Training Manual (ITM) and the suggested-conditions suite. The Planning Inspectorate (PINS) replied on 29 April 2025 with "CONS ITM Part1/2/3.pdf", described as "up to date as of this week", and "PINS suite of suggested planning conditions August 24.pdf". It repeats the standard caveat that the Manual is not Government policy and does not interpret it. It predates the August 2026 National Planning Policy Framework (NPPF) and gives no guidance on it. The live page returned 403; the local copy is the Internet Archive capture of 13 July 2025.

## What it tells officers/decision-makers to do

- Nothing directly. It confirms PINS will release the current ITM within days on request.

## Positions against our propositions

None. The page is silent on all our propositions.

## Leads

- PINS answered within two days. A request now for the post-August-2026 ITM chapters (presumption S3 to S6, Annex B settlement, TR3, GB6 to GB8 grey belt) is practical and quick.
