## Summary

Written before the final Framework, this piece covers transport-led regeneration in the Northern city regions. It brings together the Planning and Infrastructure Act 2025 (compulsory purchase (CPO) reforms, the Hope Value Direction, spatial development strategies), Greater Manchester's Places for Everyone plan, Platform4 and the draft "NPPF 2.0". On the draft Framework it highlights the vision-led transport policy (TR1), which replaces "predict and provide", and the proposed presumption for housing within reasonable walking distance of a station, "including for development outside of settlement boundaries". It also says grey belt land next to transport nodes should be mapped urgently. It gives no guidance on how to apply the walking-distance, settlement or Green Belt tests.

## What it tells officers/decision-makers to do

- Map sites against plan allocations, grey belt land and transport investment pipelines.
- Expect density around transport nodes to become a policy expectation, with under-dense schemes open to challenge.
- Engage early with emerging spatial development strategies; review CPO and option agreements.

## Positions against our propositions

None. It describes the draft station presumption and grey belt in promotional terms, without reading the walking-route, settlement or Green Belt tests.
