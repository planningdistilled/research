## Summary
Not about the 2026 Framework. This is Philip Allin's (Director, Boyer) commentary of 2 August 2024 on the July 2024 consultation draft that became the December 2024 National Planning Policy Framework (NPPF). It covers the then-new grey belt definition, predicting "more debates at appeal" on whether land strongly performs against Green Belt purposes, and the importance of robust local Green Belt reviews. It also covers the transitional arrangements for Regulation 19 plans and the new standard method, using Three Rivers as an example. It is background only for the grey belt concept now carried into GB6-GB8.

## What it tells officers/decision-makers to do
- (2024 draft) Make sure Green Belt reviews are "fit for purpose" and properly scrutinised, as they will underpin decisions on individual grey belt proposals.
- (2024 draft) Expect appeal disputes over whether a site "strongly" performs against a Green Belt purpose.

## Positions against our propositions
- None for the August 2026 Framework. The grey belt remarks concern the 2024 draft definition, which has since been replaced.

## Leads
- None new for the 2026 Framework.
