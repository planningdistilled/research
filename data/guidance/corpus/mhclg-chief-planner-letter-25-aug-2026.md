## Summary

Chief Planner letter announcing two consultations: planning powers for mayors outside London (call-in or directed refusal of applications of potential strategic importance, Mayoral Development Orders, Mayoral Community Infrastructure Levy, Local Development Order changes; closes 5 October 2026) and standard section 106 planning agreements for medium-sized housing sites (closes 20 October 2026). It restates the new National Planning Policy Framework (NPPF) definition of medium development as "between 10 and 49 homes, on a site of up to 2.5 hectares" and the NPPF rule that national model planning obligations "should be used unless there are strong reasons for taking a different approach". The draft templates were prepared with Town Legal LLP and the Planning Advisory Service (PAS). It gives no guidance on the location policies (S3 to S5), TR3 or Green Belt.

## What it tells officers/decision-makers to do

- Respond to both consultations.
- Expect standard bilateral section 106 and unilateral undertaking templates, and affordable housing schedules (with a discretionary cascade), to become the default for medium sites.
- Keep the NPPF preference for on-site affordable housing.

## Positions against our propositions

- None. Silent on all propositions.
