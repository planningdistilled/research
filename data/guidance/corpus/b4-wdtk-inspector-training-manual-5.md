## Summary

A Freedom of Information (FOI) request of 4 February 2025 for the up-to-date Inspector Training Manual (ITM), asked to take account of the December 2024 National Planning Policy Framework (NPPF), plus the model conditions. The Planning Inspectorate (PINS) replied on 11 February 2025 with "Consolidated Inspector Training Manual on 10 February 2025" (three parts) and "PINS suite of suggested planning conditions on 10 February 2025". So PINS issued a consolidated ITM about eight weeks after the December 2024 Framework. Nothing here covers the August 2026 Framework. The live page returned 403; the local copy is the Internet Archive capture of 13 July 2025, which does not show the later October 2025 update mentioned in the task brief.

## What it tells officers/decision-makers to do

- Nothing directly. It shows the cadence: a consolidated inspector manual followed a new Framework within about two months.
