# Archaeological interest

**Annex B definition** ([NPPF Annex B, p. 103](../../../nppf/NPPF-August-2026.md#AnnexB-archaeological-interest)): Archaeological interest: There will be archaeological interest in a heritage asset if it holds, or potentially holds, evidence of past human activity worthy of expert investigation at some point.
