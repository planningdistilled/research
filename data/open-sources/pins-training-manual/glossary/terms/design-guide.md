# Design guide

**Annex B definition** ([NPPF Annex B, p. 105](../../../nppf/NPPF-August-2026.md#AnnexB-design-guide)): Design guide: A document providing guidance on how development can be carried out in accordance with good design practice, often produced by a local authority.
