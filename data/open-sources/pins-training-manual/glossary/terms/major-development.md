# Major development

**Annex B definition** ([NPPF Annex B, p. 108](../../../nppf/NPPF-August-2026.md#AnnexB-major-development)): Major development[^71]: For housing, development where 10 or more homes will be provided, or the site has an area of 0.5 hectares or more. For non-residential development it means additional floorspace of 1,000m2 or more, or a site of 1 hectare or more, or as otherwise provided in the Town and Country Planning (Development Management Procedure) (England) Order 2015.
