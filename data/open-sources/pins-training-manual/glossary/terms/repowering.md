# Repowering

**Annex B definition** ([NPPF Annex B, p. 112](../../../nppf/NPPF-August-2026.md#AnnexB-repowering)): Repowering: the decommissioning and removal of existing renewable and low carbon energy infrastructure on-site, and its replacement with newer, larger or more efficient infrastructure. The repowered site may have a different overall capacity, layout and footprint from the original project. Repowering may require new associated infrastructure such as new network connection infrastructure and foundations.
