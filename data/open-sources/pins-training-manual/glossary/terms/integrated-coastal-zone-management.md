# Integrated Coastal Zone Management

**Annex B definition** ([NPPF Annex B, p. 107](../../../nppf/NPPF-August-2026.md#AnnexB-integrated-coastal-zone-management)): Integrated Coastal Zone Management: A holistic approach towards the planning and management of coastal areas (both land and marine), bringing together key interests to identify suitable approaches to long-term change and adaptation.
