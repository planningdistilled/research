# Masterplan

**Annex B definition** ([NPPF Annex B, p. 108](../../../nppf/NPPF-August-2026.md#AnnexB-masterplan)): Masterplan: A masterplan is a flexible, placemaking tool, providing a three-dimensional spatial framework and long-term vision for the development or regeneration of area-specific or site-specific locations.
