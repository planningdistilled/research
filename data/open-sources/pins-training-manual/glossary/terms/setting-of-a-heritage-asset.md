# Setting of a heritage asset

**Annex B definition** ([NPPF Annex B, p. 112](../../../nppf/NPPF-August-2026.md#AnnexB-setting-of-a-heritage-asset)): Setting of a heritage asset: The surroundings in which a heritage asset is experienced. Its extent is not fixed and may change as the asset and its surroundings evolve. Elements of a setting may make a positive or negative contribution to the significance of an asset, may affect the ability to appreciate that significance or may be neutral.
