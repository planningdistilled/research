# National Landscapes

**Annex B definition** ([NPPF Annex B, p. 109](../../../nppf/NPPF-August-2026.md#AnnexB-national-landscapes)): National Landscapes: Areas legally designated as areas of outstanding natural beauty under the National Parks and Access to the Countryside Act 1949 and Countryside and Rights of Way Act 2000.
