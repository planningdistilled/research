# Geodiversity

**Annex B definition** ([NPPF Annex B, p. 106](../../../nppf/NPPF-August-2026.md#AnnexB-geodiversity)): Geodiversity: The range of rocks, minerals, fossils, soils and landforms, together with the natural processes which form and alter them.
