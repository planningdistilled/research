# Well-connected station

**Annex B definition** ([NPPF Annex B, p. 115](../../../nppf/NPPF-August-2026.md#AnnexB-well-connected-station)): Well-connected station: Railway stations and underground, tram and light rail stops located within a top 80 Travel to Work Area located partially or fully within England by Gross Value Added (GVA)[^72] and which, in the normal weekday timetable, are served (or have a reasonable prospect of being served due to planned upgrades or through agreement with the rail operator) throughout the daytime by at least four trains or trams per hour overall, or at least two trains or trams per hour in any one direction.
