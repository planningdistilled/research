# Mansard roof

**Annex B definition** ([NPPF Annex B, p. 108](../../../nppf/NPPF-August-2026.md#AnnexB-mansard-roof)): Mansard roof: A type of roof that is characterised by two slopes, the lower steep and the upper shallow. It is generally regarded as a suitable type of roof extension for buildings which are part of a terrace of at least three buildings and at least two storeys tall, with a parapet running the entire length of the front façade.
