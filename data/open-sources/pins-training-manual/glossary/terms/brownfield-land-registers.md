# Brownfield land registers

**Annex B definition** ([NPPF Annex B, p. 103](../../../nppf/NPPF-August-2026.md#AnnexB-brownfield-land-registers)): Brownfield land registers: Registers of previously developed land that local planning authorities consider to be appropriate for residential development, having regard to criteria in the Town and Country Planning (Brownfield Land Registers) Regulations 2017. Local planning authorities will be able to trigger a grant of permission in principle for residential development on suitable sites in their registers where they follow the required procedures.
