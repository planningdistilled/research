# Climate change mitigation

**Annex B definition** ([NPPF Annex B, p. 103](../../../nppf/NPPF-August-2026.md#AnnexB-climate-change-mitigation)): Climate change mitigation: Action to reduce the impact of human activity on the climate system, primarily through reducing greenhouse gas emissions.
