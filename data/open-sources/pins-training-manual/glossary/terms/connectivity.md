# Connectivity

**Annex B definition** ([NPPF Annex B, p. 104](../../../nppf/NPPF-August-2026.md#AnnexB-connectivity)): Connectivity: The degree to which a location provides access to jobs, services, and facilities by sustainable transport modes. The Connectivity Tool (Connectivity Tool - GOV.UK) provides a means of assessing this.
