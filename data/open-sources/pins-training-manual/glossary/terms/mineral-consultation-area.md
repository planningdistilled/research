# Mineral Consultation Area

**Annex B definition** ([NPPF Annex B, p. 109](../../../nppf/NPPF-August-2026.md#AnnexB-mineral-consultation-area)): Mineral Consultation Area: A geographical area based on a Mineral Safeguarding Area, where the district or borough council should consult the mineral planning authority for any proposals for non-minerals development.
