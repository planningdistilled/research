# Pitches and Plots

**Annex B definition** ([NPPF Annex B, p. 110](../../../nppf/NPPF-August-2026.md#AnnexB-pitches-and-plots)): Pitches and Plots: ‘Pitch’ means a pitch on a gypsy and traveller site and ‘plot’ means a pitch on a travelling showpeople site (often called a ‘yard’). This terminology differentiates between residential pitches for gypsies and travellers and mixed-use plots for travelling showpeople, which may / will need to incorporate space or to be split to allow for the storage of equipment.
