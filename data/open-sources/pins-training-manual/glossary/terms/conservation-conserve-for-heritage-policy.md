# Conservation/conserve (for heritage policy)

**Annex B definition** ([NPPF Annex B, p. 104](../../../nppf/NPPF-August-2026.md#AnnexB-conservation-conserve-for-heritage-policy)): Conservation/conserve (for heritage policy): The process of maintaining and managing change to a heritage asset in a way that sustains, preserves and, where appropriate, enhances its significance.
