# Post-16

**Annex B definition** ([NPPF Annex B, p. 111](../../../nppf/NPPF-August-2026.md#AnnexB-post-16)): Post-16: Young people who are over compulsory school age but under 19, or aged 19 or over and for whom an Education, Health and Care (EHC) plan is maintained.
