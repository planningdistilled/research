# Gypsies and travellers

**Annex B definition** ([NPPF Annex B, p. 106](../../../nppf/NPPF-August-2026.md#AnnexB-gypsies-and-travellers)): Gypsies and travellers: Persons of nomadic habit of life whatever their race or origin, including such persons who on grounds only of their own or their family’s or dependants’ educational or health needs or old age have ceased to travel temporarily or permanently, and all other persons with a cultural tradition of nomadism or of living in a caravan, but excluding members of an organised group of travelling showpeople or circus people travelling together as such.
