# Habitats site

**Annex B definition** ([NPPF Annex B, p. 106](../../../nppf/NPPF-August-2026.md#AnnexB-habitats-site)): Habitats site: Any site which would be included within the definition at regulation 8 of the Conservation of Habitats and Species Regulations 2017 and also including candidate Special Areas of Conservation or Special Protection Areas, listed or proposed Ramsar sites, relevant Marine Sites, and compensatory habitats which relate to these sites[^70].
