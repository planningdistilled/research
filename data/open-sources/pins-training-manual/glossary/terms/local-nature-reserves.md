# Local Nature Reserves

**Annex B definition** ([NPPF Annex B, p. 107](../../../nppf/NPPF-August-2026.md#AnnexB-local-nature-reserves)): Local Nature Reserves: A site managed as a nature reserve under s.21 of the National Parks and Access to the Countryside Act 1949 by principal local authorities.
