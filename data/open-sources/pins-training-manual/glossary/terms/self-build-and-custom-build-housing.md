# Self-build and custom-build housing

**Annex B definition** ([NPPF Annex B, p. 112](../../../nppf/NPPF-August-2026.md#AnnexB-self-build-and-custom-build-housing)): Self-build and custom-build housing: Housing built by an individual, a group of individuals, or persons working with or for them, to be occupied by that individual. Such housing can be either market or affordable housing. A legal definition, for the purpose of applying the Self-build and Custom Housebuilding Act 2015 (as amended), is contained in section 1(A1) and (A2) of that Act.
