# Sustainable drainage system

**Annex B definition** ([NPPF Annex B, p. 114](../../../nppf/NPPF-August-2026.md#AnnexB-sustainable-drainage-system)): Sustainable drainage system: A sustainable drainage system controls surface water run off close to where it falls, combining a mixture of built and nature-based techniques to mimic natural drainage as closely as possible, and accounting for the predicted impacts of climate change. The type of system that would be appropriate will vary from small scale interventions such as permeable paving and soakaways that can be used in very small developments to larger integrated schemes in major developments.
