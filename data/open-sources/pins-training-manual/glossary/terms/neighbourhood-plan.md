# Neighbourhood plan

**Annex B definition** ([NPPF Annex B, p. 109](../../../nppf/NPPF-August-2026.md#AnnexB-neighbourhood-plan)): Neighbourhood plan: A plan prepared by a parish council or neighbourhood forum for a designated neighbourhood area. In law this is described as a neighbourhood development plan in the Planning and Compulsory Purchase Act 2004.
