# Planning condition

**Annex B definition** ([NPPF Annex B, p. 110](../../../nppf/NPPF-August-2026.md#AnnexB-planning-condition)): Planning condition: A condition imposed on a grant of planning permission (in accordance with the Town and Country Planning Act 1990) or a condition included in a Local Development Order, Mayoral Development Order or Neighbourhood Development Order.
