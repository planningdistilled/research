# Freight and logistics

**Annex B definition** ([NPPF Annex B, p. 106](../../../nppf/NPPF-August-2026.md#AnnexB-freight-and-logistics)): Freight and logistics: Uses directly associated with the processing, storage, distribution and transportation of goods, alongside supporting infrastructure. This includes (but is not limited to) storage and distribution operations, rail freight terminals, intermodal facilities, HGV operating centres, lorry parking facilities, ports and inland border facilities.
