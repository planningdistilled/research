# Historic environment

**Annex B definition** ([NPPF Annex B, p. 107](../../../nppf/NPPF-August-2026.md#AnnexB-historic-environment)): Historic environment: All aspects of the environment resulting from the interaction between people and places through time, including all surviving physical remains of past human activity, whether visible, buried or submerged, and landscaped and planted or managed flora.
