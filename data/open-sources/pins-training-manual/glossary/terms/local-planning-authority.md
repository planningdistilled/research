# Local planning authority

**Annex B definition** ([NPPF Annex B, p. 108](../../../nppf/NPPF-August-2026.md#AnnexB-local-planning-authority)): Local planning authority: The public authority whose duty it is to carry out specific planning functions for a particular area. All references to local planning authority include the district council, London borough council, county council, Broads Authority, National Park Authority, the Mayor of London and a development corporation, to the extent appropriate to their responsibilities.
