# Supplementary plans

**Annex B definition** ([NPPF Annex B, p. 114](../../../nppf/NPPF-August-2026.md#AnnexB-supplementary-plans)): Supplementary plans: A type of plan that relevant plan-making authorities can choose to prepare to set locally-specific design standards or shape and direct site-specific development. They must undergo consultation and independent examination.
