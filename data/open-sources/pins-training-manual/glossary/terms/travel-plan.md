# Travel plan

**Annex B definition** ([NPPF Annex B, p. 114](../../../nppf/NPPF-August-2026.md#AnnexB-travel-plan)): Travel plan: A long-term management strategy for an organisation or site that details how agreed sustainable transport objectives are to be delivered, and which is monitored and regularly reviewed.
