# Community Right to Build Order

**Annex B definition** ([NPPF Annex B, p. 104](../../../nppf/NPPF-August-2026.md#AnnexB-community-right-to-build-order)): Community Right to Build Order: An Order made by the local planning authority (under the Town and Country Planning Act 1990) that grants planning permission for a site-specific development proposal or classes of development.
