# Site of Special Scientific Interest

**Annex B definition** ([NPPF Annex B, p. 113](../../../nppf/NPPF-August-2026.md#AnnexB-site-of-special-scientific-interest)): Site of Special Scientific Interest: Sites notified for their flora, fauna or geological, physiographical or geomorphological features (in England, these sites are notified under s. 28 of the Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981).
