# Minerals and waste plans

**Annex B definition** ([NPPF Annex B, p. 109](../../../nppf/NPPF-August-2026.md#AnnexB-minerals-and-waste-plans)): Minerals and waste plans: A plan containing policies on mineral and waste development, drawn up by the minerals and waste planning authority in consultation with the community. Minerals and waste matters can be dealt with in separate plans or combined with local plans where an authority is responsible for all these matters.
