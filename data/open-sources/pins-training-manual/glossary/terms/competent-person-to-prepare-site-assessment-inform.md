# Competent person (to prepare site assessment information)

**Annex B definition** ([NPPF Annex B, p. 104](../../../nppf/NPPF-August-2026.md#AnnexB-competent-person-to-prepare-site-assessment-information)): Competent person (to prepare site assessment information): A person with a recognised relevant qualification, sufficient experience in dealing with the type(s) of pollution or land instability, and membership of a relevant professional organisation.
