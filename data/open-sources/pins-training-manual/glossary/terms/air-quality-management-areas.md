# Air Quality Management Areas

**Annex B definition** ([NPPF Annex B, p. 102](../../../nppf/NPPF-August-2026.md#AnnexB-air-quality-management-areas)): Air Quality Management Areas: Areas designated by local authorities because they are not likely to achieve national air quality objectives by the relevant deadlines.
