# Heritage coast

**Annex B definition** ([NPPF Annex B, p. 107](../../../nppf/NPPF-August-2026.md#AnnexB-heritage-coast)): Heritage coast: Areas of undeveloped coastline which are managed to conserve their natural beauty and, where appropriate, to improve accessibility for visitors.
