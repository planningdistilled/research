# Specialist community-based accommodation

**Annex B definition** ([NPPF Annex B, p. 113](../../../nppf/NPPF-August-2026.md#AnnexB-specialist-community-based-accommodation)): Specialist community-based accommodation: Includes, but is not limited to, accommodation for looked after children, supported accommodation for care leavers, and supervised, non-custodial housing for criminal justice purposes that provide structured support for individuals charged with or convicted of an offence, transitioning from custody, or serving community-based sentences.
