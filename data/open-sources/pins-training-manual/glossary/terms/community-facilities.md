# Community facilities

**Annex B definition** ([NPPF Annex B, p. 103](../../../nppf/NPPF-August-2026.md#AnnexB-community-facilities)): Community facilities: Meeting places, public houses, places of worship, cultural venues, youth centres, allotments, cemeteries and burial grounds, and facilities for play, sport and informal recreation.
