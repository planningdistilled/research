# Older people

**Annex B definition** ([NPPF Annex B, p. 110](../../../nppf/NPPF-August-2026.md#AnnexB-older-people)): Older people: People over or approaching retirement age, including the active, newly-retired through to the very frail elderly; and whose housing needs can encompass accessible, adaptable general needs housing through to the full range of retirement and specialised housing for those with support or care needs.
