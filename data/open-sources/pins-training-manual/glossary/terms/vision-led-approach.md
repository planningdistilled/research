# Vision-led approach

**Annex B definition** ([NPPF Annex B, p. 115](../../../nppf/NPPF-August-2026.md#AnnexB-vision-led-approach)): Vision-led approach: An approach to transport planning which sets outcomes for a development based on achieving well-designed, sustainable, inclusive and popular places, and provides the transport solutions to deliver those outcomes as opposed to predicting future demand to provide capacity (often referred to as ‘predict and provide’).
