# Significance (for heritage policy)

**Annex B definition** ([NPPF Annex B, p. 113](../../../nppf/NPPF-August-2026.md#AnnexB-significance-for-heritage-policy)): Significance (for heritage policy): The value of a heritage asset to this and future generations because of its heritage interest. The interest may be archaeological, architectural, artistic or historic. Significance derives not only from a heritage asset’s physical presence, but also from its setting. For World Heritage Sites, the cultural value described within each site’s Statement of Outstanding Universal Value forms part of its significance.
