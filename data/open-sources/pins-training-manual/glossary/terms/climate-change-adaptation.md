# Climate change adaptation

**Annex B definition** ([NPPF Annex B, p. 103](../../../nppf/NPPF-August-2026.md#AnnexB-climate-change-adaptation)): Climate change adaptation: Adjustments made to natural or human systems in response to the actual or anticipated impacts of climate change, to mitigate harm or exploit beneficial opportunities.
