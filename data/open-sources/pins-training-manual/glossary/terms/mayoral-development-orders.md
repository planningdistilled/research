# Mayoral Development Orders

**Annex B definition** ([NPPF Annex B, p. 108](../../../nppf/NPPF-August-2026.md#AnnexB-mayoral-development-orders)): Mayoral Development Orders: An Order made by a mayor (under the Town and Country Planning Act 1990) that grants planning permission for a specific development proposal.
