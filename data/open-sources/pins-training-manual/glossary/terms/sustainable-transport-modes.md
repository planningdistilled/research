# Sustainable transport modes

**Annex B definition** ([NPPF Annex B, p. 114](../../../nppf/NPPF-August-2026.md#AnnexB-sustainable-transport-modes)): Sustainable transport modes: Any efficient, safe and accessible means of transport with overall low impact on the environment, including walking, wheeling, cycling and public transport.
