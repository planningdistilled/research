# Contamination

**Annex B definition** ([NPPF Annex B, p. 104](../../../nppf/NPPF-August-2026.md#AnnexB-contamination)): Contamination: Where the actual or suspected presence of substances in, on or under the land may cause risks to people, property, human activities or the environment, regardless of whether or not the land meets the statutory definition of contaminated land in Part IIA of the Environmental Protection Act 1990.
