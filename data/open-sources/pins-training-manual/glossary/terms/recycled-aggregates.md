# Recycled aggregates

**Annex B definition** ([NPPF Annex B, p. 112](../../../nppf/NPPF-August-2026.md#AnnexB-recycled-aggregates)): Recycled aggregates: Aggregates resulting from the processing of inorganic materials previously used in construction, e.g. construction and demolition waste.
