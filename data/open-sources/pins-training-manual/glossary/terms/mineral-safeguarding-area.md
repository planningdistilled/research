# Mineral Safeguarding Area

**Annex B definition** ([NPPF Annex B, p. 109](../../../nppf/NPPF-August-2026.md#AnnexB-mineral-safeguarding-area)): Mineral Safeguarding Area: An area designated by minerals planning authorities which covers known deposits of minerals which are desired to be kept safeguarded from unnecessary sterilisation by non-mineral development.
