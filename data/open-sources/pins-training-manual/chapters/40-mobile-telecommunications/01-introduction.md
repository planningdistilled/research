# 40 Mobile Telecommunications: Introduction

<!-- Part3 p.348 -->

## Introduction

1. Inspectors make their decisions on the basis of the evidence before them. Consequently, they may, where justified by the evidence, depart from the advice given in this training material, although the National Planning Policy Framework and the Government’s Planning Practice Guidance will still be relevant in all cases.

2. Most proposals for mobile communications installations relate to the mobile phone network and take the form of lattice towers, poles or antennas fixed to buildings. There can, however, also be fixed line broadband cabinets, telegraph poles and underground cables and cabinets.

3. This training material applies to casework in England only.
