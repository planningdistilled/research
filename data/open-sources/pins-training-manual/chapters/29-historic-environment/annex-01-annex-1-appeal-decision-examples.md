## Annex 1 – Appeal Decision Examples

The following decisions were all written after the 2019 revision of the NPPF (but before the July 2021 revised NPPF, so references to NPPF paragraph numbers may be misleading), and therefore may not reflect the most recent policy and changes in case law. Most of the following hyperlinks will not work unless you change your default browser to Internet Explorer or have this browser open and paste the link into the address bar. This is a known and long-standing IT fault. Whilst the style and length inevitably vary, each decision applies the 3-step approach:
