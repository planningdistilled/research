# 37c Local Plan Examinations (NPPF 2024): Role of the Inspector in Examination: ANNEXES

<!-- Part2 p.914 -->

## ANNEXES

Each annex comprises a Sharepoint folder that includes examples of the different types of examination documents (which may go by different names in different examinations) that are referred to in the Role of the Inspector in the Examination Process section of the ITM Local Plan Examinations chapter.

There are two or more examples of each of the types of documents. They are examples, and should not be seen as models to be followed exactly. Instead, please see them as illustrations of the ways in which different Inspectors have produced material for each of the various stages of the examination process.

Please note that some of the examples were produced in the context of NPPF December 2023 rather than NPPF December 2024.

Many other examples of examination documents are available online, on the websites of current and recent local plan examinations. You may wish to look at some of those as alternative examples to the ones provided here.
