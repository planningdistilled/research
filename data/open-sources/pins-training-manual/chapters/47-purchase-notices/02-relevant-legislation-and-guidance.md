# 47 Purchase Notices: Relevant Legislation and Guidance

<!-- Part3 p.835 -->

## Relevant Legislation and Guidance

Town and Country Planning Act 1990 (TCPA) - Sections 137 - 144

Planning (Listed Buildings and Conservation Areas) Act 1990 (LBCA) - Sections 32 to 36

The Town and Country Planning General Regulations 1992

Purchase Notices (MHCLG 2024 Guidance) – this MHCLG Guidance replaces the previous 2019 guidance on the compulsory purchase process and the Crichel Down Rules

Compulsory Purchase Guidance – MHCLG Guidance Collection

Encyclopaedia of Planning Law (pages 2-3503 to 2-3514) The commentary to sections 137 and 32 provides useful background detail and reference to relevant judgments)
