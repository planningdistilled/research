# 24 Green Belts: Removal of existing buildings

<!-- Part2 p.105 -->

## Removal of existing buildings

118. If it is argued that the demolition of existing buildings would either increase openness or would balance any loss of openness caused by the proposal, then it will generally be preferable to consider the overall consequences for openness in Step 1. Should it be concluded that the proposal would bring about a positive outcome in terms of openness, this should also be weighed in the balance within Steps 3-5. Whether the removal of existing buildings would lead to a visual improvement is a separate matter to openness and is therefore best covered as an ‘other consideration’.

119. If the demolition of existing buildings is necessary to enable permission to be granted, a condition must be imposed that requires the buildings to be demolished within a reasonable time frame. You may also need to consider whether a building could be erected in any event under permitted development rights and the likelihood of this occurring.
