# 24 Green Belts: Golden Rules – Policy GB8

<!-- Part2 p.104 -->

## Golden Rules – Policy GB8

111. The Golden Rules apply to all “major development” (as defined in the NPPF) involving the provision of housing on sites in the Green Belt. This includes all development, not just those meeting the exceptions in Policy GB7(1)(g) or (h). It will be a matter to assess based on the circumstances of the case as to whether all the contributions by Policy GB8(1)(a), (b) and (c) have been met.

112. As set out above, if the proposal is being considered under GB7(1)(g) or (h), and the Golden Rules are not met, then the proposal would be inappropriate development. However, if the proposal is being considered under one of the other criteria under GB7 (for example, it is previously developed land and meets GB7(1)(e), then a failure to meet the Golden Rules under GB8 would weigh against the proposal as a material consideration, but it would not mean the proposal reverted to being inappropriate development. The weight to be given to this failure would be a matter for the Inspector.

113. Policy GB8(2) states that substantial weight should be given to the importance of complying with the Golden Rules. This only relates to development within the Green Belt. Adherence to them will be a material consideration to be taken into account in any final balance in cases where the proposal is either inappropriate development or not inappropriate development.

114. Policy GB8(3) introduces 3 circumstances in which a viability assessment demonstrating that it would not be possible for a development to comply with the Golden Rules may be submitted as part of a development proposal. Policy GB8(4) then seeks to ensure that development proposals should still make the maximum

<!-- Part2 p.105 -->

possible contribution to affordable housing and other infrastructure requirements, and where contributions are significantly below those set out at GB8(1)(a)(i) or (a)(ii), they will only be acceptable in exceptional cases where fully evidenced and justified. This will be a planning judgement on the evidence before you.

115. Footnote 43 to GB8 also specifies that the Golden Rules do not apply to:

(a) developments brought forward on land released from the Green Belt through plans adopted prior to 12 December 2024;

(b) developments that were granted planning permission on Green Belt land prior to 12 December 2024;

or (c) Traveller sites.
