# 24 Green Belts: What if the parties have not raised the question of inappropriateness?

<!-- Part2 p.95 -->

## What if the parties have not raised the question of inappropriateness?

62. Sometimes, although the site is in the Green Belt, the question of inappropriateness may not have been raised. If it is an important issue (perhaps because it is clear that the proposal might be inappropriate development), you should seek the views of the parties.

63. Alternatively, it might be clear to you that the proposal would not be inappropriate or that the location in the Green Belt is immaterial to your consideration of the appeal (for example, this might be the case where the appeal is against a condition which would not have any implications for openness). If those circumstances it might be prudent to confirm this but it is not essential if the location of the site in the Green Belt is essentially a non-issue.
