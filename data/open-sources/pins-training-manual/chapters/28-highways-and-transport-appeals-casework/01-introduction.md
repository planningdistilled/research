# 28 Highways and Transport (Appeals Casework): Introduction

<!-- Part2 p.243 -->

## Introduction

1. Inspectors make their decision on the basis of the evidence before them. Consequently, they may, where justified by the evidence, depart from the advice given in this chapter.

2. Matters relating to traffic, parking, and highway safety crop up frequently in appeals. This training material is primarily intended to assist in addressing those issues in a practical manner having regard to both technical and non-technical evidence.
