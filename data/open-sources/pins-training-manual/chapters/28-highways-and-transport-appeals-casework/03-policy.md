# 28 Highways and Transport (Appeals Casework): Policy

<!-- Part2 p.243 -->

## Policy

3. Specific policies on transport are set out in the NPPF policies on Promoting Sustainable Transport. You should be familiar with all these policies and with those on Promoting Healthy Communities and Achieving Well-Designed Places..

4. The most commonly referred to technical evidence in many appeals can be found in Manual for Streets (MfS) and Manual for Streets 2 (MfS2). MfS provides standards on lightly trafficked residential streets, whereas MfS2 extends their application to busier streets and non-trunk roads. The Design Manual for Roads and Bridges (DMRB), which is now part of the design manual for the Strategic Road Network (SRN), sets out standards for trunk roads and motorways which are the responsibility of Highways England. Highway authorities may adopt a combination of these standards.
