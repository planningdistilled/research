# 00 Role of the Inspector: The Planning Inspectorate

<!-- Part1 p.8 -->

## The Planning Inspectorate

1. The Planning Inspectorate is an Executive Agency of the Department for Housing, Communities and Local Government (MHCLG).

2. We report to the Secretary of State for Housing, Communities and Local Government under the terms of an Agency Framework Document.

3. We are responsible for a wide variety of work, including:

- Planning, enforcement and listed building appeals

- Applications which have been ‘called-in’ by the Secretary of State or Welsh Ministers

- National Infrastructure Applications/Developments of National Significance

- Development plan examinations

- Rights of Way and other specialist casework

- Work for other government departments (including the Departments for Environment, Food & Rural Affairs and Transport)

4. Our purpose and vision are as follows:

Purpose (from Strategic Plan 2024 - 27 and The Planning Inspectorate Annual Report and Accounts 2022/23) To maintain a fair planning system, support significant infrastructure development and help communities shape where they live

Mission (From Strategic Plan 2024 - 27) To provide consistently timely, high-quality and cost-effective decisions, recommendations and advice.

Values – Open, Fair, Impartial and Customer-focussed.

5. This Training Manual Chapter is mainly aimed at Inspectors carrying out planning and appeals casework. However, guidance on the ‘Franks’ Principles’, natural justice, human rights and the Code of Conduct also applies to other casework.
