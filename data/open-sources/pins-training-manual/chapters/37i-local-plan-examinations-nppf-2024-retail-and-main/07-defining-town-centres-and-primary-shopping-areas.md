# 37i Local Plan Examinations (NPPF 2024): Retail and Main Town Centre Uses: Defining town centres and primary shopping areas

<!-- Part3 p.101 -->

### Defining town centres and primary shopping areas

22. As the NPPF Glossary makes clear, it is the local plan policies map that defines the geographical extent of each centre. The bigger town centres will usually include a designated “primary shopping area” (the area where retail development is concentrated) together with areas predominantly occupied by main town centre uses within and adjacent to the primary shopping area. District and local centres will usually comprise mainly retail uses.

23. The PPG advises that planning policies are expected to define the extent of primary shopping areas. It goes onto advise that Authorities may, where appropriate, also wish to define primary and secondary frontages where their use can be justified in supporting the vitality and viability of particular centres.
