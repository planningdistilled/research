# 37i Local Plan Examinations (NPPF 2024): Retail and Main Town Centre Uses: Complementary strategies

<!-- Part3 p.103 -->

### Complementary strategies

36. According to the PPG the town centre strategy should also consider what complementary strategies are needed to enhance centres, to encourage the centres’ vitality. Inspectors should be aware of any such complementary initiatives as they may have implications for site allocations and policies.
