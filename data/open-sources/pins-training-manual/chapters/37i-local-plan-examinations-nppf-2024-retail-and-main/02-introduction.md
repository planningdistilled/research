# 37i Local Plan Examinations (NPPF 2024): Retail and Main Town Centre Uses: Introduction

<!-- Part3 p.97 -->

## Introduction

1. This topic section of the ITM Local Plans Examinations chapter applies to the examination of plans under the December 2024 NPPF (“legacy plans”) prepared under the 2004 Act (before the 2026 amendments introduced by the LURA 2023) and the 2012 Regulations under NPPF December 2024 (as amended in February 2025).

2. It provides advice on dealing with policies and evidence on retail and other main town centre uses in examinations.

3. Inspectors should ensure they are familiar with relevant advice contained within the NPPF, especially Chapter 7 Ensuring the vitality of town centres, and with the PPG sections entitled Town centres and retail, Housing and economic needs assessment, and Plan-making.

4. This chapter only addresses plans to which the policies of the December 2024 NPPF apply. It takes into account the December 2024 National Planning Policy Framework (NPPF or Framework) and the latest versions of Planning Practice Guidance (PPG).
