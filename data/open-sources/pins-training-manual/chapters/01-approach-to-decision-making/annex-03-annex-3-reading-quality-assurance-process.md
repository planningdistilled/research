# 01 Approach to Decision Making: Annex 3 - Reading (quality assurance) process

<!-- Part1 p.112 -->

## Annex 3 - Reading (quality assurance) process

The expectation is that Inspectors outside of training issue their own decisions without them being pre-issue read.

They may, at times, nevertheless wish to submit parts of their decisions to their Inspector Manager (IM) for informal comment or advice. That process should continue as agreed between the Inspector and their manager and is unaffected by the quality assurance system or this guidance.

However, there may be a need for full pre-issue reading in some circumstances although this is a matter of discretion for the individual Inspector in consultation with their IM (and Professional Lead, if necessary). These must be submitted via the Checkmark system. The situations where this may arise include:

- Where the case raises novel or specialist issues particularly if they are unfamiliar to the Inspector, or

- Where the outcome may have wider-reaching implications for the application of policy or where complex legal arguments have been made in enforcement casework;

But these types of case do not need to be read before issue as a matter of course.

Where the case is a re-determination of a remitted or quashed decision, the decision must be sent for pre-issue reading via Checkmark.

Where pre issue reading is necessary it must be submitted via Checkmark and preferably be undertaken by the IM or by another IM if the Inspector’s manager is unable to do so either because the decision raises specialist issues outside the experience of the IM or because they are unavailable. Checkmark allows you to flag the name of the Inspector Manager.

All pre issue decisions that need to be read by the Professional Leads must also be submitted via Checkmark.

Given the specific issues arising in the enforcement and specialist casework area and the potential high risk of challenge, Inspectors are advised to consult their line managers before submitting re-determination cases for issue.

All Secretary of State reports will be reviewed by the QA Panel.
