# 34 Landscape and Visual Impact Assessment: Methodology for highway schemes

<!-- Part2 p.535 -->

## Methodology for highway schemes

79. For highway schemes relating to trunk roads and motorways, the relevant guidance is published by National Highways10. It supersedes DMRB Volume 11 Part 5 - Landscape Effects, and Interim Advice Note 135/10 – Landscape and visual effects assessment, which are now withdrawn. This is a standard, not guidance, but should only be used for highway projects.
