# 02 Site Visits: Representations and late evidence

<!-- Part1 p.131 -->

## Representations and late evidence

48. You should firmly resist accepting any evidence or revised plans which you may be offered at the site visit. This is to avoid any accusations of unfairness. At the site visit, depending on the circumstances, you might advise that:

<!-- Part1 p.132 -->

- evidence should be submitted on time unless there are exceptional circumstances

- you cannot accept any evidence on site

- if someone wishes to submit additional evidence they should be told to contact the Case Officer in writing, as soon as possible, to explain why late evidence is being submitted but you should not give any indication that it will be accepted.

49. You can find further information on late evidence in ‘The Approach to Decision Making’ ITM.
