# 31 Housing: First Homes

<!-- Part2 p.374 -->

## First Homes

156. Footnote 31 of the 2024 NPPF states “The requirement to deliver a minimum of 25% of affordable housing as First Homes, as set out in 'Affordable Homes Update' Written Ministerial Statement dated 24 May 2021, no longer applies.

<!-- Part2 p.375 -->

Delivery of First Homes can, however, continue where local planning authorities judge that they meet local need.” Footnote 90 confirms this and also that the definition of affordable homes in the Glossary should be read in conjunction with the WMS. Further detail about First Homes is set out in the PPG.

157. The implication is therefore that the delivery of First Homes is a matter of local discretion but there are no fixed national policy requirements. They can nonetheless be considered as a type of affordable housing. If there is a dispute about the inclusion of First Homes in developments or their proportion as part of the affordable housing contribution, then Inspectors will need to decide the weight to be given to the national policy requirement for First Homes when judged against existing development plan policies.

158. As First Homes are an affordable housing product, the guidance in the Affordable Housing section of this ITM chapter is relevant. Developers of First Homes may obtain an exemption from the requirement to pay CIL under the Community Infrastructure Regulations 2010.

159. A Model S106 Agreement has been published by MHCLG. These model clauses are for use by local authorities and home builders preparing s106 agreements delivering First Homes through developer contributions.

### Exception Sites

160. The WMS and PPG outline a First Homes Exception Site policy. These exception sites may be on land which is not already allocated for housing. They should:

- Comprise First Homes, and

- Be adjacent to existing settlements, proportionate in size to them, not compromise the protection given to areas or assets of particular importance in the NPPF58, and comply with any local design policies and standards.

161. The WMS states that these exception sites should be supported by Local Authorities unless the need for such homes is already being met within the local authority’s area. First Homes exception sites may deliver a small proportion of market housing provided it is necessary to ensure overall viability of the site. This should be backed up by evidence.

162. Guidance states that what constitutes a 'proportionate development' will vary depending on local circumstances. Plan-makers are encouraged to set policies which specify their approach to determining the proportionality of First Homes exception site proposals.

[^58]: That is, those areas referred to in footnote 7 of the NPPF.

<!-- Part2 p.376 -->

163. PPG makes clear that First Homes exception sites cannot come forward in areas designated as Green Belt or in designated rural areas as defined by Annex 2 of the NPPF.
