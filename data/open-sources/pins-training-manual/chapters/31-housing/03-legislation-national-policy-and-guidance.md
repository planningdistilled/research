# 31 Housing: Legislation, national policy and guidance

## Legislation, national policy and guidance

5. At the outset it is important to remember that the statutory provisions in 1 and section 38(6) of the 2004 Act 2 apply to all

s70(1)(a) of the 1990 Act planning appeals, including housing appeals. Those provisions are not displaced by paragraph 11 or by any other part of the National Planning Policy Framework ( the NPPF), as NPPF paragraph 12 makes clear. In the context of s38(6), the NPPF has the status of a material consideration which (when considered together with any other relevant material considerations) may or may not indicate that an appeal should be determined otherwise than in accordance with the development plan.

6. Specific policies on housing are set out in Section 5 ‘Delivering a sufficient supply of homes’ of the NPPF. You should be familiar with those policies and also with what is said about planning for housing in NPPF Section 3 ‘Plan-making’ as well as with the NPPF as a whole.

8. Some of the implications of this national policy and guidance are explored in the rest of this chapter. The chapter also reflects the extensive caselaw concerning housing appeals since the publication of the 2012 NPPF. A new and extensively revised NPPF was published in July 2018 with updated NPPFs following in February 2019, July 2021 and December 2023 and December 2024. However, some of the caselaw referring to the 2012 NPPF remains relevant, since many of its provisions have been carried forward into the NPPF, albeit with modifications and, in most cases, different paragraph numbers. Inspectors may need to refer back to the 2012 NPPF and other versions of it to understand how the caselaw relates to the new edition. The footnotes to this chapter provide extracts from, and references to, key judgments.
