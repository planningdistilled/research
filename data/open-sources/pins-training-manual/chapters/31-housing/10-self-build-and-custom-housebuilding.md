# 31 Housing: Self-build and custom housebuilding

<!-- Part2 p.376 -->

## Self-build and custom housebuilding

### Background

164. Paragraph 70 b) of the NPPF supports small sites to come forward for self-build and custom-build housing. The Self-Build and Custom Housebuilding Act 2015 requires local planning authorities to establish and publicise a register of those who are seeking to acquire serviced plots of land in the authority's area for their own self-build and custom housebuilding. The Self-build and Custom Housebuilding Regulations 2016 (SI 2016/950) make provision in relation to the definition of a serviced plot of land, registers kept under the Act and exemption from the duty.

165. The Housing and Planning Act 2016 added to the above Act a duty to grant planning permission subject to exemptions at s2A. This provides that authorities must give development permission (for the carrying out of self-build and custom housebuilding59) in respect of enough serviced plots of land to meet the demand for self-build and custom housebuilding in the authority's area arising in each base period. However, there is scope for an exemption under s2B of the 2016 Act which may be applied for under Regulation 11.

166. The legislation does not specify how LPAs must record suitable permissions, but the PPG60 provides examples of what methods an LPA may wish to consider to determine whether an application or development is for self-build or custom housebuilding:

- Whether developers have identified that self-build or custom build plots will be included as part of their development and it is clear that the initial owner of the homes will have primary input into its final design and layout

- Whether a planning application references self-build or custom build and it is clear that the initial owner of the homes will have primary input into its final design and layout, and

- Whether a Community Infrastructure Levy or Section 106 exemption has been granted for a particular development.

167. There is further guidance in the PPG chapter Self-build and Custom Housebuilding including how relevant authorities can increase the number of planning permissions which are suitable for self-build and custom housebuilding. It also indicates that at the end of each base period authorities

### Issues in casework

168. Depending on the circumstances of the case, including any relevant development plan policies, it may be necessary for the planning permission to incorporate some means of ensuring that custom / self-build proposals are constructed in this manner. Specifying it in the description of development alone is unlikely to be sufficient to ensure that dwellings are constructed as self-build / custom. Conditions requiring occupation of the dwelling by the person who built it would be unlikely to pass the tests within paragraph 56 of the NPPF. A s106 obligation is likely to be the most appropriate method of ensuring that the development is self or custom build housing rather than market housing. If a condition is suggested, the best approach would be to seek submissions from the LPA on the practicalities of enforcing any suggested condition and submissions from both parties on whether the condition meets the legal and policy tests. Whilst conditions may be useful, it should be noted that for self and custom builds conditions can be difficult to enforce. To ensure a self/custom build remains in the possession of the Appellant the condition needs to be applied to the owner of the land, rather than the land. This can mean that if owner sells or leaves the property before the timeframe for occupation is complete, the house would no longer have permission to exist. Therefore, whilst conditions can be used, they should be thoroughly tested before being imposed.

169. Under the CIL regulations, it is possible to apply for a CIL exemption if the dwelling is custom / self-build. The regulations require evidence to be submitted and if personal circumstances of the applicant change at any time within 3 years of initial occupation, they must notify the LPA and pay the appropriate CIL. The LPA, as the CIL charging authority, are responsible for enforcing this. However, there is nothing in the Self Build and Custom Housebuilding Act or the Self Build and Custom Housebuilding Regulations that requires an occupation period.

170. Unlike its predecessor, the 2024 NPPF does not contain any exemptions to the requirement to provide affordable housing in major development where it is for people wishing to build or commission their own homes. Furthermore, footnote 28 states that self-build and custom housebuilding could “provide market or affordable housing.” such schemes are not exempt from providing it. If any relevant local plan polices include an exception to provide affordable housing for self-build and custom housebuilding (based on the 2023 NPPF), they will not be in line with paragraph 66 of the 2024 NPPF and as such, you will need
