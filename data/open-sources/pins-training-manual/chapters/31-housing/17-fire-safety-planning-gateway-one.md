# 31 Housing: Fire Safety (Planning Gateway One)

<!-- Part2 p.398 -->

## Fire Safety (Planning Gateway One)

249. The PPG, ‘Fire safety and high-rise residential buildings (from 1 August 2021)’, sets out background information on the introduction of the policy and how measures are to ensure fire safety matters as they relate to land use planning are incorporated at the planning stage. ‘Planning gateway one’ has two key elements:

- To require the developer to submit a fire statement with a relevant application for planning permission for development which involves one or more relevant buildings; and

<!-- Part2 p.399 -->

- to establish the Health and Safety Executive (HSE) as a statutory consultee for relevant planning applications. HSE have established a new Building Safety Regulator (BSR) to fulfil this task.

250. From 1 August 2021, applications for planning permission for development which involves the provision of one of more relevant building, development of an existing relevant building, or development within the curtilage of a relevant building, must be accompanied by a fire statement.

251. Relevant buildings contain two or more dwellings or educational accommodation and satisfy the height condition of being 18 metres or more in height or containing 7 or more storeys.86

252. Fire statements should contain information about the fire safety design principles, concepts and standards that have been applied to the development. They must be on a form published by the Secretary of State87, which includes information regarding (but not limited to) site layout, emergency vehicle access locations, consultation undertaken on issues relating to fire safety of the development, and how local policies relating to fire safety have been taken into account.

253. Exemptions from the requirement to submit a fire statement are outlined in paragraph 010 of the PPG. For example, it is not mandatory to submit a fire statement where the application is for outline planning permission.

254. From 1 August 2021 LPAs are required to consult the HSE regarding relevant planning applications which involve one or more relevant building.

255. Further details can be found in fire safety and high-rise residential buildings PPG.
