# 03 Hearings: After the hearing – writing your decision

<!-- Part1 p.162 -->

### After the hearing – writing your decision

116. Your approach to writing the decision is likely to be similar to cases considered by written representations. However, if a specific point was only raised at the hearing or if particular matters were agreed, then this should be mentioned.

117. At the end of your decision, you will need to add lists of:

any documents, plans and photos handed to you during the hearing as evidence. The lists need to be comprehensive but it is not always necessary to refer to every individual document – for example – “bundle of documents submitted by Mrs #”

The names of all who spoke, for example:

For each principal party -Advocate (name, King’s Counsel/Counsel and who instructed by)
