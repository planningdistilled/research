# 03 Hearings: Video evidence

<!-- Part1 p.166 -->

### Video evidence

142. You may be asked to view video evidence (for example showing highway conditions or a virtual reality model of the proposed development). If so, you should make sure that all those at the hearing can see the recording and are able to comment on it.
