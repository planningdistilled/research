# 03 Hearings: Introduction

<!-- Part1 p.141 -->

### Introduction

1. Inspectors make their decisions on the basis of the evidence before them. Consequently, they may, where justified by the evidence, depart from the advice given in this guide.

2. This advice relates mainly to the conduct of Hearings in planning, advertisement and listed building consent appeals, although the principles set out may have wider relevance.

3. Further advice on the conduct of enforcement (s174) and lawful development certificate (s195) Hearings can be found in the 'Enforcement’ chapter of the ITM.

4. Advice about Hearings relating to applications made direct to the Planning Inspectorate in respect of underperforming authorities in England can be found in PINS Note 44/2013r1 'Major Applications - underperforming authorities' and in ‘Planning Applications Process: Section 62A Authorities in Special Measures’. Please note that there are differences in format and procedure when compared to s78 appeals.
