# 03 Hearings: Note taking

<!-- Part1 p.159 -->

### Note taking

90. You need to record the discussion and your notes will probably be the only record of what took place. However, you do not need to keep a word-by-word account. Instead focus on the main points made, particularly those which have not previously been set out in writing. If necessary, you can ask the parties to slow down or repeat a point if you wish to make sure you record it accurately.

91. You need to strike the right balance between engaging with the parties and taking notes.

<!-- Part1 p.160 -->

92. A more thorough note will be needed if a costs application is made orally (see below).

93. Bear in mind that your notes may subsequently be disclosed, for example, if a request is made by one of the parties. See the Approach to Decision Making on the retention of notes.
