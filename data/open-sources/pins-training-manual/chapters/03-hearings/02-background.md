# 03 Hearings: Background

<!-- Part1 p.141 -->

### Background

5. Hearings were introduced in 1982 as an alternative to public inquiries. They were originally known as ‘informal hearings’ and are sometimes still referred to in this way.

6. Hearings are inquisitorial. They can be thought of as a structured discussion which is led by the Inspector. The inquisitorial burden falls on the Inspector.1

7. In contrast, Inquiries are adversarial. The parties present their cases to the Inspector and witnesses are subject to cross-examination. The inquisitorial burden mainly falls on the opposing party rather than the Inspector.

8. Despite the differences, Hearings are, nevertheless, a formal and structured procedure.
