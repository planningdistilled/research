# 03 Hearings: What is unreasonable/unacceptable behaviour?

<!-- Part1 p.181 -->

### What is unreasonable/unacceptable behaviour?

7. Basically, anything which disrupts the smooth running of a Hearing or Inquiry and prevents you from focusing on the arguments or any other party from making their case. This could range from threats or shows of aggression to constant low-level interruptions, particularly if they are aimed at destabilising another party’s attempt to make their case.

8. The ITM advises that the general principle is that filming and recording should be allowed. However, if you consider the way you or the event is being filmed or recorded to be intimidating you should ask that it stops. If the person recording refuses this constitutes unreasonable behaviour.
