# 03 Hearings: Pre-Hearing note

<!-- Part1 p.146 -->

### Pre-Hearing note

30. If you have time it is often useful to send out a Pre-Hearing note to the main parties. This can set out the agenda for the Hearing itself, including your initial identification of the main issues.

<!-- Part1 p.147 -->

31. Such a note can also include queries you may have as to any procedural matters4, amended plans, or missing documents so that the main parties can arrange for them to be responded to at the Hearing more efficiently.

32. It is useful to ask the LPA to put this note on its website. Interested parties can often register for ‘alerts’ on LPA websites when new information is posted on a case so that they can also be made aware of it prior to the Hearing.
