# 03 Hearings: Filming/recording

<!-- Part1 p.171 -->

### Filming/recording

Does anyone intend to film or record the event?

[If so] – does anyone have any objections to this? [if so, can they be resolved by restricting filming to certain angles?]

[If filming/recording takes place] – please make sure any filming or recording is carried out responsibly and does not interfere with the smooth running of the hearing
