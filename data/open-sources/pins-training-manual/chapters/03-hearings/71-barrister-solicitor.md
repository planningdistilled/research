# 03 Hearings: Barrister/Solicitor:

<!-- Part1 p.182 -->

### Barrister/Solicitor:

Mr/Ms X, I have asked you on 3 occasions now not to interrupt me/AN Other. If you do so again I will exercise my powers under Rule 11(8) of the Town and Country Planning (Hearings Procedure) (England) Rules 2000 and require you to leave. I will consider whether to make an award of Costs against your client for unreasonable behaviour. I will also take action to report your unreasonable behaviour to [The Bar Standards Board] [The Law Society].
