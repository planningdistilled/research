# 03 Hearings: Legal representation

<!-- Part1 p.163 -->

### Legal representation

122. Rule 9(3) allows that a person who is entitled to appear may be represented by another person. It is up to the party to decide who represents them and this may be a solicitor or barrister. However, this should not affect how you run the hearing. If necessary, you can remind the parties that there will be no cross examination and that any questions should be put through you.
