# 03 Hearings: Legislation and procedural guidance

<!-- Part1 p.141 -->

### Legislation and procedural guidance

9. The statutory rules governing Hearings are contained in the Town and Country Planning (Hearings Procedure) (England) Rules 2000 (SI 2000/1626) (which have been amended on a number of occasions subsequently).

10. Procedural guidance can be found in ‘Planning Appeals: Procedural Guide – England’ and ‘Guide to taking part in planning and listed building appeals proceeding by a hearing’.
