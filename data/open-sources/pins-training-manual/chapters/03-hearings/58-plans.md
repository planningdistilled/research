# 03 Hearings: Plans

<!-- Part1 p.173 -->

### Plans

Clarify which plans were before the LPA when it made its decision.

Clarify the status of any other plans (superseded, illustrative, revised plans provided at appeal)

<!-- Part1 p.174 -->

If revised plans submitted at appeal – decide whether to accept – ask:

Would they materially change the proposal?

Would any party be prejudiced – because they might have been denied an opportunity to comment having regard to the Holborn principles

Decide whether to accept or not
