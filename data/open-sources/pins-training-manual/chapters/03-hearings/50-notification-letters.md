# 03 Hearings: Notification letters

<!-- Part1 p.171 -->

### Notification letters

Can I have a copy of the Council’s letters of notification

of the appeal and

confirming the date, time and location of the Hearing

[if not already provided & satisfactory]

[check – were the letters sent to those they should have been, in time – eg at least 2 weeks before the hearing – are the details of the date, time and venue correct?]

[If the letters cannot be provided, were not sent or are incorrect – consider whether the interests of any parties would be prejudiced – is it necessary to adjourn the hearing to allow the correct notification to take place?]
