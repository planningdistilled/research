# 03 Hearings: Challenges to the validity of the appeal or application

<!-- Part1 p.165 -->

### Challenges to the validity of the appeal or application

137. Listen to the arguments put to you. Unless the interests of a party have been seriously prejudiced you should continue with the hearing. A breach of the Rules does not itself invalidate the proceedings or require redress. If no-one is at a disadvantage, the breach is unlikely to be serious.

138. If objections persist you may need to advise the person making them that, although you intend to continue with the hearing, they may also make their concerns known by writing to the case officer straightaway.
