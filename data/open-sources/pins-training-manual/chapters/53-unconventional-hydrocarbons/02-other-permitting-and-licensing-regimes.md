# 53 Unconventional Hydrocarbons: Other Permitting and Licensing Regimes

<!-- Part4 p.114 -->

## Other Permitting and Licensing Regimes

39. The North Sea Transition Authority (NSTA) regulates the licensing of exploration and development of England’s onshore oil and gas resources. The NSTA issues well consents, development programme approvals, completion of work programme approvals and production consents.

40. As required by section 4A of the Petroleum Act 1998 (inserted by section 50 Infrastructure Act 2015), all well consents issued on or after 6th April 2016 contain a requirement that the Licensee obtain consent from the Secretary of State (“hydraulic fracturing consent” or “HFC”) before carrying out any associated hydraulic fracturing as defined in section 4B of that Act.
