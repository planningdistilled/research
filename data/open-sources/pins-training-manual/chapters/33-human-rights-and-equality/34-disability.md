# 33 Human Rights and Equality: Disability

<!-- Part2 p.489 -->

### Disability

203. S6(1) provides that a person has a disability if (a) they have a physical or mental impairment and (b) the impairment has a substantial and long-term adverse effect on their ability to carry out normal day-to-day activities. Schedule 1 of the EA10 contains supplementary provisions on the determination of disability, and there is also guidance from the Commission.

204. The EA10 is based on the ‘social model’ of disability, which is that disability is created by barriers in society, particularly environmental, attitudinal and organisational barriers. Focussing on the removal or mitigation of such barriers

– by making reasonable adjustments – is a more positive and inclusive approach than the ‘medical model’ to try and ‘fix’ disabled people in order that they can participate in society.

205. The Census 2021 found that 9.8 million people representing 17.7% of the population in England were disabled; the number being higher but proportion lower than in 2011. Six million or 25.4% of households in England had at least one disabled member in 2021.

206. Advice on writing about people with disabilities is set out in Annex A.
