# 33 Human Rights and Equality: Race

### Race

235. S9(1) of the EA10 provides that race includes (a) colour, (b) nationality56, (c) ethnic or national origin. S9(2) provides that a person with this protected

Under s9(3) and (4), a racial group is a group of persons defined by reference to race, and it may comprise two or more distinct racial groups (such as Black British).

236. S13(5) of the EA10 provides that, if a person has the protected characteristic of race, less favourable treatment (amounting to direct discrimination) includes segregating that person from others.

237. The Census 2021 showed that 80.5% of the population, down from 87.5% in 2011, identify as English, Welsh, Scottish, Northern Irish or British White. The next largest ethnic group encompasses Asian, Asian British or Asian Welsh people, accounting for 9.3% of the population of England and Wales, up from 7.5% in 2011. The third and fourth largest ethnic groups were Black, Black British, Black Welsh and Caribbean or African, and Mixed or Multiple ethnic groups which had increased by 2021 to 3% and 2.9% of the population.

238. There were significant increases in other groups, including other white and any other ethnic group. 10.1% of households in England and Wales consisted of members identifying with two or more different ethnic groups, again up from 8.7% in 2011. Advice on writing about race and ethnicity is set out in Annex A.

239. The Census 2021 showed that English or Welsh was the main language for 91.1% of the population, down from 92% in 2011. A further 7.1% of respondents were proficient in English or Welsh. The most common other main languages were Polish, Romanian, Panjabi and Urdu, but others were spoken in 6% of households.
