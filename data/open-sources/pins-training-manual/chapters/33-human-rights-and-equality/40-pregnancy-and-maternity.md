# 33 Human Rights and Equality: Pregnancy and Maternity

<!-- Part2 p.493 -->

### Pregnancy and Maternity

232. ‘Pregnancy’ and ‘maternity’ are not defined in the EA1055 but are subject to various specific provisions as explained further by the Commission.

233. S17 is concerned with pregnancy and maternity discrimination in ‘non-work cases’, including services, public functions and premises. A woman is discriminated against if she is treated unfavourably because of her pregnancy or giving birth, including for breast-feeding, in the previous 26 weeks.

234. Under s13(6), direct discrimination in relation to the protected characteristic of sex includes less favourable treatment of a woman because she is breast-feeding, whether within 26 weeks of giving birth or not.
