# 33 Human Rights and Equality: Overall Conclusion – Human Rights and the PSED, limited interference but compliance with the three aims (personal permission)

<!-- Part2 p.514 -->

### Conclusion

…the hardstanding [and other structures] are all inappropriate development in the Green Belt. In accordance with the Framework, I attach substantial weight to the harm caused to the Green Belt. I also find that the harm caused by the structures to

<!-- Part2 p.515 -->

the character and appearance of the site and surrounding area is serious and conflicts with…………...[policy]

…The appellants’ and their customers’ needs for accessible car parking [and other considerations] only carry moderate weight…[and] do not clearly outweigh the harm…Very special circumstances…do not exist to justify the development.

I have had due regard to the Public Sector Equality Duty set out under the Equality Act 2010. The harm caused by the appeal development outweighs its benefits in terms of eliminating discrimination against persons with the protected characteristics of disability, advancing equality of opportunity for those persons and fostering good relations between them and others.
