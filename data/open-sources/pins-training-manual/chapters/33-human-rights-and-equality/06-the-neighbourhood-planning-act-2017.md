# 33 Human Rights and Equality: The Neighbourhood Planning Act 2017

<!-- Part2 p.457 -->

### The Neighbourhood Planning Act 2017

40. S8 of the Neighbourhood Planning Act 2017 inserted s34(2) into the Planning and Compulsory Purchase Act 2004 so that ‘the Secretary of State must issue guidance for local planning authorities on how their local development documents (taken as a whole) should address housing needs that result from old age or disability’.

41. In 2019, therefore, the Commission produced a toolkit for local authorities in England on Housing and Disabled People, and a chapter on Housing for Older and Disabled People was added to the Planning Practice Guidance as set out below and in the Local Plan Examinations ITM.
