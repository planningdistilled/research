46. In Harris v Haringey LBC [2010] EWCA Civ 703, the Council granted permission for the redevelopment of an indoor market. The decision was challenged on the basis that the Council had failed to ‘promote equality of opportunity and good relations between different racial groups’. Objections to the application had been made on the basis that the loss of the market would harm traders and customers from local ethnic minority communities for whom the market was a social and economic hub.

47. The Council argued that they had complied with the then Race Equality Duty because their report had referred to policies which promoted regeneration and quality of life. Nonetheless, the CoA held that the Council had not demonstrated ‘due regard’ to the impacts on equality of opportunity and good race relations.
