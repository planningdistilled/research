### Costs Order

15.In exercise of the powers under Section 5 of the Acquisition of Land Act 1981 and section 250(5) of the Local Government Act 1972, and all other enabling powers in that behalf, IT IS HEREBY ORDERED that [full name or respondent] shall pay to [full name of applicant], the costs of the appeal proceedings described in the heading of this decision [limited to those costs incurred in]; such costs to be assessed in the Senior Courts Costs Office if not agreed.
