# 32 Housing Compulsory Purchase Orders: Unopposed Lands

<!-- Part2 p.437 -->

### Unopposed Lands

[This section is only required where there are some parts of the Order that are

not subject to objection, and then not in every instance. If the description of the unopposed lands is adequately covered by the general description of the Order lands, then the section will not be necessary. Otherwise only a brief description will usually be necessary, but sufficient to support any conclusions the Inspector may reach in regard to that part of the Order area.]
