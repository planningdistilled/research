# 32 Housing Compulsory Purchase Orders: The Case for the [name] [Acquiring Authority]

<!-- Part2 p.436 -->

### The Case for the [name] [Acquiring Authority]

[Generally, the case for the acquiring authority should be reported first and

should record the whole of its general case, although in as concise a form as is practicable. Sub-headings may be used where appropriate. Any modifications to the Order suggested by the authority should be recorded.]
