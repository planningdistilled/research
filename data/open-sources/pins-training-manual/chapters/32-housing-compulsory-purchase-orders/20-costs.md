# 32 Housing Compulsory Purchase Orders: Costs

<!-- Part2 p.428 -->

### Costs

22. The advice on costs in the Government’s Planning Practice Guidance applies generally [PPG ID:16 paragraphs 027 to 064). PPG ID:16 paragraphs 057 to 064 relate specifically to costs awards for CPOs; this is relevant to Orders made under the Housing Acts.

A) Where Remaining Objectors are successful an award will be made in their favour unless there are exceptional reasons for not doing so. There is no need for an application for costs to be made by the Objector for an award to be made. However, if the case goes to an inquiry and there are applications for costs for unreasonable behaviour then the Inspector will need to hear those applications. If the Inspector decides not to confirm the CPO then the Inspector will not be able to award the objector costs on the grounds of unreasonable behaviour (because this would mean the objector would be paid twice).

B) Where Remaining Objectors are unsuccessful and the CPO is confirmed, an award of costs may be made to the objector or the order-making authority because of unreasonable behaviour [PPG ID:16-060].

C) A template for a costs decision relating to a housing CPO written representations case is included at Annex 5

D) A template for a costs decision relating to a housing CPO inquiry case is included at Annex 6
