# 32 Housing Compulsory Purchase Orders: Harassment

<!-- Part2 p.423 -->

### Harassment

10. Aside from the criminal offence of harassment, it has been held that the conduct of a landlord towards tenants may be so unreasonable as to give rise to conditions of unsatisfactory housing (R –v- Secretary of State for the Environment ex parte Royal Borough of Kensington and Chelsea (1987)). Section 29 of the Housing Act 1988 describes the statutory circumstances of harassment. Part II CPOs arising from harassment are very rare. Inspectors dealing with an Order where harassment is alleged should discuss the case with their Professional Lead before the inquiry and be alert to the possible need for evidence to be taken on oath.
