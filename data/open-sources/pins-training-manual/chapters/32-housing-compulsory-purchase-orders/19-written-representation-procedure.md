# 32 Housing Compulsory Purchase Orders: Written Representation Procedure

<!-- Part2 p.427 -->

### Written Representation Procedure

22. When there are objections to the authorisation of a CPO the written representation procedure may be used as an alternative to an inquiry. Only if all remaining Objectors

<!-- Part2 p.428 -->

agree will this procedure be used. The Inspector will determine the case or report to the Secretary of State following the holding of a site visit.
