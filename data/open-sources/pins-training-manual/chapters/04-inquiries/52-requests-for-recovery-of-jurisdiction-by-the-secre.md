# 04 Inquiries: Requests for recovery of jurisdiction by the Secretary of State

<!-- Part1 p.225 -->

### Requests for recovery of jurisdiction by the Secretary of State

241. You may be asked to refer the appeal to the Secretary of State. If so, note the arguments put forward and inform the parties that consideration will be given to seeking the Secretary of State's ruling as to whether jurisdiction should be recovered. The matter must be brought to the attention of the Case Officer immediately so that a decision on recovery can be made.
