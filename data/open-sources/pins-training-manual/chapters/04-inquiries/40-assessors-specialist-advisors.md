# 04 Inquiries: Assessors/specialist advisors

<!-- Part1 p.217 -->

### Assessors/specialist advisors

180. An Assessor is a specialist adviser, usually scientific or technical, selected to assist you by hearing, testing and weighing evidence of a specialised nature that may be outside your experience but which may have an important bearing
