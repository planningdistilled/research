### Appeal Reference(s)s: [ ]

### Address [ }

### Description of development [ }

### INSPECTOR’S PRE-INQUIRY DIRECTION

1. The above appeal arises from the failure of the Council to reach a decision on the application for planning permission. The appeals are to be heard at Public Inquiry and the Appellant has suggested a duration of [Weeks] weeks.

2. At this stage it is impossible to gauge the likely issues before the Inquiry and its likely duration.

3. In this regard, the Local Planning Authority is requested to provide by no later than [Date]:

(i) ‘putative reasons for refusal’ – if the putative reasons require Member approval beyond that date, the recommended putative reasons should be submitted;

(ii) identify unresolved matters between the Council and the Appellant and whether any of those might be capable of resolution by consultees and/or planning conditions/obligations;

(iii) identify the topics that might need to be debated at the Inquiry and the potential number of witnesses; and

(iv) any other information which may assist in preliminary arrangements for the Inquiry.

4. The Council is also urged to prioritise engagement in the Statement(s) of Common Ground. These should clearly identify matters that are agreed; those that are not agreed; and those that might be capable of agreement. The Statement(s) of Common Ground can be topic based if this would assist preparation and agreement. They should also be regarded as iterative documents with revisions should matters be narrowed or fall away.

5. The Inspector will hold a Case Management Conference on [Date and time] to discuss arrangements for the Inquiry and may issue further notes and/or directions before that meeting.

6. The Inquiry will open on [Date and time]. The parties should thereafter reserve weeks commencing [Weeks] for the Inquiry.

7. These provisional arrangements will be discussed, and hopefully finalised, at the Case Management Conference.
