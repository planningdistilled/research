# 04 Inquiries: Annex C: Content And Format of Proofs and Appendices

<!-- Part1 p.247 -->

### Annex C: Content And Format of Proofs and Appendices

(Edit as required for personal preference)

### Content

Proofs of evidence must:

- refer to the information that witnesses wish the Inspector to consider;

- cover only areas which remain at issue between the parties;

- contain the witness’s concisely expressed opinion;

- contain a clear cross reference to any supporting documents in the CD library

- Be page and paragraph numbered

Proofs must not:

- duplicate information already included in other Inquiry material, such as site description, planning history and relevant planning policy (usually in the SOCG(s);

- include new evidence;

- include extensive appendices – these should be in the CD library

- include long irrelevant biographical detail of the witness.

### Format of the proofs and appendices:

- Proofs to be no longer than 3,000 words if possible and where proofs are longer than 1,500 words, summaries are to be submitted. They must not introduce new or different evidence nor go beyond the scope of the text they summarise.

- Front covers to proofs and appendices are to be clearly titled, with the name, job title and qualifications of the witness on the cover.

- If paper copies are provided, proofs are to be spiral bound or bound in such a way as to be easily opened and read. Any appendices are to be bound separately and indexed using projecting tabs, labelled and paginated.
