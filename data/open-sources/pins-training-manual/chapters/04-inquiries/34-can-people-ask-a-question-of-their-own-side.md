# 04 Inquiries: Can people ask a question of their own side?

<!-- Part1 p.215 -->

### Can people ask a question of their own side?

160. There is nothing in the Rules preventing this. The general convention is, however, that a witness should not be cross-examined by their own side because the point of cross-examination is to test the evidence. Occasionally, however, Rule 6 parties or interested persons may want to ask a question of a witness who is on the same side as them. This will usually be if there are fundamental differences between them in some areas, even if they agree on others. Generally it can be confusing and is best discouraged.
