# 04 Inquiries: How to address people at planning appeals

<!-- Part1 p.201 -->

### How to address people at planning appeals

70. It is conventional to refer to Inspectors as “Sir”, “Madam/Ma’am” or “Inspector”. Although you may find yourself referred to as “Mr/Mrs/Ms Inspector” or

[^3]: An advocate is usually a legal professional who presents or pleads a party’s case. They will be referred to as either “Counsel” or “King’s Counsel” (the latter are known for short as “KCs” and colloquially as “Silks”). Both are barristers, but a KC is more senior. Sometimes the appellant may act as their own advocate or their agent may be their advocate.

<!-- Part1 p.202 -->

“Mr/Mrs/Ms Your Surname”. If you are referred to in the third person, it is will usually be as “the Inspector”.

71. Witnesses and third parties should be addressed with courtesy and an appropriate degree of formality. This means that in almost all cases the use of surnames prefixed by the preferred title of the person being addressed.

72. You may wish to state up front, in opening, that you will refer to witnesses as Mr/Mrs/Ms, as appears appropriate, unless you are aware of, or they express, an alternative preference. If in doubt, just ask them.
