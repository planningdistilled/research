# 04 Inquiries: Annex H: Venue Health, Safety and Other Things checklist

<!-- Part1 p.256 -->

### Annex H: Venue Health, Safety and Other Things checklist

Yes/no | Any comments

The evacuation procedure from the Inquiry room and retiring rooms, the location of fire exits, evacuation routes and assembly points

Any planned fire alarm testing or fire evacuation drills

The location of toilets, including disabled/accessible

Ensure persons attending at the start of each day are aware of the above

Check that fire exits from the Inquiry room are not blocked by tables or chairs etc

It the venue publicly accessible? (i.e. no passes needed)

Is the PA system working? Sound loop?

Are there any trip hazards from loose wires etc?

Is WiFi working?

Is the livestream working (if appropriate)?
