# 04 Inquiries: Requests to summons a witness

<!-- Part1 p.226 -->

### Requests to summons a witness

245. It may be that a representative of a body from which you wish to hear will not agree to attend the Inquiry. If this persists, following clear statements that attendance will be very helpful to you, which will often achieve the desired result, you have the power under s250(2) of the Local Government Act 1972 to issue a witness summons. It is a power that is used very rarely and should be exercised only as the very last resort. In any case, although you can compel someone to attend, you cannot require them to speak – so the exercise may be pointless. Seek advice from your IM or a PfL before using this power. For more information see Annex D.
