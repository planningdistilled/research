# 04 Inquiries: What if the validity of the appeal or application is challenged?

<!-- Part1 p.225 -->

### What if the validity of the appeal or application is challenged?

239. Listen to the arguments put to you. Unless the interests of a party have been seriously prejudiced you should continue with the Inquiry. A breach of the Rules (including the displaying and advertising of notices) does not in itself invalidate the proceedings. If no party is prejudiced the breach is unlikely to be serious.

240. If objections persist you may need to advise the person making them that, although you intend to continue with the Inquiry, they should make their concerns known in writing to the Case Officer and have the right to challenge the appeal decision in the High Court.
