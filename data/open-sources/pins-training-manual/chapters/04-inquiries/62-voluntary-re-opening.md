# 04 Inquiries: Voluntary re-opening

<!-- Part1 p.230 -->

### Voluntary re-opening

278. Powers are also available to you to enable an Inquiry to be re-opened voluntarily when not required by the Rules. Inquiries should only be re-opened voluntarily in highly exceptional circumstances (for example if an issue is likely to be of particular concern to interested persons) as the point at issue can usually be dealt with by written representations. If you consider that an Inquiry should be re-opened, you should consult your IM or a PfL.
