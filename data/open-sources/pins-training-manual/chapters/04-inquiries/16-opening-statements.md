# 04 Inquiries: Opening statements

<!-- Part1 p.202 -->

### Opening statements

73. This is where the main parties (including Rule 6 parties) briefly outline their overall case. You will know it already, but it will set the scene for what is to follow and can be particularly helpful to other persons who are present and may not be familiar with the entirety of the cases. The openings should also be submitted in writing and sent electronically to the Case Officer.

74. If opening statements are made, encourage brevity. 5-15 minutes should be enough for even the most complex of cases. They should not be used to recite or present evidence.
