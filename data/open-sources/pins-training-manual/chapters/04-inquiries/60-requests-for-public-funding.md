# 04 Inquiries: Requests for public funding

<!-- Part1 p.228 -->

### Requests for public funding

267. You might exceptionally be told that individuals are applying for legal aid (public

<!-- Part1 p.229 -->

funding) based on Article 6(1) of the European Convention on Human Rights. However, neither you nor the Secretary of State can entertain applications for public funding.

268. You should offer to assist those unfamiliar with Inquiry procedure as far as possible consistent with your role. It could also be suggested that the individuals might be able to co-operate with another party sharing part or all the same case; and that assistance may be available from Planning Aid, Citizens Advice, or other organisation offering free assistance or funding.

269. If a party decides to apply for public funding, it might very exceptionally be necessary to adjourn the Inquiry to give time for the application to be processed, although in a long Inquiry it may be possible to rearrange the programme to avoid an adjournment. See the Human Rights and Equality ITM Chapter for further advice.
