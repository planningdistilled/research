# 04 Inquiries: Discussion between an advocate and their witness

<!-- Part1 p.214 -->

### Discussion between an advocate and their witness

157. Once a witness has started giving evidence (Evidence in Chief), they should not be permitted to speak with anyone on their team until their re-examination

<!-- Part1 p.215 -->

has been completed. Consequently, where possible, it is best to avoid adjourning overnight or for weekends if a witness has not completed their session. If this is not possible you should remind the witness of the need to avoid communicating with members of their party.
