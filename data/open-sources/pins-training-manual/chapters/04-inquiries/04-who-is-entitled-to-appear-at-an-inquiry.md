# 04 Inquiries: Who is entitled to appear at an Inquiry?

<!-- Part1 p.188 -->

### Who is entitled to appear at an Inquiry?

11. The Appellant, the Local Planning Authority (LPA) and various other bodies, such as other local authorities in the area, parish/community councils, ‘Rule 6 parties’ and statutory parties, are entitled to appear at the Inquiry.

12. Paragraph 11.2.6 of the Guidance notes that interested parties will usually be allowed to participate in the inquiry. Rule 11(2) states that there is nothing that

<!-- Part1 p.189 -->

shall prevent you from permitting any other person to appear and such permission shall not be unreasonably withheld.

13. Rule 11.3 explains that those given Rule 6 status will be treated like the main parties, and that this status will normally be given to groups rather than individuals.
