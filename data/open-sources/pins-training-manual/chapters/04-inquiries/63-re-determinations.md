# 04 Inquiries: Re-determinations

<!-- Part1 p.230 -->

### Re-determinations

279. An Inquiry may be re-opened for the re-determination of a case when a transferred or SoS decision has been quashed by the High Court, Appeal Court or Supreme Court.12 The quashed decision is treated as if it had not been made and is incapable of having had any legal effect. It is, however, capable of being a material consideration and, if so, you will need to explain in your decision/report any departures you may make from the previous reasoning.

280. Procedure at the re-opened Inquiry follows the normal sequence. In your opening announcement, you must make clear that you are re-opening the Inquiry held earlier and that the case must be re-determined as the previous decision was quashed by the court.

281. Because you must deal with the case afresh, all the original issues should be considered, as well as taking into account any new evidence or material changes since the first Inquiry (such as the emergence of new planning policies).
