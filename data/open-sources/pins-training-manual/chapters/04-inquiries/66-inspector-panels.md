# 04 Inquiries: Inspector Panels

<!-- Part1 p.233 -->

### Inspector Panels

296. Occasionally, a case may require a panel of Inspectors. This will be due to the length or complexity of the case being determined; recent examples are the Bristol and Luton Airport appeals and the Cambridge Guided Busway. There will always need to be a Lead Inspector, although just the term ‘Inspector’ should be used on a daily basis. The role of each Inspector is to work on aspects of the appeal both before, during and after the Inquiry and to write part of the decision/report. The overall report and conclusions/recommendations should be jointly agreed.

297. It is for the Inspectors to agree what tasks each panel member undertakes, under the guidance of the Lead Inspector. Each Inspector may:

- Read the evidence on particular topics, including supporting evidence, and prepare any questions on those topics.

- Conduct sessions of the Inquiry on specific topics.

- Draft parts of the report, including (but not exclusively) sections on particular topics where they conducted the Inquiry session.

298. All the Inspectors should, if possible, attend the CMC and all sessions of the Inquiry and any site visits. In the event of an Inspector becoming ill after the Inquiry has been opened, it may be possible for the Inquiry and decision/report to proceed if this is appropriate. In that way the need to re-start or re-open the Inquiry would be avoided.
