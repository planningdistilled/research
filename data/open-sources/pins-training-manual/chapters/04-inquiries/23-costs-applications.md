# 04 Inquiries: Costs applications

<!-- Part1 p.207 -->

### Costs applications

110. National guidance on the award of costs is provided in this link - Claim planning appeal costs: Overview. Detailed advice is also provided in the ITM chapter on Costs. All costs applications must be made formally before the Inquiry is closed and it is good practice for them to be submitted in writing.

111. To assist with timetabling, you should ask about the costs intentions of the parties during the CMC and in your opening. Even so, you should always provide the formal opportunity at the end of the Inquiry because matters may have arisen during evidence.

112. If both parties make applications these should be heard one after the other. Start with the LPA or R6.

<!-- Part1 p.208 -->

113. If the Inquiry is adjourned to another day, then any costs applications should be heard at the end of the resumed event.

114. If a costs application has been made in writing:

- Does the applicant intend to add anything to it, orally?

- Has the written application been provided beforehand to the other party and to you? If not, ensure copies are provided and, if necessary, allow an adjournment for both you and the other party to read the application.

- If it was provided beforehand, has the other side responded to it in writing? If so, do they have any further response? If they have not prepared a written response, they should be given the opportunity to respond orally.

- If you and the parties have had time to read and understand the application and any response, neither needs to be read out.

115. If the costs application is made, or added to, orally, the other side should be given the chance to respond, and the costs applicant should then be given the final chance to comment.

116. If the costs application and response are made orally, you will need to take a full note of both. Ask the parties to proceed at a steady pace. In some cases it may be reasonable, in the interests of fairness, to allow an adjournment so that a response to a costs application can be prepared.
