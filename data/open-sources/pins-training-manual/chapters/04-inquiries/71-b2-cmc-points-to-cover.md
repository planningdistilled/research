# 04 Inquiries: B2 CMC Points to cover

<!-- Part1 p.240 -->

### B2 CMC Points to cover

1. Introduction

Introduce self and appeal.

2. Introduction of those present

Parties to introduce main speaker

Check they have seen agenda.

3. Purpose of the conference

As Pre-CMC note.

Summary note after CMC – focus on key dates.

4. Likely main issues and other matters

Views of parties on main issues as in Pre-CMC note.

Appellant to address local residents’ issues.

5. How the main issues will be dealt with

For each issue – Round table or formal.

Topic based or party order.

6. Inquiry programme and running order

Duration – realistic?

0930 start on subsequent days?

Day 1

- My opening, main parties opening (Appellant – R6 – LPA)

- Interested persons (some flexibility of timing)

Subsequently

- Set out order as you see it..

Finally

- Conditions and obligation R

- Closings (LPA – R6 – Closings)

<!-- Part1 p.241 -->

- Costs

Site visit

- Accompanied (private land) or unaccompanied

- Who, where when.

- Agreed plan for pre inquiry look and subsequent visit

7. Time estimates

- From parties 2 weeks before opening. Subsequently Inspector issues timetable.

8. Conditions – without prejudice

- When draft conditions

- I will comment (Track Changes) before Inquiry.

9. Planning Obligation

(If likely)

- Early draft of the obligation – when?

- Need CIL Compliance Statement

10. Core Documents

- Who is hosting – usually LPA. If appellant – clearly flagged from LPA website

- Clearly subdivided

- Two weeks before proofs?

- All to contribute

- After proofs, no additional CDs unless exceptions accepted by PINS.

- Appeal decisions need to be referenced in terms of relevance

11. Venue

- Where – size – any availability restrictions

- Disabled access

- Microphones

<!-- Part1 p.242 -->

- Livestream (is there demand?)

- Hearing loop?

- Wi-Fi?

- Retiring room? Room for appellants and R6

12. Pre Inquiry timetable (adjust as circumstances dictate)

- CD library

- SOCGs

- Proofs

- Rebuttals if essential

- Conditions and Obligation

- CIL Compliance Statement

- Time estimates

- Inquiry notification letter and list of those notified

13. Use of AI

(If there is any reason to suspect use of AI)

- Refer the parties (neutrally) to PINS AI external guidance on the use of AI

14. Costs

- Any indications at this stage?

15. Any other procedural matters

- Encourage close collaboration
