# 04 Inquiries: The order of the Inquiry

<!-- Part1 p.197 -->

### Running the Inquiry

### The order of the Inquiry

57. Of the Rules governing the procedures at an Inquiry2 the most pertinent are:

- Rule 16(1): “except as otherwise provided in these Rules, the Inspector shall determine the procedure at an Inquiry”.

[^2]: Note that there are specific differences relating to enforcement and LDC Inquiries

<!-- Part1 p.198 -->

- Rule 16(4): the LPA shall begin and the appellant has the right of final reply (unless you determine otherwise). Other persons will be heard in such order as you determine.

- Rule 16(5): A person entitled to appear shall be entitled to call evidence and the appellant, LPA and any statutory party shall be entitled to cross-examine persons giving evidence.

58. The Inquiry will follow the running order agreed during the CMC and/or confirmed in your post-Conference note (subject to any changes arising since). An indicative Inquiry programme can be found at Annex F.

59. Inquiries normally follow the order below:

- Inspector’s opening announcements including confirmation of the main issues and the timetable, an outline of where you have been and what you have seen already, confirmation of who is representing the parties, and identification of anyone else present who wishes to speak. Seek the views of the parties as to whether an accompanied visit is necessary, and when.

- Opening statements (if you deem them necessary – the main parties will assume this) from the main parties (usually the Appellant followed by the LPA and any Rule 6 party).

- Evidence from any interested parties present who wish to speak.

- The evidence from the main parties using the method(s) agreed during the CMC, and subsequently. As well as the formal presentation of evidence and cross-examination, there may be round table sessions led by you.

60. After all the evidence has been heard, the rest of the Inquiry normally follows this order:

- Discussion of conditions and planning obligations by means of an Inspector-led round-table discussion.

- Site Visit if this has not taken place at another point during the inquiry.

- Closing submissions, usually beginning with the LPA, followed by any statutory or Rule 6 parties and ending with the Appellant.

- Cost applications if any.

- Closing of the Inquiry.

<!-- Part1 p.199 -->

61. It is good practice to check that everyone has been heard before you move on from each stage and before you close the Inquiry.
