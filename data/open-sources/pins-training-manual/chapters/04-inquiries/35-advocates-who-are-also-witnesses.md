# 04 Inquiries: Advocates who are also witnesses

<!-- Part1 p.215 -->

### Advocates who are also witnesses

161. Sometimes professional persons may appear in a dual capacity as an advocate and as an expert witness. This should not normally present any difficulties. It is important to distinguish between the two roles, however, and they should sit at the witness table when giving evidence. They should not give evidence when acting as the advocate. For obvious reasons, they will be unable to re-examine themselves.
