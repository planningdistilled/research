# 04 Inquiries: Procedures at a re-opened Inquiry

<!-- Part1 p.229 -->

### Procedures at a re-opened Inquiry

270. Although uncommon, there are various reasons why an Inquiry may be re-opened. This may include new evidence, new issues of fact or matters relating to natural justice. It may be at the request of the parties, the Secretary of State or at your own discretion. Consult your IM or a PfL if you have this in mind.

271. When new evidence or opinion is to be considered, the relevant witness(es) must attend the re-opened Inquiry to give their views on this evidence only.

272. When a new issue of fact has caused the Inquiry to be re-opened, the parties will have been told what it is, and they will be entitled to submit any evidence that reasonably bears upon it. It may not be necessary for anyone to attend and give evidence, although you can explain how you would like to proceed. If anyone does appear, this will be on the terms set out in the preceding paragraph.

273. When re-opening the Inquiry, you should emphasise that the proceedings are strictly limited to the consideration of the specific topic(s) or matter(s) requiring further examination.

274. Ideally, the re-opened Inquiry is taken by the original Inspector. If this is not possible, and it has fallen to you, you should prepare as you would for any other Inquiry, albeit the focus may be very narrow. Should you be faced with this discuss the position with your IM and/or PfL.

275. The usual procedure for your opening should be complied with. Before any representations are heard, you should explain the procedure to be adopted. If there are any procedural objections you should hear them and, if possible, resolve them by agreement. It may be relevant to consider what additional conditions, if any, might be imposed in relation to the matters discussed.

<!-- Part1 p.230 -->

276. The Rules regarding the previous submission of Rule 6 statements and proofs of evidence do not apply in a re-opened planning Inquiry. However, the Case Officer will normally have written to the parties to require statements and proofs. If this has not been done, you can set out a timetable for the receipt of evidence before the re-opening.

277. The body or person responsible for producing the new evidence or calling attention to the new issue should be asked to present their case first. The parties should then be heard in turn, followed by any interested persons11, with the Appellant being allowed the right of final reply. The Inquiry should then be closed.
