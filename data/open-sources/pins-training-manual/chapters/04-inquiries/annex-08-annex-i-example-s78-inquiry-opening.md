# 04 Inquiries: Annex I: Example s78 Inquiry Opening

<!-- Part1 p.257 -->

### Annex I: Example s78 Inquiry Opening

To be adapted as appropriate.

Consider use of bullet points only – can sound better than reading from a script.

### Introduction

- Good morning. 10 o’clock. Inquiry is open.

- Can everyone hear and see me?

- My name is []. [Qualifications], appointed by the Secretary of State to hold this Inquiry and [decide this appeal/report with a recommendation to the SoS].

- The Inquiry is into an appeal made by [] for [] at []

- Couple of housekeeping, procedural and admin matters.

- Firstly, please ensure that phones and other devices are switched to silent.

- Secondly, fire alarm test, fire escape, assembly points, toilets.

- If being observed – colleague from Inspectorate – no part in proceedings or decision/report.

### Appearances

- Names of those who will be actively involved in the Inquiry – including later sessions on conditions and obligations. Ask for a typed list from the main parties for accuracy.

- Helpful if names, qualifications, position appended to closing submissions.

For the Appellant:

<!-- Part1 p.258 -->

For the LPA:

For any Rule 6

- Anyone else wishing to speak?

Name | From? | Support/objecting Support?

Support?

Objecting?

Objecting?

Objecting?

- I have allocated time in the programme later this morning for interested parties to speak.

- I shall assume that you will be happy to take questions on your evidence if any of the main parties wish to question you?

[If no, you do not have to answer questions, but I have to give your evidence less weight in those circumstances.]

- You should also note that, if you do speak, give evidence or ask questions during the inquiry your name will be listed in the decision/report.

If large numbers, consider appointing spokesperson. Repetition unhelpful

- Is anyone present from the press? Decision on PINS website.

### Filming/recording

<!-- Part1 p.259 -->

- Is anyone intending to film/record. (Be aware if livestreamed that usually automatically recorded.)

- If yes, any problems with this? Must be done so as not to interfere with Inquiry or disturb speakers.

### Notification letters

- I have a copy of the Council’s letter of notification of the appeal [Date] and a copy of the letter informing interested parties of the date, time and venue for the Inquiry [Date of second letter] and the list of those to whom the notification was sent.

[If not got letters – get them and check]

- Can the Council confirm that it notified all relevant parties and that the site notices have been posted?

### Representations

- I have all the responses to the original planning application, and those made in response to the appeal. I will take all of these into account.

[If any doubt offer main parties opportunity to check during a programmed adjournment. Or give out your list if you have one.]

Case Management Conference [only if members of the public are present]

- I held a CMC call on Teams on [Date] with representatives of the appellant, the Council [R6]. It was held to discuss the procedure for the inquiry and the merits of the appeal were not discussed.

### Main issues

- Main issues as the CMC note, unless something has changed.

- List your main issues

### Plans

- Are these as set out in draft conditions and/or in SOCG?

- Assume that plans are those which were before Council – if not - clarify.

[If not, ask for an agreed list after lunch.]

### Costs

- If any costs applications are to be made this should be done before close of Inquiry.

<!-- Part1 p.260 -->

- I have a power to initiate an award of costs, whether or not any application has been made by the parties. Were I to do this, it would follow a written process with the relevant party after the appeal decision is issued.

[May be helpful to indicate that this is not in your mind at that stage.]

- Costs will be heard – claimant, respondent, final by claimant.

### Documents

- All documents and evidence should have been provided. Not inviting any additional documents- but if there are any which might be submitted I will discuss at the end of my opening.

### Conditions and Planning Obligation

- Got draft conditions – normal practice – without prejudice and no prejudgement on my behalf.

[Already seen and sent Track Changes comments?]

- I have a final draft planning obligation – to be discussed later in Inquiry and will fix date for a final version.

### Proofs of evidence

- I have received proofs of evidence of the main parties’ witnesses, including rebuttals - I would expect them to be largely taken as read.

- I have the agreed main/topic SoCGs. No need to repeat.

- Just to confirm, have the parties got each other’s proofs and associated documents?

[If any missing, sort out at next scheduled adjournment]

- For the interested parties, Core Documents - on the Council’s website? Proofs etc in the room?

### Running order

[In case of queries, based on principles in Procedure Rules.]

- Opening statements from appellant, then R6, then LPA – brief openings to set the scene.

<!-- Part1 p.261 -->

If Topic Based (revise if the inquiry is going to be party by party- in order with LPA first)

- Council’s witness on [topic], xx by appellant and re-examination by Council’s advocate. Advocates are urged to keep both evidence and chief and cross-examination brief and to the point – bearing in mind that the Inspector will have already read the evidence of each witness.

- Then R6 evidence, in the same format as the Council (no repetition)

- Finally, evidence on this topic by the appellant’s witness, xx by Council and by R6 (no repetition), re-examination by appellant’s advocate.

Then next topic(s) in same format.

Describe any round table sessions.

[On [topic] which I will lead. I have produced a small agenda to help keep that discussion focussed on the matters at issue.]

- Then conditions and obligation round table.

- Then closings (LPA, R6, appellant)

- Then any costs applications and close Inquiry – no new documents without exceptional reasons.

### My questions

- I may ask questions at any stage in the proceedings to clarify a point, as well as possibly after xx of each witness.

### Timetable

- The Inquiry is scheduled to last for [] days. I set out a draft timetable [when]

– based on estimates from the parties. The agreed timetable is reasonable in the circumstances of the case, and is based on the parties’ estimates. I expect it to be adhered to.

- If a party unnecessarily prolongs proceedings there could be costs implications.

- Each day short breaks in the morning and afternoon, lunch at about 1300. So there should be 4 sessions each day. I will aim to finish around 1700 – not sit much later

- On subsequent days I intend to start at 09:30 – OK with parties?

### Site visit

<!-- Part1 p.262 -->

- Already seen site/area [when] from public land.

- After/during inquiry further site visit – need to go onto private land/be accompanied?

- Ask for agreed site visit locations/route from parties if not already submitted.

- If accompanied, who will attend?

### Closings

Closings will need to be circulated to room before delivery and sent electronically to the PINS Case Officer.

SoS cases only - remind of note agreed by PEBA and circulated after CMC regarding the most helpful way of structuring closings for the SOS (SoS Casework ITM) .

### Behaviour at the Inquiry

(Only necessary is any sign of bad behaviour/high tension.)

- Remind of need for courtesy to all participants. Listen respectfully and in silence to those who may have opposing views.

- Applause or booing will not be tolerated.

- I will pick up at once on any behaviour, from anyone, which is impolite or disrespectful.

- (In very extreme cases, you could prepare an abbreviated version of Annex K to set out the position.)

### Use of AI

(If there is any reason to suspect use of AI)

- Refer the parties (neutrally) to PINS AI external guidance

- Seek advice of your IM or PfL is there are issues

### Finally

- That concludes my opening remarks

- Any questions?

- Any suggested additional documents and why
