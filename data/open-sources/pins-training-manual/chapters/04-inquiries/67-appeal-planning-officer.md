# 04 Inquiries: Appeal Planning Officer

<!-- Part1 p.233 -->

### Appeal Planning Officer

299. If you are provided with the assistance of one of our APOs, they will have been selected to assist you in administrative tasks and can help with things like summarising representations or reports. They do not need to join you at any part of the event/CMC, unless this has been agreed as a development opportunity for them, but if they do you should briefly introduce them and explain their functions. It should be made clear that they have no part in the decision making process and that any recommendations / conclusions in your report / decision remain yours.
