# 04 Inquiries: Video evidence

<!-- Part1 p.227 -->

### Video evidence

259. You may be asked to view video evidence (for example showing highway conditions or a virtual reality model of the proposed development). If so, you should make sure that all those at the Inquiry have had the opportunity to view

<!-- Part1 p.228 -->

and comment upon the recording, and that a witness will be available to explain the evidence and answer questions.
