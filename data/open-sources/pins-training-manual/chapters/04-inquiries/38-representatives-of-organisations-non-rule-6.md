# 04 Inquiries: Representatives of organisations (non-Rule 6)

<!-- Part1 p.216 -->

### Representatives of organisations (non-Rule 6)

172. Check the position of anyone who states that they are representing an organisation. Do they have authority to represent that organisation?

<!-- Part1 p.217 -->

173. It can also be helpful to know the number of members and how the organisation arrived at their position on the appeal. Was there, for example, a vote at a meeting or a committee decision? This is something the opposing advocate may ask.
