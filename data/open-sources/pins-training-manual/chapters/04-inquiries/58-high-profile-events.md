# 04 Inquiries: High profile events

<!-- Part1 p.228 -->

### High profile events

260. Some events will attract a significant number of interested parties, demonstrations and scrutiny by the press. It is essential that you refer to the Role of the Inspector chapter for full detail on how to deal with these circumstances.

261. It could be that either the appellant or the nature of the proposal, combined with press and social media interest, leads to exceptional levels of comment and potential risk.

262. The advice in the chapter referenced above is not repeated here, but it is essential that Inspectors discuss a potential issue with their IM and PfL at a very early stage – so that appropriate Risk Assessments and discussions with the Press Office can be undertaken.
