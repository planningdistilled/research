- The Town and Country Planning Act 1990 (as amended) – ‘the 1990 Act’

- The Town and Country Planning (Appeals) (Written Representations Procedure) (England) (Amendment and Saving Provisions) Regulations 2026 – ‘the 2026 Regs’

- Town and Country Planning (Appeals) (Written Representations Procedure) (England) Regulations 2009 (SI 2009/452), as amended – ‘the 2009 Regs’

- Town and Country Planning (Development Management Procedure) (England) Order 2015 (SI 2015/595), as amended – ‘the 2015 Order’

- Procedural Guide – Planning Appeals – England - Post 1st April 20261.
