# 05 Written Representations Part 1: Conditions

<!-- Part1 p.279 -->

## Conditions

46. If the LPA suggests any non-standard conditions you should consider whether the appellant should be given the opportunity to comment on them. However, some conditions would be unlikely to come as a surprise and so you would not need to seek comments - for example, obscure glazing a bathroom window or the ‘plans condition’. Consultation with the appellant will be required for pre-commencement conditions in the usual way. See the Conditions ITM chapter.
