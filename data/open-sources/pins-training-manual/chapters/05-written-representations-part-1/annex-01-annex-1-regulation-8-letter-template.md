## Annex 1 - Regulation 8 letter template

The Inspector is seeking further information under Regulation 8 of The Town and Country Planning (Appeals) (Written Representations Procedure) (England) Regulations 2009.

[Insert details of the specific further information required]

The information must be provided in writing within 5 working days. [Longer if, exceptionally, the Inspector considers this necessary]

You are reminded that this is not an opportunity to provide any further information that has not been requested within this letter. Any such information will be disregarded and returned, unseen by the Inspector.
