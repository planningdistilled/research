# 05 Written Representations Part 1: The scope of Part 1 Appeals

<!-- Part1 p.273 -->

## The scope of Part 1 Appeals

10. The cases which fall within the scope of Part 1 are set out in the 2026 Regulations, the 2015 Order and in the Procedural Guide.

11. With few exceptions (see para 12/13 below) all s78(1) appeals will follow the Part 1 process in the first instance. These are appeals against:

- Refusal of planning permission;

- Grant of planning permission subject to conditions to which the applicant objects;

- Refusal of prior approval;

- Refusal of advertisement consent;

- Refusal of an application to approve a reserved matter;

- Refusal of an application to modify or remove a condition under section 73 of the 1990 Act;

<!-- Part1 p.274 -->

- Refusal of an application for planning permission for development already carried out under section 73A of the 1990 Act; and

- Refusal of Permission in Principle or Technical Details Consent.

12. The following section 78(1) appeals will not usually follow the Part 1 process after initial screening:

- Appeals proceeding as Hearings or Inquiries;

- Appeals against Biodiversity Net Gain Plans because of the requirements of article 37E of the 2015 Order; and

- Redetermination appeals. These may follow a different process so the relevant PfL will decide which process it will follow. It may be possible for the appeal to remain in Part 1.

13. Appeals that will not follow the Part 1 process because they are not made under section 78(1) of the 1990 act include:

- Appeals against non-determination (failure cases);

- Appeals made under directions given under section 77 of the 1990 Act (call-in cases);

- Appeals made under section 20 of the Planning (Listed Buildings and Conservation Areas) Act 1990 for listed building consent (Y appeals);

- Tree Preservation Order appeals, Hedgerow removal and High Hedge appeals;

- Appeals following the service of an enforcement notice (C appeals);

- Appeals relating to certificates of lawful development or use (X appeals); and

- Modification of section 106 agreements (Q appeals).
