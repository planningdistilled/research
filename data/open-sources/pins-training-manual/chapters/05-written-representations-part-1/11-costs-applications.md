# 05 Written Representations Part 1: Costs applications

<!-- Part1 p.280 -->

## Costs applications

53. The 2008 Planning Act permits costs applications to be sought and awarded in written representations cases.

54. The appellant’s claim for costs should be made at the same time as the appeal. The LPA has 14 days from the start date of the appeal to make a claim. The party against whom the costs application has been made will be given an opportunity to comment within a set timescale. National guidance on the award of costs is provided in the Appeals section of the government’s ‘Planning Practice Guidance’.

<!-- Part1 p.281 -->

55. It is usual practice, where possible, to issue the appeal and costs decisions at the same time. However, it can be acceptable to issue the appeal decision first, to be followed by the Costs one. See the Costs Awards ITM chapter.
