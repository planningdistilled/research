# 10 Appeals against Conditions: Annex H. Examples of standard wording

<!-- Part1 p.424 -->

## Annex H. Examples of standard wording

### A: Type 1 (s79) Appeals directly following a conditional grant of planning permission

DART: PLG conds (1) variation of existing (s79(1))

### Banner Heading

- The appeal is made under section 78 of the Town and Country Planning Act 1990 (as amended) against a grant of planning permission subject to conditions.

- The appeal is made by [appellant] against the decision of [LPA].

- The application Ref [….] was approved on [….] and planning permission was granted subject to condition[s].

- The development permitted is [description of development given on planning permission].

- The condition[s] in dispute [is] [are] No[s] [….] which state[s] that: [….].

- The reason[s] given for the condition[s] [is] [are]: [….].

### Decision: Allowed

The appeal is allowed and the planning permission Ref [LPA ref] for [description of development given on planning permission] at [address] granted on [date] by [LPA name], is varied by deleting condition(s) [….] [and substituting for them the following conditions: [….]].

### Decision: Dismissed

The appeal is dismissed.

<!-- Part1 p.425 -->

B: Type 2 (s73) Appeals following a refusal of (or failure to determine) an application to carry out development without complying with a condition imposed on a permission

DART: PLG conds (2) variation (s73) – refusal or PLG conds (2) variation (s73) -failure

### Banner Heading

- The appeal is made under section 78 of the Town and Country Planning Act 1990 (as amended) against [a refusal to grant planning permission under section 73 of the Town and Country Planning Act 1990 (as amended)] OR [a failure to give notice within the prescribed period of a decision on an application for planning permission under section 73 of the Town and Country Planning Act 1990 (as amended)] for the development of land without complying with conditions subject to which a previous planning permission was granted.

- The appeal is made by [appellant] against the decision of [LPA].

- The application Ref is [….].

- The application sought planning permission for [description of original act of development] without complying with [a] condition[s] attached to planning permission Ref [….], dated [….].

- The condition[s] in dispute [is] [are] No[s] [….] which state[s] that: [….].

- The reason[s] given for the condition[s] [is] [are]: [….].

### Decision: Allowed with conditions

The appeal is allowed and planning permission is granted for [description of original act of development] at [address] in accordance with the application Ref [insert ref for application subject of the appeal not the original permission ], without compliance with condition number[s] [list all conditions which have been successfully appealed against or have been discharged or are no longer relevant] previously imposed on planning permission Ref [insert ref no from original planning permission] dated [insert date of original planning permission] and [subject to the following conditions: [set out in full all conditions which you intend to impose on the permission you are granting].

### Decision: Allowed - no conditions

The appeal is allowed and planning permission is granted for [description of original act of development] at [….] in accordance with the application Ref [….] without compliance with the conditions previously imposed on the planning permission Ref [….] dated [….].

### Decision: Dismissed

The appeal is dismissed [and planning permission is refused for […]]

NOTE: If the original permission is a prior approval or approval of reserved matters, the template will be different as will the first bullet point of the banner heading as follows;

Prior Approval DART: PLG conds (2) variation (S73) - refusal/failure - condition on prior approval - IF ALLOWED WILL RESULT IN GRANT OF PLANNING PERMISSON

The appeal is made under section 78 of the Town and Country Planning Act 1990 (as amended) against a [refusal to grant planning permission] [failure to give notice within the prescribed period of a decision on an application for an application for planning permission] under section 73 of the Town and Country Planning Act 1990 for the development of land without complying with conditions imposed on a grant of prior approval required under Article 3(1) and Schedule 2, Part [specify part], Class [specify class] of the Town and Country Planning (General Permitted Development) (England) Order 2015 (as amended).

Reserved Matters DART: PLG conds (2) variation (S73) - refusal/failure -condition on approval of reserved matters or detailed pursuant - IF ALLOWED WILL RESULT IN NEW GRANT OF PLANNING PERMISSON

The appeal is made under section 78 of the Town and Country Planning Act 1990 (as amended) against a [refusal to grant planning permission] [failure to give notice within the prescribed period of a decision on an application for an application for planning permission] under section 73 of the Town and Country Planning Act 1990 for the development of land without complying with conditions imposed on the approval of reserved matters or details pursuant to a previous planning permission.

### D: Type 4 appeals seeking to extend ‘temporary permissions’

DART: PLG conds (4) exp temp pp (s73A(2)(b)) – refusal or PLG conds (4) exp temp pp (s73A(2)(b)) - failure

### Banner Heading

- The appeal is made under section 78 of the Town and Country Planning Act 1990 (as amended) against [a refusal to grant planning permission] OR [a failure to give notice within the prescribed period of a decision on an application for planning permission] under section 73A of the Town and Country Planning Act 1990 as amended by the Planning and Compensation Act 1991 for [description of original act of development] for which a previous planning permission was granted for a limited period.

- The appeal is made by [appellant’s name] against the decision of [LPA’s name].

- The application Ref is [….].

- The application sought planning permission for [description of original act of development] granted planning permission for a limited period Ref [….], dated [….].

- The permission is subject to a condition requiring the [cessation of the use] OR [removal of the buildings or works] on or before [….].

- The reason given for the condition is: [….].

### Decision: Allowed

The appeal is allowed and planning permission is granted for [description of original act of development] at [address] effective from [insert date the time limit expired] in accordance with application Ref [….] subject to the following conditions: [….].

### Decision: Dismissed

The appeal is dismissed

### NOTE

- use the wording in bold if you intend to back date the permission (it can be good practice to backdate permissions where there is evidence that a failure to do so could cause problems).

- Conditions attached to the expired original permission cease to exist, so any conditions necessary must be imposed on the permission granted.

### F: Application for approval of reserved matters – conditional grant

DART: PLG details pursuant and res matters - conditional grant

### Banner Heading

- The appeal is made under section 78 of the Town and Country Planning Act 1990 (as amended) against a grant subject to conditions of consent, agreement or approval to details required by a condition of [a planning permission]/[a consent] /[an agreement]/[an approval].

- The appeal is made by [Appellant’s name] against the decision of [LPA’s name].

- The application Ref [….], sought approval of details pursuant to condition[s] No[s] [….] of [a planning permission]/[a consent]/[an agreement]/[an approval] Ref [….] granted on [….].

- The development proposed is [description of development on outline permission].

- The condition[s] in dispute [is] [are] No[s] [....] which state[s] that: [….].

- The reason[s] given for the condition[s] [is] [are]: [….]

### Decision: Allowed

The appeal is allowed and the approval Ref [LPA ref] given to the details pursuant to conditions Nos [….] of a planning permission Ref [outline PP ref] given on [….] is varied by deleting conditions [….] [and substituting for them the following conditions: [….]].

### Decision: Dismissed

The appeal is dismissed

### G: Application for approval of reserved matters – refusal or failure

DART: PLG details pursuant and res matters - refusal or PLG details pursuant and res matters - failure

### Banner Heading

- The appeal is made under section 78 of the Town and Country Planning Act 1990 (as amended) against a [refusal to grant] OR [failure to give notice within the prescribed period of a decision on an application for] consent, agreement or approval to details required by a condition of an outline planning permission.

- The appeal is made by [Appellant’s name] against the decision of [LPA’s name].

- The application Ref [….], sought approval of details pursuant to condition[s] No[s] [….] of a planning permission Ref [….], granted on [….].

- The application was refused by notice dated [….] [If a failure case leave blank].

- The development proposed is [description of development from outline permission].

- The details for which approval is sought are: [access, scale, layout, appearance, landscaping]

### Decision: Allowed

The appeal is allowed and the reserved matters are approved, namely [Access / scale / layout/ appearance / landscaping] submitted in pursuance of condition [….] attached to planning permission [….] dated [….]

### Decision: Dismissed

The appeal is dismissed and approval of the reserved matters is refused, namely: [specify the reserved matters covered] details submitted in pursuance of condition [….] attached to planning permission Ref [….] dated [….].

### H: Application to discharge a condition

DART: PLG details pursuant and res matters - refusal or PLG details pursuant and res matters - failure

### Banner Heading

- The appeal is made under section 78 of the Town and Country Planning Act 1990 (as amended) against a [refusal to grant] OR [failure to give notice within the prescribed period of a decision on an application for] consent, agreement or approval to details required by a condition of a planning permission.

- The appeal is made by [Appellant’s name] against the decision of [LPA’s name].

- The application Ref [LPA ref], sought approval of details pursuant to condition[s] No[s] [….] of [a planning permission]/[a consent]/[an agreement]/[an approval] Ref [….], granted on [….].

- The application was refused by notice dated [ ] [If a failure case leave blank].

- The development proposed is [….].

- The details for which approval is sought are: [….].

### Decision: Allowed

The appeal is allowed, and the details submitted pursuant to condition No [xx] attached to planning permission [Ref of PP] granted on [date] in accordance with the application dated [date of application to discharge condition] and the details submitted with it are approved.

### Decision: Dismissed

The appeal is dismissed
