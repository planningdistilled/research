# 17 Design: Introduction

<!-- Part1 p.661 -->

## Introduction

1. Inspectors make their decisions on the basis of the evidence before them. Consequently, they may, where justified by the evidence, depart from the advice given in this training material, although the National Planning Policy Framework (NPPF), the Government’s Planning Practice Guidance (PPG) and National Policy Statements (NPS) will still be relevant in all cases.

2. This training material applies to casework in England only.1
