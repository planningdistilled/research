# 07 High Court Challenges: Public Rights of Way and Common Land casework

<!-- Part1 p.300 -->

## Public Rights of Way and Common Land casework

32. The administration of challenges against these casework types is handled by the Operations team rather than the High Court team, though they follow much the same process. However, as PINS undertakes this casework on behalf of Defra, they have overall authority when it comes to instructing on the challenge. As part of the process, we still provide comments and recommendations to GLD and Defra.
