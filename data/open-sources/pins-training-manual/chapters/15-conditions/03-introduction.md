# 15 Conditions: Introduction

<!-- Part1 p.576 -->

## Introduction

2. This chapter sets out legal, policy and practical considerations regarding the imposition of conditions on planning permissions in England.

3. This chapter is written with planning appeals in mind but contains advice that is relevant to all casework where existing or proposed conditions are before the decision-maker.

4. Inspectors make their decisions on the evidence before them, which may sometimes justify departure from the advice given in this chapter.
