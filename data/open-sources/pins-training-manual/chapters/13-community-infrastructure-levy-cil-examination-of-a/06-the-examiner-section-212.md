# 13 Community Infrastructure Levy (CIL): Examination of a Charging Schedule: The examiner (Section 212)

<!-- Part1 p.491 -->

## The examiner (Section 212)

8. The charging authority [not the Secretary of State] appoints the examiner, who is ‘independent’ and ‘suitably qualified and experienced’

9. With the examiner’s agreement, the charging authority can appoint an assistant e.g. development economics advisor, although in practice such appointments are unusual.

10. PINS will recover the examiner’s costs plus expenses from the charging authority.
