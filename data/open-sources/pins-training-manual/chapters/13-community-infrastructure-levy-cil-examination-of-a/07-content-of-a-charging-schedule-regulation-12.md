# 13 Community Infrastructure Levy (CIL): Examination of a Charging Schedule: Content of a charging schedule (Regulation 12)

<!-- Part1 p.491 -->

## Content of a charging schedule (Regulation 12)

11. The charging schedule must name the charging authority and contain the rates (set at pounds per square metre) at which CIL is to be chargeable in the authority’s area.

12. It must provide an explanation of how the chargeable amount will be calculated.
