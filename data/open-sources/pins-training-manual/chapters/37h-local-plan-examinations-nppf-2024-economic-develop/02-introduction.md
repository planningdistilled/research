# 37h Local Plan Examinations (NPPF 2024): Economic Development: Introduction

<!-- Part3 p.83 -->

## Introduction

1. The planning system economic objective is to help build a strong, responsive and competitive economy, by ensuring that sufficient land of the right types is available in the right places and at the right time to support growth, innovation and improved productivity; and by identifying and coordinating the provision of infrastructure. (NPPF 8a)

2. The NPPF’s policies for building a strong competitive economy apply to all economic and business development, which covers a wide range of sectors. This includes everything from the manufacturing sector through data driven and creative industry and distribution to service, retail and rural industries.

3. This chapter provides advice on issues that may arise in the examination of all the economic and business development content of local plans. In terms of the Use Classes Order, this includes all B class development plus use class E(g). However, it may also be relevant to all other parts of class E since these uses commonly support industrial and business areas.

4. It may be regarded as a road map which directs the reader to the relevant policies and procedures and provides information about their practical implications for the local plan examination.

5. The very specific issues arising from the need to support the vitality of town centres are dealt with in a separate chapter.

6. The chapter deals in order with:

- policy and guidance;

- the assessment of need;

- plan allocations and policies.

7. It takes into account the December 2024 National Planning Policy Framework (NPPF or Framework) and the latest versions of Planning Practice Guidance (PPG), including the transitional arrangements set out in the Framework.

8. Paragraphs 234 to 241 and Annex 1 of the December 2024 NPPF set out transitional arrangements for plan-making and examinations. For the purposes of preparing local plans, the policies of the December 2024 NPPF apply from 12 March 2025 unless one or more of the 5 exceptions set out in NPPF 234a to 234e apply, in which case the plan will be examined under the relevant previous version of the Framework.
