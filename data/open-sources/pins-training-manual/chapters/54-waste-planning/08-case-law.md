# 54 Waste Planning: Case Law

<!-- Part4 p.147 -->

## Case Law

94. Cornwall Waste Forum (St Dennis Branch) v SSCLG & SITA Cornwall Ltd – confirms the principle that the Inspector does not have to deal with matters that are dealt with in the environmental permit (in this case, an assessment under the Habitat Regs).

95. Bristol City Council v SSCLG – confirms the importance of robust analysis of capacity needed to deal with commercial and industrial waste.

96. D Skrytek v SSCLG, Derbyshire County Council & Resource Recovery Solutions (Derbyshire) Ltd - confirmed the Inspector’s reasoning that WFE with some energy recovery comes higher than disposal by landfill in the hierarchy.

97. Veolia ES (UK) Ltd v SSCLG, Hertfordshire CC, Welwyn Hatfield BC, New Barnfield Action Fund & Gascoyne Cecil Estates – confirmed the need to evaluate consequences of a Waste Site Allocations Plan (where finding proposed waste site complied in principle with Green Belt policy).

98. Hertfordshire CC v SSCLG & Metal and Waste Recycling Ltd – where breach of planning permission has been defined in the form of a material change of use (MCU) (by increase in throughput), the LPA cannot introduce different issues to establish a MCU.
