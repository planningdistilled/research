# 54 Waste Planning: Definition of ‘Waste’?

<!-- Part4 p.122 -->

## Definition of ‘Waste’?

4. Under the current EU Waste Framework Directive (rWFD), waste is defined in Article 3(1) as ”any substance or object which the holder discards or intends or is required to discard”.
