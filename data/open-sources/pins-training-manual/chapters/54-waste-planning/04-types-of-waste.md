# 54 Waste Planning: Types of Waste

<!-- Part4 p.122 -->

## Types of Waste

5. In waste casework, reference will be made to ‘waste streams’. The following are the most common (see Glossary for definitions):

Inert Waste

Municipal Solid Waste

Household waste

Hazardous waste

Clinical waste

Industrial waste

Commercial waste

Radioactive waste

Biodegradable waste

6. The European Waste Catalogue (EWC) brought into UK law by the List of Wastes (England) Regulations 2005, sets out the many types of waste for classification purposes. Although mainly used for the purposes of regulation and pollution control, these categories are sometimes relevant to waste planning casework.
