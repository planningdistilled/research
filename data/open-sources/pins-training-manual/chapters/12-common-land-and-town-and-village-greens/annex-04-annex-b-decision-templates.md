### Preliminary matters

1. Section 16(1) of the Commons Act 2006 (‘the 2006 Act’) provides, amongst other things, that the owner of any land registered as Common Land / a town or village green may apply for the land (‘the release land’) to cease to be so registered. If the area of the release land is greater than 200m2 a proposal must be made to replace it with other land to be registered as Common Land / a town or village green (‘the replacement land’). However, even if the land to be deregistered is not more than 200m² the Secretary of State will usually expect land to be offered in exchange for the land being deregistered so that stock of Common Land / village greens will not diminish.
