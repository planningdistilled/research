# 37g Local Plan Examinations (NPPF 2024): Housing: ANNEX 1: Some considerations in assessing housing site deliverability

<!-- Part3 p.75 -->

## ANNEX 1: Some considerations in assessing housing site deliverability

### Site allocations

- What preliminary work has been carried out to assess the site’s deliverability, such as any masterplanning exercises?

- What constraints and impediments exist on or around the site that will need to be addressed?

- What infrastructure needs to be in place to enable the site to come forward?

- What is the timescale for the provision of that infrastructure, what agencies are involved and what is the stated position of those agencies?

- What environmental, transport and other mitigation measures will be required?

- What are the intentions of the owners/developers?

- When is development likely to commence?

- How many outlets will there be and what are the build-out rates likely to be?

### Sites with planning permission

In addition to the above:

- Is the site still available for development?

- If planning permission is subject to the completion of a planning obligation, what progress has been made in negotiating the agreement?

- If it is an outline permission, what progress has been made with discharging conditions?

<!-- Part3 p.76 -->

### Sites allocated in the previous development plan

In addition to the above:

- How long has the site been allocated?

- What has prevented the site from coming forward for development?

- What does the SHLAA say about the site constraints?
