# 37g Local Plan Examinations (NPPF 2024): Housing: Introduction

<!-- Part3 p.18 -->

## Introduction

1. It is the Government’s policy to boost the supply of homes significantly by ensuring that a sufficient amount and variety of land can come forward where it is needed, with the overall aim of meeting an area’s identified housing need. (NPPF 61).

2. Housing is likely to form a large component of most local plan examinations. Local plans, and local plan examinations, will need to consider a wide range of housing topics.

3. This chapter addresses the main issues that arise in the examination of the housing aspect of local plans. It provides references to a range of different policies and considers issues that commonly arise in the examination.

4. It may be regarded as a road map which directs the reader to the relevant policies and procedures and provides information about their practical implications for the local plan examination.

5. It is not a policy document. Users of this document must refer directly to the NPPF and PPG for full details of policies and procedures.

6. The structure of the chapter follows this sequence:

- housing need

- the plan’s housing requirement

- housing land availability

- selecting housing sites

- overall housing land supply

- the 5 year housing land supply

- affordable housing

- plan policies, including site allocation policies, specialist needs and optional technical standards

<!-- Part3 p.19 -->

7. This chapter only addresses plans to which the policies of the December 2024 NPPF apply. For advice on examining plans submitted before 25 January 2019, please refer to Local Plan Examinations: NPPF 2012. For advice on plans examined under NPPF 2021, please refer to Local Plan Examinations: NPPF 2021

8. This chapter takes into account the December 2024 National Planning Policy Framework (NPPF or Framework) and the latest versions of Planning Practice Guidance (PPG), including the transitional arrangements set out in the Framework.

9. The Schedule to the Town and Country Planning (Local Planning) (England) Regulations 2026 sets out transitional provisions for local plans prepared under previous regulations that are currently in examination and paragraphs 234 to 241 and Annex 1 of the December 2024 NPPF set out national policy on the subject. For the purposes of preparing local plans, the policies of the December 2024 NPPF apply from 12 March 2025 unless one or more of the 5 exceptions set out in NPPF 234a to 234e apply, in which case the plan will be examined under the relevant previous version of the Framework. There is a cut-off date of 31 December 2026 for the submission of local plans for examination under the existing system. An issue of interpretation has arisen regarding NPPF 234a in respect of the relationship between housing requirement and local housing need, that may have a bearing on which version of the NPPF should be used at examination, and this is addressed in paragraphs 93-97 below.

10. There are policy references at the end of many of the paragraphs. These allow the reader to look up the subject in more detail. They do not denote quotations from the policy.

11. “Authority” has been used throughout to mean “plan-making authority”. “Local plan” means a full local plan containing strategic policies unless otherwise indicated.
