# 37g Local Plan Examinations (NPPF 2024): Housing: Some definitions

<!-- Part3 p.19 -->

## Some definitions

12. It is very important to use the right terminology when dealing with housing in development plan examinations. The following definitions can all be found in this chapter but are brought together here for clarity.

<!-- Part3 p.20 -->

13. Local housing need is the number of homes identified as being needed through the application of the standard method set out in national planning practice guidance. It is an unconstrained assessment of the minimum number of homes needed in an area. Once the plan is submitted for examination, the housing need figure calculated through the standard method can be relied on for plan making for 2 years from the date of submission.

14. The housing requirement is the minimum number of homes that a plan seeks to provide from all sources during the plan period. It is usually expressed as an annual figure.

15. The strategic housing land availability assessment (SHLAA) identifies how much land in the plan area is suitable, available and achievable for housing (and economic) development during the plan period.

16. Site allocations or broad areas for growth are sites or broad areas identified for development in the plan. They are usually the key components that enable the plan’s overall housing requirement to be met. Selecting housing sites for inclusion in the plan is a separate exercise from the SHLAA.

17. Housing supply is a future supply of land which is suitable, available and achievable for housing and can potentially be delivered over the plan period from the plan’s site allocations, existing commitments, completions since the start of the plan period, and estimated windfalls.

18. The 5 year housing land supply is the supply of specific deliverable sites for the five years following the adoption of the plan, with an appropriate buffer.
