# 46 Public Rights of Way: Public Path Orders

<!-- Part3 p.669 -->

## Public Path Orders

### General Principles

26. PPOs can alter the alignment and existence of footpaths, bridleways and restricted byways – introduced by the Natural Environment and Rural Communities Act 2006 (NERCA2006). Changes cannot be made to BOATs by PPOs with the exception of s118B, 119B and 119D.

27. The HA1980 allows changes under the following sections:

Section 26: Creation

Section 118: Stopping up (extinguishment)

Section 118A: Stopping up of public paths crossing railway lines

Section 118B: Stopping up of certain highways for purposes of crime prevention etc

Section 119: Diversion

Section 119A: Diversion of public paths crossing railway lines

Section 119B: Diversion of certain highways for purposes of crime prevention etc

Section 119D: Diversion of certain highways for protection of sites of special scientific interest (SSSI)

28. The TCPA1990 allows changes under the following sections:

Section 247: Public paths affected by development: orders by Secretary of State

<!-- Part3 p.670 -->

Section 257: Public paths affected by development

Section 258: Extinguishment of public rights of way over land held for planning purposes

Section 261: Temporary stopping up of highways for minerals working
