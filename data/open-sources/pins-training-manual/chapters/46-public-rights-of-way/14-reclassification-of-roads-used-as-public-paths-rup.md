# 46 Public Rights of Way: Reclassification of Roads used as public paths (RUPPs)

<!-- Part3 p.698 -->

## Reclassification of Roads used as public paths (RUPPs)

227. Section 54 of WCA81 placed a duty on surveying authorities to review the DMS and reclassify all RUPPs as footpaths, bridleways or BOATs by way of reclassification orders. S54 ceased to have general effect after the commencement (on 11 May 2006) of s47 of CROWA2000 but any undetermined reclassification orders made prior to that must proceed to a conclusion. It is believed there are a significant number of such cases still outstanding in Wales.

228. The Wildlife and Countryside (Definitive Maps and Statement) Regulations 1983 did not require a width to be recorded by a reclassification order. Revised 1993 Regulations specified that widths should be shown. An Inspector determining a pre- 1993 reclassification order is not obliged to add a width but may consider it requisite to do so subject to the usual requirements of evidence and advertisement.

229. Where the width of a RUPP is recorded in a definitive statement, there is no need to re-state it in the schedule of a pre-1993 reclassification order, although it is open to an Inspector to do so. Since this is already conclusive evidence by virtue of its inclusion in the definitive statement, there would be no need to advertise its addition to the order.
