### Class A: Casino, Betting Office, Pay Day Loan Shop or Hot Food Takeaway to Commercial Business and Service

48. Development is permitted under Class A only for a change of use of a building

– not including land within the curtilage or any building operations.

49. Class A was amended in May 201635 and again on 1 August 202136 to change the scope of the PD right, with latter amendments being pursuant to those made to the UCO. In short, development is now permitted under Class A for a change of use from a use falling within Article 3(6)(m), (n), (o) or (r) of the UCO to a use falling within Class E of Schedule 2 to the UCO.

50. The PD right is subject to one condition, A.1, that the developer must provide written notification to the LPA, before beginning the development, of the date on which the use of the building will change.

### Class M: Certain Uses to Dwellinghouses

61. Class M was amended in April 201644 and again in August 202145 in respect of the uses from which a change to a dwellinghouse may be made. Where there is a mixed use, it does not matter if permission was granted via Class G of Part 3.

Under M.1(e) and (f), development is not permitted if it would result in the external dimensions of the building being extended beyond those existing or consist of demolition other than partial demolition of the building to the extent that is reasonably necessary for the building to function as a dwelling. 64. Thus, operations involved in the excavation, installation or strengthening of foundations could be permitted. Such works would be likely to represent ‘development’ – subject to what works are proposed as a whole – and they could be described as reasonably necessary if, for example, they are required to support a replacement wall which is reasonably necessary.

65. However, if new or reinforced foundations are proposed simply to support the existing building – or the totality of works would amount to complete or substantial re-building as per Hibbitt (as discussed above) – the works may not be PD under Class M. Any structural evidence before you will be crucial, and your judgment should be made on a fact and degree basis.

### Class O: Offices to Dwellinghouses

84. Any application for prior approval with respect to Class O must have been made on or before 31 July 2021. ### Class P: Storage or Distribution Centres to Dwellinghouses

88. Development is permitted under Class P for a change of use of a building and land within its curtilage from a use falling within use class B8 to a use falling within use class C3. The term ‘curtilage’ is defined for the purposes of Class P in paragraph P.3.

89. Under paragraph P.1(a) and (b), development is not permitted if the building was not [lawfully] used solely for a storage and distribution use on 19 March 2014, or when last in use, and the building was not so used for a period of least four years before the date that development under Class P begins.

90. Since Class P only permits a change of use, and given the limited prior approval matters set out in P2.2.(b), court cases relating to conversion or rebuilding, such as Hibbitt, are not relevant to Class P prior approval applications. Any building works which amount to development will require express planning permission.

91. Class P is a temporary one; paragraph P.1(c) provides that development is not permitted if the prior approval date falls on or after 1 June 201960. Subsequent amendments to the GPDO in relation to natural light and space standards did not apply to Class P. The development must have been completed – under P.1(k) – within a period of three years starting with the prior approval date.

92. For the purposes of Class P, the term ‘building’ does not include ‘part of a building’; see Article 2(1)61. Under P.1(d), development is not permitted if the gross floor space of the existing building exceeds 500m2, and that must be taken as a limit to the size of the whole building. Class P previously permitted the change of use of up to 500m2 of a larger storage and distribution depot.

93. Since the term ‘gross’ floorspace is used in paragraph P.1(d), walls should be included in the measurements in accordance with the RICS Gross External Area. The measurement of gross floorspace should also include any communal residential areas, for example, lifts, stairs and corridors.

### Class Q: Agricultural Buildings to Dwellinghouses

The ‘Old’ and ‘New’ Class Q and Transitional Arrangements

99. The development permitted under Class Q and the limitations to such PD were significantly amended on 21 May 2024, when the Town and Country Planning (General Permitted Development) (England) (Amendment) Order 2024/579 comes into force.

100. Given the transitional arrangements set out under Article 10 of the amending Order, prior approval applications could be made in respect of the old Class Q up until the end of 20 May 2025. Inspectors needing to consider Class Q as it was before 21 May 2024 should consult the 1 August 2020 version of the GPDO 2015.

101. Where prior approval is sought in respect of the new Class Q, however, it does not matter when the application was made. As advised above, Inspectors must decide prior approval appeals with regard to the Order in force on the date of the decision unless transitional arrangements apply.
