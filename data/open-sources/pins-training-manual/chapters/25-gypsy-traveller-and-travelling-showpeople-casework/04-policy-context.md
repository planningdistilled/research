# 25 Gypsy, Traveller and Travelling Showpeople Casework: Policy Context

<!-- Part2 p.123 -->

## Policy Context

### The Development Plan

47. As PPTS paragraph 23 indicates, the statutory provisions in s70(1)(a) of the TCPA90 and s38(6) of the Planning and Compulsory Purchase Act 2004 mean that planning applications and appeals must be determined in accordance with the development plan, unless material considerations indicate otherwise. The NPPF and PPTS are material considerations.

48. PPTS paragraph 11 expects that criteria are set [in local plan policies] to guide land supply allocations where there is an identified need, and to provide a basis for decisions on applications that might come forward even where there is no identified need.

49. As with bricks and mortar housing, identified need is not a prerequisite for a grant of permission for a Traveller site. Nonetheless, in order to properly conclude against the development plan, the appeal decision should set out whether there is a criteria-based policy for Traveller sites, the extent to which the development would comply and/or conflict with the criteria, and the degree of consistency between the policy and PPTS and the NPPF.

50. Inspectors may also need to establish in an appeal whether there is an adopted development plan document (DPD) which includes allocations for housing, including Traveller sites. The Local Plans ITM Chapter advises on meeting the needs of Travellers in the examination of development plans.

### The National Planning Policy Framework

51. PPTS paragraph 24 states that applications for Traveller sites should be assessed and determined in accordance with the presumption in favour of sustainable development and the application of specific policies in the NPPF as well as PPTS. In accordance with NPPF paragraph 11d), planning permission should be granted where there are no relevant development plan policies, or the policies which are most important for determining the appeal are out of date, unless the circumstances set out in NPPF paragraphs 11d)i) or ii) apply.

52. Footnote 8 to NPPF paragraph 11d) describes situations where the most important policies are rendered out of date for applications involving the provision of housing. Footnote 8 applies where permission is sought for a caravan site occupied by persons without PPTS status, but it does not apply to Traveller site appeals16. As NPPF footnote 27 explains, ‘[PPTS] sets out how Travellers’ housing needs should be assessed for those covered by the definition in Annex 1 of that document.’

53. It follows that, in a Traveller case, the most important policies are not rendered out of date for the purposes of NPPF paragraph 11d) by a shortfall in the supply of general housing land. However, they are rendered out of date by a shortfall in deliverable sites for Travellers. PPTS paragraph 28 states that if a local planning authority cannot demonstrate an up to date five year supply of deliverable sites [required by virtue of PPTS paragraph 10a)], the provisions in [NPPF] paragraph 11d) apply17.

54. Footnote 7 to NPPF paragraph 11d)i) is relevant to Traveller sites, as indeed are other NPPF policies as discussed throughout this chapter.

### Planning Policy for Traveller Sites (PPTS)

55. Inspectors should ensure that they are familiar with the entirety of this document, but key paragraphs include:

- Paragraph 3 states that the ‘Government’s overarching aim is to ensure fair and equal treatment for Travellers in a way that facilitates [their] traditional and nomadic way of life…while respecting the interests of the settled community’.

- Paragraph 4 sets out aims to help achieve that overarching aim.
