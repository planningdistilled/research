# 21 Enforcement Case Law: COMPLETION NOTICES

<!-- Part1 p.790 -->

## COMPLETION NOTICES

### Cardiff CC v NAW & Malik [2006] EWHC 1412; [2007] JPL 60

The incomplete operations remaining on land after a failure to comply with a s94 completion notice are lawful and cannot be enforced against. Serving a s102 discontinuance notice is the only remedy available to the LPA.
