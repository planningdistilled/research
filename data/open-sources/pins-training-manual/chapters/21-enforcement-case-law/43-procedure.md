# 21 Enforcement Case Law: PROCEDURE

<!-- Part1 p.892 -->

## PROCEDURE

### Manchester City Council v SSLUHC [2022] EWHC 1062 (Admin)

PINS had decided that the appeal could proceed on ground (a) on the basis that the application was not a single ‘related application’ (see s118 of the Levelling-up and Regeneration Act 2023). However, the Council argued that a ground (a) appeal was precluded by virtue of section 174(2A) and (2B). It was found that the plain and ordinary meaning of s174(2B) accorded with the approach taken by the Council, in that if it had granted planning permission for the totality of the development applied for, it would therefore have granted planning permission inter alia for the retrospective planning application applied for. Accordingly, it would have granted permission “for the matters specified in the enforcement notice as constituting a breach of planning control”; that is all of the development now being enforced against would have been authorised and regularised.
