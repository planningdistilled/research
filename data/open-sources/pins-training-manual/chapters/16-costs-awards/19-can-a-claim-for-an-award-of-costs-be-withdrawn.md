# 16 Costs Awards: Can a claim for an award of costs be withdrawn?

<!-- Part1 p.642 -->

## Can a claim for an award of costs be withdrawn?

48. Yes, if the party who applied for an award of costs formally notifies the Planning Inspectorate of the withdrawal. However, this does not prevent another party from seeking costs, nor the potential for an Inspector to initiate an award against either party.
