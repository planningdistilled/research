# 16 Costs Awards: An application for a full award of costs

<!-- Part1 p.639 -->

## An application for a full award of costs

38. An application for a full award of costs:

- relates to the applicant’s whole costs of the statutory process, including submission of the appeal statement and supporting documentation (including the expense of making the costs application); and

- could be granted in full, refused or allowed in part (even if the applicant has applied for a full award and has made no specific reference to a partial award).
