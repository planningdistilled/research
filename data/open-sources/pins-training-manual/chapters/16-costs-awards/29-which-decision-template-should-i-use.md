# 16 Costs Awards: Which decision template should I use?

<!-- Part1 p.649 -->

## Which decision template should I use?

94. The appeal decision should refer to the costs application (using the standard paragraph) making clear costs is the subject of a separate decision.

95. The relevant costs decision template should be selected from DaRT options:

- Costs Decision – w rep

- Costs Decision – I/H

- Costs report

96. An example decision template is shown at Annex C.
