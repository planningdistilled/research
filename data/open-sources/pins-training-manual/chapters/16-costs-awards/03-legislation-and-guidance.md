# 16 Costs Awards: Legislation and guidance

<!-- Part1 p.631 -->

## Legislation and guidance

1. The legislation underpinning costs awards in planning-related proceedings under the Town and Country Planning Act 1990 is:

2. Section 320 – This section incorporates s250(5) of the Local Government Act 1972 into the 1990 Act1 and by doing so allows orders as to costs to be made by Inspectors in circumstances where a local inquiry has been held.

Section 322 – this section applies the costs regime (as set out in s320 above) for orders as to costs to be made by Inspectors in hearings and written representations appeals in the same way as it applies to local inquiries.

Section 322A – this section allows orders as to costs to be made where a local inquiry or a hearing has been scheduled but the inquiry or hearing does not take place.

Section 322B – this section applies the costs regime (as set out in s320 above) for orders as to costs to be made by Inspectors in circumstances where a local inquiry is held as a result of the London Mayor directing refusal of a planning application.

3. Guidance can be found in the Planning Practice Guidance (PPG) Section 16: Appeals, The award of Costs – general2.
