# 16 Costs Awards: An application for a partial award of costs

<!-- Part1 p.639 -->

## An application for a partial award of costs

39. An application for a partial award of costs:

- may be made in appropriate circumstances, for instance where the application relates only to one ground of refusal, or to a particular aspect or part of the appeal process up to (or after) a specified date;

- in such cases, an award of costs would be limited to the expense caused by the unreasonable behaviour identified, e.g. the time and effort expended on pursuing that particular part of the case (you do not have to define the specific amount of any award); and

- may be allowed in the terms of the application; refused; or allowed in part (that is, a smaller partial award than that sought may be made).
