# 16 Costs Awards: Charting arrangements

<!-- Part1 p.645 -->

## Charting arrangements

66. You will normally be charted half a day per costs application (except for Part 1 appeals).

67. For inquiry and hearings cases where applications are not known about in advance of the event, you should ‘claim’ reporting time by e-mailing the Case Officer and by adding an entry to your SAP timesheet. This will be added to your work programme at the earliest available opportunity.

68. For inquiry and hearings cases where costs applications are made in advance, time will be allocated as part of the reporting on the case.

69. In written representations cases, costs reporting time will be added as soon as the Case Officer is made aware of the costs application. The reporting will be charted as close to the site visit as possible taking into account the latest deadline for comments on the costs application.
