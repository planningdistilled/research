# 16 Costs Awards: Procedural matters (Written Representations)

<!-- Part1 p.642 -->

## Procedural matters (Written Representations)

49. The costs application will be made by written submissions and all the costs correspondence will be found in the 06 Costs Folder of the Inspector E-File

50. When a timely costs application is made, the Case Officer will invite the other party to respond within 7 days, giving the applicant a further 7 days for final comment on the response, before the decision can be issued (the applicant always has the opportunity to make a final reply in writing).

51. The Case Officer will check correspondence received to identify either an application for costs or any costs response and, where possible, this will be added separately to the 06 Costs Folder. If the costs application or response is contained within another document such as the full statement of case then the case officer will rename the document to include COSTS as a suffix, that is: 02 statement and appendices and costs.

52. Whilst the Case Officer will aim to identify and put all of the costs application material in the 06 Costs Folder, you will need to satisfy yourself that you have had regard to all the relevant costs material when writing the decision.

53. Costs applications in relation to appeals following the Part 1 (expedited) Written Representations procedures are dealt with by Inspectors within the time allocated for the appeal. However, if dealing with a costs application takes a substantial amount of time – then additional time can be charted (discuss with your IM/SIT).

54. You should decide all costs applications in non-Part 1 cases where the application has been received by the deadline for final comments. Applications received after this deadline will be dealt with by CDT. CDT will also deal with any applications which concern conduct at the site visit whether or not received

<!-- Part1 p.643 -->

within 7 days of the event. To assist CDT you should record what happened at the event and email it to the Case Office for adding to the e-file.

55. It is usual practice, where possible, to issue the appeal and costs decisions at the same time. However, it can be acceptable to issue the appeal decision first, to be followed by the Costs one.
