# 48 Retail and Town Centre Developments: Introduction

<!-- Part3 p.850 -->

## Introduction

1. Inspectors make their decisions on the basis of the evidence before them. Consequently, they may, where justified by the evidence, depart from the advice given in this training material, although the National Planning Policy Framework and the Government’s Planning Practice Guidance will be relevant in all cases.

2. The casework covered by this chapter includes retail development and other town centre development. Issues such as highways, visual impact and living conditions may also arise but this chapter focuses on those that are unique to these types of development.
