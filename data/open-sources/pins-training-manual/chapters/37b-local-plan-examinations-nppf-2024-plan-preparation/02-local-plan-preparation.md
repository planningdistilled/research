# 37b Local Plan Examinations (NPPF 2024): Plan Preparation: Local Plan Preparation

<!-- Part2 p.821 -->

## Local Plan Preparation

### Introduction

1. This topic section of the Local Plan Examinations chapter of the Inspector Training Manual (ITM) applies to the examination of “legacy plans” prepared under the Planning and Compulsory Purchase Act 2004 Act (before the 2026 amendments introduced by the LURA 2023) and the 2012 Regulations under NPPF December 2024 (as amended in February 2025).

2. Local planning authorities are responsible for preparing local plans. Planning Inspectors have a very limited role in that process: providing advice at advisory meetings in response to requests from LPAs, and providing a response to the LPA’s pre-examination checklist. Inspectors appointed to examine a plan should read the notes of any advisory meeting and the pre-examination checklist documents.

3. However, it is not the role of the examining Inspector to scrutinise the process that the LPA went through to prepare the Plan that it submitted for examination, other than in the limited respects set out in section 20(5)(a) of the 2004 Act (which are covered in ITM chapter 03 The Role of the Inspector in the Examination Process).

4. This ITM chapter is therefore intended primarily to provide contextual information about the plan preparation process that occurs before local plans are submitted for examination. It covers:

- Procedural requirements for local plan preparation

- Content of local plan policies

- Preparation of the policies map

- The relationship between local plans, neighbourhood plans and supplementary planning documents

5. Inspectors should also ensure they are familiar with relevant advice in the NPPF, especially Chapter 3 Plan-making, and in PPG ID:61 https://www.gov.uk/guidance/plan-making.
