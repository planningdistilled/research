Site visit made on 3 September 2026

Decision date: 8th September 2026


Appeal Ref: 6010708
Workshop, 20 Bury Lane, Bramfield, Hertfordshire SG14 2QL
    The appeal is made under section 78 of the Town and Country Planning Act 1990 (as amended)
     against a grant of planning permission subject to conditions.
    The appeal is made by Adam Bird against the decision of East Hertfordshire District Council.
    The application Ref 3/25/1349/FUL was approved on 4 December 2025 and planning permission
     was granted subject to conditions.
    The development permitted is the demolition of the existing workshop and construction of a new
     replacement workshop.
    The condition in dispute is No 4, which states: “The use of the premises shall be restricted to the
     hours 08.00 to 18.00 Monday to Saturdays, and 10.00 to 16.00 on Sundays and Public Holidays”.
    The reason given for the condition is: “In the interests of the amenities of the occupants of nearby
     properties and in accordance with Policies DES4 and EQ2 of the East Herts District Plan 2018”.

Decision
1.    The appeal is allowed, and the planning permission Ref 3/25/1349/FUL for the
      demolition of the existing workshop and construction of a new replacement
      workshop, at 20 Bury Lane, Bramfield, Hertfordshire SG14 2QL, granted on 4
      December 2025 by East Hertfordshire District Council, is varied by deleting
      condition No 4 and substituting the following new condition:
       (4)     The use of the premises hereby approved shall only take place between the
               following hours:
               Mondays – Fridays: 06.00 – 21.00
               Saturdays: 06.00 – 18.00
               Sundays and Public Holidays: 10.00 – 14.00
Reasons
2.    The appeal site comprises a motor repair workshop and yard, set within the small
      village of Bramfield. The existing building is said to have been built in the early
      1950s, and subsequently extended in around the 1980s or 90s. Since 2018 the
      premises have been operated by the present appellant Mr Bird, as Bramfield
      Automotive. To either side are residential properties, and to the rear is a scrap
      yard.
3.    The permission granted under 3/25/1349/FUL provides for a development which
      would demolish the existing outdated structure, and replace it with a more
      weathertight and energy-efficient modern workshop. The disputed condition, No 4,
      limits the use of the new building to the hours of 08.00 – 18.00 on Mondays to
      Saturdays, and 10.00 – 16.00 on Sundays and public holidays.
4.    Subsequently, in June 2026, an alternative permission has been granted
      (3/26/0359/VAR) which extends these permitted hours by allowing the use to
      commence at 06.00 on Mondays to Saturdays, and to continue until 21.00 on

         Mondays to Fridays, but also reduces the hours on Sundays and public holidays,
         by requiring any work to finish by 14.00.
5.       The appellant contends that the business has operated from the site for many
         years without giving rise to any complaints, and has done so despite the lack of
         any previous limits on its operating hours, and indeed seemingly without any other
         planning conditions of any kind. This appears to be borne out by the several
         letters of support from local residents. However, planning permission runs with the
         land, and without some means of control, there is no guarantee that this situation
         would always continue. In particular, if work were to take place late at night, or at
         other unsocial hours, there would be a risk that neighbouring occupiers would
         suffer unacceptable noise. On balance therefore, I consider that a condition
         limiting the hours of work is justified.
6.       I appreciate that the hours provided for under the existing Condition 4 of
         permission 3/25/1349/FUL are more generous than those originally indicated by
         the appellant. But that position has evidently been overtaken by subsequent
         discussions and events. Now that the Council has accepted a further relaxation, in
         the form of the new Condition 4 in permission 3/26/0359/VAR, it seems to me that
         it would no longer be reasonable to maintain Condition 4 of 3/25/1349/FUL in its
         existing, disputed form.
7.       It follows therefore that Condition 4 of 3/25/1349/FUL should now be varied to
         match 3/26/0359/VAR. In the light of the above, I am satisfied that this variation is
         compatible with the aims of the relevant provisions of Local Plan1 Policies EQ2
         and DES4, relating to noise and other impacts on neighbours.
Other matters
8.       The Council suggests that any change to Condition 4 should be accompanied by
         an additional new condition, similar to Condition 5 of 3/26/0359/VAR, restricting
         the use of the site to motor repairs, servicing and MOT testing and no other use,
         including other uses within Use Class B2. However, the appellant contends that
         this would make it impossible for the development to be funded. Whilst this claim
         has not been further evidenced, it seems to me fairly obvious that such a narrow
         restriction on the type of use permitted would increase the level of perceived risk in
         the scheme, and make lenders less willing to offer loans on normal commercial
         terms. It follows that the additional condition sought by the Council would
         jeopardise the viability of the development, and threaten the future of the existing
         business, thus risking the benefits that the scheme offers in terms of employment
         and other benefits to the local economy.
9.       In addition, the Council seeks an addition to Condition 3 to ensure the inclusion of
         the proposed acoustic insulation. But as the type of insulation is already specified
         in the approved plans, the additional wording is unnecessary.
Conclusion
10. For the reasons explained in this decision, the appeal is allowed, and Condition 4
    of permission 3/25/1349/FUL is varied as set out in paragraph 1 above.
