Decision
1. The appeal is allowed and planning permission is granted for Loft conversion with a
   rear dormer window and two front dormer windows to dwellinghouse at 109
   Catalina Drive, Poole, BH15 1TQ in accordance with the terms of the application,
   Ref P/26/00121/HOU, and the plans submitted with it, subject to the following
   conditions:
     1)       The development hereby permitted shall begin not later than three years from
              the date of this decision.
     2)       The development hereby permitted shall be carried out in accordance with
              the following approved plans: 607-D01-P6, 607-D02-P6, 607-D03-P6, 607-
              D04-P6, 607-D05-P6, 607-D06-P6, 607-D07-P6, 607-D08-P6, 607-D09-P6,
              607-D10-P6.
     3)       Demolition or construction works shall take place only between 0800 and
              1800 hours on Mondays – Fridays and 0900 – 1300 hours on Saturdays and
              shall not take place at any time on Sundays or on Bank or Public Holidays.
Appeal Procedure
2. The site visit was undertaken by a representative of the Inspector whose
   recommendation is set out below and to which the Inspector has had regard before
   deciding the appeal.
Preliminary Matter
3. A revised National Planning Policy Framework (the Framework) was published in
   August 2026. In this instance, the issues most relevant to the appeal are unaffected
   by the revisions to the Framework. I am therefore satisfied that there is no
   requirement to seek further submissions on the revised Framework, and that no
   party would be disadvantaged by this.

Main Issue
4. The main issue is the effect of the proposed development on the character and
   appearance of the host dwelling and the surrounding area.
Reasons for the Recommendation
5. The appeal property is a three storey mid-terrace dwelling within a U-shaped group
   that encloses a parking courtyard. The wider estate is notable for its variation in
   height, with buildings stepping up and down across the site, together with
   staggered building lines, a range of balconies and a mix of house types, including
   both two-storey and three-storey dwellings. The roofscape across Cataline Drive
   includes a number of dormer additions. As such, dormer windows form an
   established feature of the area, contributing to its evolving character.
6. Within the immediate and wider context, there are several examples of substantial
   dormer extensions, including at Nos 94, 98 and 93 Catalina Drive. These
   developments include large front-facing formers, in some cases spanning
   significant portions of the roof plane and sometimes also incorporating balconies.
   These schemes are clearly visible within the street scene.
7. The appeal proposal seeks to convert the existing loft space through the
   introduction of two gabled dormers on the front elevation and a single flat-roofed
   dormer to the rear. Compared to the previous refused scheme, the front-facing
   element has been broken down into two smaller dormers, reducing its visual mass.
   The ridge height of the property would remain unchanged, and the dormers would
   be read as additions within the existing roof form.
8. The rear dormer, whilst extending across much of the roof width, would be set in
   from the edge and set down from the ridge, resulting in a subservient appearance.
   Further it would be positioned on a less publicly visible elevation and is of a similar
   scale and appearance to other examples in the surrounding area.
9. The proposal would not result in an overly dominant or incongruous features and
   would preserve the character and appearance of the area. The proposed
   development would therefore accord with Policy PP27 of the Poole Local Plan
   2018, which seeks high-quality design that respects local character, as well as
   being in accordance with the Framework.
Other Matters
10. I have had regard to the comments made by interested parties with regards to
    character and appearance, overshadowing and disturbance. I have found that the
    proposed development would not harm the character of the area. With regards to
    overshadowing, in relation to the host building the proposed additions are minor in
    scale and do not extend above the existing ridge height. These minor additions
    would not increase overshadowing to a harmful extent. With regards to noise and
    disturbance during construction, whilst this would be temporary, due to the terraced
    nature of the host dwelling, a condition restricting construction hours would limit
    disturbance to daytime hours.
Conditions
11. I have recommended a condition requiring development to be carried out in
    accordance with the approved plans to provide certainty.
