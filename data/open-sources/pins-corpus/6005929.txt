Decision
1. The appeal is allowed and planning permission is granted for replacement of
   existing door at first floor level with timber sash window. at 76 Elmbourne Road,
   Wandsworth, London, SW17 8JJ in accordance with the terms of the application,
   Ref 2025/4004, subject to the following conditions:
     1)       The development hereby permitted shall be carried out in accordance with
              drawings ‘Location Plan’, ‘Proposed Front Elevation’, ‘Proposed Section AA’
              and ‘Proposed 1st Floor Layout’.
     2)       The development hereby permitted shall begin not later than three years from
              the date of this decision.
     3)       All external materials used in the construction of the development hereby
              approved shall match the type, texture, tone, colour, size and profile of those
              used on the existing building and shall be retained permanently.
Appeal Procedure
2. The site visit was undertaken by a representative of the Inspector whose
   recommendation is set out below and to which the Inspector has had regard before
   deciding the appeal.
Main Issue
3. The main issue is the effect of the proposal on the character and appearance of the
   host dwelling and on the preservation of the Heaver Estate Conservation Area.
Reasons for the Recommendation
4. The appeal property at 76 Elmbourne Road is a two-storey mid-terrace dwelling
   forming part of the Heaver Estate Conservation Area, a designated heritage asset
   of special architectural and historic interest. The conservation area is characterised
   by late Victorian terraced housing designed by Alfred Heaver, with a strong sense
   of uniformity derived from consistent plot widths, red brick elevations, decorative
   detailing and cast iron balconies. Collectively, these elements create a cohesive

     townscape that makes a positive contribution to the character and appearance of
     the conservation area, particularly in prominent streets such as Elmbourne Road
     which faces Tooting Common.
5. Elmbourne Road displays a strong rhythm of repeated architectural elements,
   including balconies with a mix of original sash windows and glazed doors. Whilst
   the conservation area identifies the retention of original features as important, the
   street scene is not wholly uniform in this regard. It is evident that a number of
   properties along Elmbourne Road have experienced alterations over time, including
   the replacement of first-floor balcony doors with sash windows of varying designs.
6. The proposal seeks to replace an existing first-floor balcony door on the front
   elevation with a vertically sliding timber sash window, with a glazing bar design to
   match the adjacent original sash windows serving the same balcony. Although the
   proposal represents a departure from the original form of the building, it sits within a
   row of similar openings at first-floor level, is partially screened by the balcony
   balustrade, and is viewed at some distance from the public realm. The visual
   difference between the existing door and the proposed window would be subtle,
   particularly as the upper sections of both elements are effectively identical in
   appearance.
7. In the context of a street where a range of similar alterations are already evident,
   the proposal would not appear visually discordant, nor would it materially disrupt
   the established rhythm of the terrace. Importantly, the key characteristics which
   define the significance of the conservation area – the terrace form, scale, materials,
   balconies and overall architectural composition – would remain intact and readily
   appreciable. The variation introduced would be consistent with changes already
   present along Elmbourne Road and would not undermine the overall coherence or
   significance of the Heaver Estate Conservation Area
8. The proposal would not harm the character and appearance of the host dwelling
   and would preserve the conservation area. It would therefore comply with Policy
   HC1 of the London Plan (2021), policies LP1, LP3 and LP5 of the Wandsworth
   Local Plan (2023), the Wandsworth Housing SPD (2016), and the Heaver Estate
   Conservation Area Appraisal and Management Strategy (2010).
Conditions
9. I have considered the advice contained within the Planning Practice Guidance
   (PPG) relating to conditions. A condition regarding the time for commencement and
   plans condition are necessary in the interest of certainty. A condition regarding
   matching materials is necessary in the interest of the character and appearance of
   the area.
Conclusion and Recommendation
10. For the reasons given above and having had regard to all other matters raised, I
    recommend that the appeal should be allowed.
