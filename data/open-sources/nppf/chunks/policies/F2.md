#### <a id="F2"></a>F2: Planning for effective flood risk management

1. <a id="F2-1"></a>Development plans should take a risk-based approach when identifying suitable locations for development, taking into account all sources of flood risk and the current and future impacts of climate change. They should do this, and manage any residual risk, by:

   a. <a id="F2-1-a"></a>Applying the sequential test in policy F5 and, where relevant, policy F6;

   b. <a id="F2-1-b"></a>Safeguarding land that is, or is likely to be, required for current or future flood management;

   c. <a id="F2-1-c"></a>Using opportunities provided by new development and improvements in green and other infrastructure to reduce the causes and impacts of flooding (making as much use as possible of natural flood management techniques as part of an integrated approach to flood risk management); and

   d. <a id="F2-1-d"></a>Where climate change is expected to increase flood risk so that some existing development may not be sustainable in the long-term, seeking opportunities to relocate development, including housing, to more sustainable locations.
