#### <a id="S6"></a>S6: Neighbourhood plans and the presumption

1. <a id="S6-1"></a>For development proposals involving the provision of housing, the benefits of approving development are likely to be substantially outweighed by the adverse effects where a proposal would conflict with a neighbourhood plan, provided the following apply:

   a. <a id="S6-1-a"></a>The neighbourhood plan became part of the development plan five years or less before the date on which the decision is made; and

   b. <a id="S6-1-b"></a>The neighbourhood plan contains allocations to meet its identified housing requirement (see policy HO2).
