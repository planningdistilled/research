#### <a id="P2"></a>P2: Ground conditions

1. <a id="P2-1"></a>Sites proposed for development should be suitable for their proposed use taking into account:

   a. <a id="P2-1-a"></a>Ground conditions, including any risks arising from land instability or contamination (whether due to natural hazards or current and former activities such as mining or fuel storage); and

   b. <a id="P2-1-b"></a>The extent to which any such risks can be mitigated, and the potential impacts on the natural environment arising from remediation.

2. <a id="P2-2"></a>Where land is remediated it should, as a minimum, not be capable of being determined as contaminated land under Part IIA of the Environmental Protection Act 1990.

3. <a id="P2-3"></a>Adequate site assessment information, prepared by a competent person, should be available to inform these considerations.

4. <a id="P2-4"></a>Where a site is affected by contamination or land stability issues, responsibility for securing a safe development rests with the developer and/or landowner.
