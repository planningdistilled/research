#### <a id="PM11"></a>PM11: Demonstrating cooperation between plan-making authorities

1. <a id="PM11-1"></a>Plan-making authorities should prepare, maintain and publish one or more statements of common ground to demonstrate that relevant cross-boundary matters have been addressed where possible, including by:

   a. <a id="PM11-1-a"></a>Taking a consistent approach to planning the delivery of major infrastructure where this involves one or more neighbouring authorities;

   b. <a id="PM11-1-b"></a>Distributing and providing for unmet needs across neighbouring and/or nearby areas; and

   c. <a id="PM11-1-c"></a>Planning effectively for cross-boundary growth locations, allocations or designations with significant implications across neighbouring areas.

2. <a id="PM11-2"></a>Where it has not been possible to fully address cross-boundary matters, plan-making authorities should demonstrate that they have taken all reasonable steps to address them.
