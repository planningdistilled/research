#### <a id="TR2"></a>TR2: Local parking standards

1. <a id="TR2-1"></a>Development plans should set local parking standards for residential and non-residential development, across the plan area or for specific sites or sub-areas, in a way which aligns with the transport vision for the plan and opportunities to promote sustainable transport.

2. <a id="TR2-2"></a>Maximum standards for car parking should be set where they will support efforts to encourage the use of sustainable transport modes and shared transport, optimise the density of development in well-connected locations, or where they are necessary for managing the local road network.

3. <a id="TR2-3"></a>Parking standards should take into account:

   a. <a id="TR2-3-a"></a>The connectivity of development in different locations by different modes of transport, especially in terms of access to employment and day-to-day services;

   b. <a id="TR2-3-b"></a>Opportunities for improvements in walking, wheeling, cycling and public transport;

   c. <a id="TR2-3-c"></a>Local car ownership levels and travel trends, and opportunities to promote car sharing and other schemes which may reduce vehicle parking requirements;

   d. <a id="TR2-3-d"></a>Providing sufficient spaces for electric vehicle charging, blue badge holders, pedal and electric cycles and e-scooters, considering likely future demand and the need for associated infrastructure; and

   e. <a id="TR2-3-e"></a>The appropriateness of setting different standards for the type and mix of development, including to address specific business requirements where commercial standards are being set; and of using ranges where a degree of flexibility is desirable.
