#### <a id="TR8"></a>TR8: Public rights of way

1. <a id="TR8-1"></a>Development proposals should:

   a. <a id="TR8-1-a"></a>Protect and enhance the network of public rights of way, including through suitable diversions agreed with the highway authority; and

   b. <a id="TR8-1-b"></a>Take opportunities to extend, link or improve the quality of existing routes where this enables one or more of the following:

      i. <a id="TR8-1-b-i"></a>Improved access to green spaces and the countryside;

      ii. <a id="TR8-1-b-ii"></a>New direct and circular routes and connections between local and long-distance footpaths, bridleways and cycle routes;

<!-- p.73 -->

      iii. <a id="TR8-1-b-iii"></a>The provision of accessible, safe and convenient links to services and facilities; and

      iv. <a id="TR8-1-b-iv"></a>Improved connections with public transport stops and interchanges.
