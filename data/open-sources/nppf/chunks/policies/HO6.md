#### <a id="HO6"></a>HO6: Planning for a diverse mix of sites

1. <a id="HO6-1"></a>To support the provision of a diverse mix of sites, local plans should:

   a. <a id="HO6-1-a"></a>Allocate land to accommodate at least 10% of the housing requirement on sites no larger than one hectare, and a further 10% on sites of between one and two and a half hectares, unless there are strong reasons why these targets cannot be achieved; and

   b. <a id="HO6-1-b"></a>Allocate sites which will support and enhance the vitality of rural communities and enable villages to grow and thrive, especially where this will support local services.
