#### <a id="GB1"></a>GB1: Establishing new Green Belts

1. <a id="GB1-1"></a>As the general extent of Green Belt land across the country is already established, new Green Belts should be established only in exceptional circumstances, for example when planning for new settlements or major urban extensions. Any proposals to establish new Green Belts in development plans should demonstrate:

   a. <a id="GB1-1-a"></a>Whether major changes in circumstances exist which make establishing a new Green Belt necessary as an exceptional measure;

   b. <a id="GB1-1-b"></a>Why normal planning and development policies would be inadequate for addressing these circumstances; and

   c. <a id="GB1-1-c"></a>That it would be compatible with long-term growth ambitions, including those beyond the plan period, for the relevant area.
