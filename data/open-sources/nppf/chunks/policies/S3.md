#### <a id="S3"></a>S3: Presumption in favour of sustainable development

1. <a id="S3-1"></a>Decisions on development proposals should apply a presumption in favour of sustainable development. This means:

   a. <a id="S3-1-a"></a>Policy S4 in this Framework should be applied when considering development proposals within settlements;

   b. <a id="S3-1-b"></a>Outside settlements, policy S5 should be applied; and

   c. <a id="S3-1-c"></a>In all locations, development proposals that accord with both an up-to-date development plan and the decision-making policies in this Framework should be approved without delay.

2. <a id="S3-2"></a>Where a development proposal falls partly within and partly outside a settlement, policies S4 and S5 should be applied to the relevant parts which are inside or outside of the settlement boundary (as appropriate), before coming to an overall view on the proposal.
