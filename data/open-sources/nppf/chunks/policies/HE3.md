#### <a id="HE3"></a>HE3: Historic Environment Records

1. <a id="HE3-1"></a>To support effective plan-making and decision-making, local planning authorities should maintain or have access to a Historic Environment Record. This should contain up-to-date evidence about the historic environment in their area and be used to:

   a. <a id="HE3-1-a"></a>Assess the significance of heritage assets and the contribution they make to their environment and inform the preparation of local lists;

   b. <a id="HE3-1-b"></a>Help identify where currently unidentified heritage assets, particularly sites of historic and archaeological interest, will be discovered in the future; and

   c. <a id="HE3-1-c"></a>Inform development plans, including scoping opportunities and constraints on land allocated for development, inform the preparation of local heritage lists and provide applicants and decision-makers with information to help assess the potential effects of proposed development on heritage assets.

2. <a id="HE3-2"></a>Local planning authorities should make information about the historic environment, gathered as part of plan-making or development management activities, publicly accessible.
