#### <a id="GB2"></a>GB2: Assessing existing Green Belt land

1. <a id="GB2-1"></a>The preparation of spatial development strategies should be informed by an assessment of the strategic role of Green Belt land within the strategy area. This strategic Green Belt assessment should take into account the five purposes of the Green Belt, which are to:

   a. <a id="GB2-1-a"></a>Check the unrestricted sprawl of large built-up areas;

   b. <a id="GB2-1-b"></a>Prevent neighbouring towns merging into one another;

   c. <a id="GB2-1-c"></a>Assist in safeguarding the countryside from encroachment;

   d. <a id="GB2-1-d"></a>Preserve the setting and special character of historic towns; and

   e. <a id="GB2-1-e"></a>Assist urban regeneration by encouraging the recycling of derelict and other urban land.

2. <a id="GB2-2"></a>The strategic assessment should be used to help develop the spatial development strategy and to identify whether there are areas where Green Belt boundaries may require further consideration through the preparation of local plans.

3. <a id="GB2-3"></a>The preparation of local plans should be informed by an assessment, conducted in accordance with the process at Annex E, which identifies grey belt and informs any Green Belt boundary alterations which accord with policy GB3.
