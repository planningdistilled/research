#### <a id="M6"></a>M6: Safeguarding mineral resources and infrastructure through decision-making

1. <a id="M6-1"></a>Proposals for non-mineral development should not:

   a. <a id="M6-1-a"></a>Constrain potential future use for mineral working in a Mineral Safeguarding Area; or

   b. <a id="M6-1-b"></a>Prevent or constrain the use of existing, planned and potential sites for:

      i. <a id="M6-1-b-i"></a>The bulk transport, handling and processing of minerals;

      ii. <a id="M6-1-b-ii"></a>The manufacture of concrete and concrete products; or

<!-- p.54 -->

      iii. <a id="M6-1-b-iii"></a>The handling, processing and distribution of substitute, recycled and secondary aggregate material.

2. <a id="M6-2"></a>Where proposals for non-mineral development are located within a Minerals Consultation Area, the decision-making authority should consult the mineral planning authority and take account of the local minerals plan before determining the planning application.

3. <a id="M6-3"></a>The prior extraction of minerals, where practical and environmentally feasible, should be supported where it would prevent the sterilisation of minerals deposits as a consequence of other forms of development proposals or allocations.
