#### <a id="E2"></a>E2: Meeting the need for business land and premises

1. <a id="E2-1"></a>To support business growth, substantial weight should be given to:

   a. <a id="E2-1-a"></a>The economic benefits of proposals for commercial development which allow businesses to invest, expand and/or adapt; especially where this would support the economic vision and strategy for the area, the implementation of the Industrial Strategy, support improvements in freight and logistics and/or reflect proposals for Industrial Strategy Zones and AI Growth Zones; and

   b. <a id="E2-1-b"></a>Benefits for domestic food production, animal welfare and/or the environment which can be demonstrated through proposals for development for farm and agricultural modernisation.

2. <a id="E2-2"></a>Where a development proposal is required to demonstrate whether an unmet need exists (including under policy S5) consideration should be given to whether:

   a. <a id="E2-2-a"></a>Market signals indicate an undersupply of specific types of business land or premises, taking into account the anticipated catchment area for the type of development proposed, the changing needs of different sectors and the availability of suitable existing land and buildings; or

   b. <a id="E2-2-b"></a>The specific locational requirements of the proposal are met by existing allocations in the development plan. This includes, but is not limited to, situations where:

      i. <a id="E2-2-b-i"></a>Existing businesses plan to expand or improve their premises, or clusters or networks of businesses need to grow (such as clusters of knowledge and data-driven, creative or high technology industries and associated facilities and infrastructure); or

      ii. <a id="E2-2-b-ii"></a>The availability of infrastructure (such as electricity network infrastructure to provide adequate grid connections, or water and wastewater capacity) makes certain locations particularly important, including opportunities to co-locate large-scale generators and users of power (such as data centres); or

      iii. <a id="E2-2-b-iii"></a>Proposals would meet a local, regional or national need for the provision of new, expanded or upgraded facilities that would result in more efficient, reliable or sustainable handling of goods (whether for their receipt, storage, processing, interchange or distribution).
