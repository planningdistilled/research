#### <a id="DM10"></a>DM10: Removal of national permitted development rights

1. <a id="DM10-1"></a>The use of Article 4 directions to remove permitted development rights should:

   a. <a id="DM10-1-a"></a>Be limited to situations where it is necessary to protect local amenity or the wellbeing of the area (for example safeguarding against the demolition of local facilities, preventing an over-concentration of uses which could affect the quality of life such as small Houses in Multiple Occupation, or supporting the vitality and viability of parts of town centres);

   b. <a id="DM10-1-b"></a>Be based on robust evidence; and

   c. <a id="DM10-1-c"></a>Apply to the smallest area required to mitigate the evidenced harm.
