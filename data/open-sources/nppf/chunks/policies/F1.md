#### <a id="F1"></a>F1: Assessing flood risk for plan-making

1. <a id="F1-1"></a>In order to manage development in ways which minimise the risk of flooding to people and property, development plans should:

   a. <a id="F1-1-a"></a>Be informed by an up-to-date strategic flood risk assessment which considers current and future flood risk from all sources, including cumulative impacts in, or affecting, areas susceptible to flooding; and

   b. <a id="F1-1-b"></a>Take account of advice from the Environment Agency and other relevant flood risk management authorities, such as Lead Local Flood Authorities and internal drainage boards, about levels of risk, options for mitigation, and the implications for development in the plan area.
