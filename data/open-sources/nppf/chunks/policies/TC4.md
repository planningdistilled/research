#### <a id="TC4"></a>TC4: Assessing the impact of development on town centres

1. <a id="TC4-1"></a>Development proposals for retail and leisure uses located outside town centres should be accompanied by an impact assessment if the proposal exceeds a floorspace threshold set out in an up-to-date development plan, and is not on a site which is allocated for the proposed use in the plan. If no threshold for impact assessments has been set in the development plan, a default threshold of 2,500m2 gross floorspace should be applied.

<!-- p.45 -->

2. <a id="TC4-2"></a>In circumstances where an impact assessment is required, it should be demonstrated that the development would be unlikely to have a significant adverse impact on:

   a. <a id="TC4-2-a"></a>Existing, committed and planned public and private investment in a town centre or centres in the catchment area of the proposal; and

   b. <a id="TC4-2-b"></a>Town centre vitality and viability, including local consumer choice and trade in the town centre and the wider retail catchment (as applicable to the scale and nature of the scheme).
