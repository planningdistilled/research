#### <a id="GB6"></a>GB6: Control of development in the Green Belt

1. <a id="GB6-1"></a>Development in the Green Belt is inappropriate unless it falls within one of the categories in policy GB7.

2. <a id="GB6-2"></a>Inappropriate development is, by definition, harmful to the Green Belt and should not be approved except in very special circumstances. Such circumstances will not exist unless the potential harm to the Green Belt by reason of inappropriateness and any other harm resulting from the proposed development, is clearly outweighed by other considerations. In making this assessment, substantial weight should be given to the harm to the Green Belt which would be caused, including harm to its openness.

<!-- p.62 -->

3. <a id="GB6-3"></a>In the case of proposals for renewable and low carbon energy development, very special circumstances may include the wider environmental benefits associated with increased production of energy from renewable sources.
