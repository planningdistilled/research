#### <a id="W4"></a>W4: Water infrastructure

1. <a id="W4-1"></a>In considering proposals for water supply, drainage and wastewater development, substantial weight should be given to the benefits of:

   a. <a id="W4-1-a"></a>Providing the capacity needed to serve proposed development and/or improving the security of supply and capacity for existing users (both residential and commercial, including agricultural users); and

   b. <a id="W4-1-b"></a>Improving water quality and reducing water-borne pollution.

2. <a id="W4-2"></a>Applicants should not be required to demonstrate the need for water infrastructure developments.
