#### <a id="PM9"></a>PM9: Identifying and assessing land for development

1. <a id="PM9-1"></a>Development plans should be informed by an assessment of the land available in their area to meet development needs. For spatial development strategies, this can be a strategic level assessment of land availability and development capacity to support the identification of broad locations.

2. <a id="PM9-2"></a>For plans that allocate specific sites for development, the assessment should be undertaken with reference to national guidance where applicable[^16], and be proportionate to the type of plan being prepared. The assessment should include:

   a. <a id="PM9-2-a"></a>A thorough site identification process to identify a sufficient range and quantity of potential sites;

   b. <a id="PM9-2-b"></a>An assessment of the availability, suitability and achievability (including likely viability) of those sites;

   c. <a id="PM9-2-c"></a>An assessment of the amount of development those sites have the potential to accommodate and the potential timescales over which development could be delivered[^17]; and

   d. <a id="PM9-2-d"></a>The identification of appropriate sites for development, taking into account the emerging vision and spatial strategy of the plan and the information requirements set out in this policy.
