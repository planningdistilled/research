#### <a id="PM12"></a>PM12: Developer contributions

1. <a id="PM12-1"></a>Development plans should, at the most appropriate level, set out the contributions expected from development to support the delivery of the plan.

2. <a id="PM12-2"></a>These policy requirements may apply to different areas covered by the plan. This includes:

   a. <a id="PM12-2-a"></a>Plan-wide policies;

   b. <a id="PM12-2-b"></a>Site-specific policies; and

   c. <a id="PM12-2-c"></a>Policies for different types or location of site or types of development.

3. <a id="PM12-3"></a>Policy requirements should:

   a. <a id="PM12-3-a"></a>Include the levels and types of affordable housing provision required (with reference to policy HO5), along with other infrastructure required;

   b. <a id="PM12-3-b"></a>Be set at a level that allows for the planned types of development and sites to be deliverable, maximising certainty and reducing the need for viability assessment at the decision-making stage; and

   c. <a id="PM12-3-c"></a>Be clear so that they can be accurately accounted for in the price paid for land. To provide this certainty, affordable housing requirements should be expressed as a single figure rather than a range.

4. <a id="PM12-4"></a>Plans should set out the circumstances in which review mechanisms will be used for development proposals where contributions are proposed to be reduced below the requirements set out in plan policies, in accordance with planning practice guidance. Plans should clearly set out the processes and terms of engagement regarding how and when viability will be reassessed over the lifetime of the development to maximise policy compliance.
