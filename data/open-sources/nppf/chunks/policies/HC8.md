#### <a id="HC8"></a>HC8: Development affecting Local Green Space

1. <a id="HC8-1"></a>Development proposals for land which has been designated as Local Green Space should be determined in a manner consistent with the relevant national decision-making policies for land in the Green Belt, excluding provisions relating to grey belt and previously developed land.
