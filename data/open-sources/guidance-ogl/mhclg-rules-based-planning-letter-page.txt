 Creating a clear, rules-based planning system: letter to local authority leaders and mayors

 The Minister of State for Housing and Planning has written to local authority leaders, mayors and chief executives of local authorities about the government’s next phase of reforms to accelerate growth and housebuilding.

 From:

 Ministry of Housing, Communities and Local Government and The Rt Hon Matthew Pennycook MP

 Published:
 18 August 2026
